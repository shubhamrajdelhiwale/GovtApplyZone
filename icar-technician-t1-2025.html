About ICAR Technician (T-1) 2025

The ICAR Technician (T-1) recruitment is conducted by Indian Council of Agricultural Research for entry-level technical positions across ICAR institutes. Suitable for candidates with diplomas or ITI in relevant trades. The selection typically includes a computer-based objective test (Tier-I) and possibly a skill/physical test or document verification.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

Diploma/ITI/Graduate in relevant discipline as specified per post in the notification. Some posts require specialized diplomas (Agriculture, Horticulture, Farm Machinery, Lab Technician etc.).

Age Limit

Usually 18–30 years (UR). Category-wise relaxations apply — check notification for exact age limits.

Nationality

Candidate must be an Indian citizen. PIO/OCI rules if applicable will be mentioned in notification.

Syllabus (Subject-wise Highlights)

Trade/Subject Specific

  • Agricultural Practices & Crop Production basics
  • Soil Science & Fertility
  • Farm Machinery fundamentals (if applicable)
  • Veterinary & Lab Techniques for lab technician posts
  • Greenhouse & Horticulture basics (for horticulture posts)

General Sections

  • General Intelligence & Reasoning
  • Basic Arithmetic & Data Interpretation
  • General Knowledge — Agriculture sector updates & national schemes
  • Computer Basics (where specified)

How to Apply — Step by Step

  1. Read the official ICAR T-1 notification PDF carefully for post-wise eligibility & documents.
  2. Register on the ICAR/portal specified in the notification using a valid email & mobile number.
  3. Fill the online application form, select post/trade, and upload scanned certificates (education, caste, domicile, photograph & signature).
  4. Pay the application fee (if applicable) through the online payment gateway.
  5. Submit the application and download the confirmation / challan for future reference.

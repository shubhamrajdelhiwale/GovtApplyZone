Recruiting Body

National Bank for Agriculture & Rural Development (NABARD)

Post Name

Grade A — Agriculture Specialist

Exam Mode

Online CBT + Interview

Eligibility Criteria

  • Educational Qualification: B.Sc / M.Sc Agriculture, Agri Engineering, Horticulture, Soil Science, Plant Breeding, Agronomy or equivalent.
  • Age Limit: Usually 21–30 years; category relaxations apply as per Govt. norms.
  • Nationality: Indian citizen.
  • Experience: Not mandatory, but relevant agriculture field experience is preferred.

Exam Pattern (Expected)

Phase 1 — Prelims

  • Reasoning
  • Quantitative Aptitude
  • English Language
  • General Awareness
  • Agriculture & Rural Development (ARD)

Phase 2 — Mains

  • Objective Paper (ARD)
  • Descriptive Paper (Agriculture Applied Concepts)

Phase 3 — Interview

Personality assessment & agriculture subject expertise evaluation.

Syllabus (Agriculture Specialist)

  • Crop Production & Management
  • Agro-Ecology & Soil Science
  • Plant Breeding, Genetics & Biotechnology
  • Irrigation Systems & Water Management
  • Seed Technology
  • Agricultural Economics
  • Fertilizer & Nutrient Management
  • Sustainable Agriculture & Precision Farming
  • Agri Machinery & Agri Engineering fundamentals
  • Government Schemes related to Agriculture

Salary & Benefits

NABARD Grade A officers receive an attractive pay scale.

  • Basic Pay: ₹44,500 per month
  • Gross Salary: ₹1,00,000+ per month (approx.)
  • House Rent Allowance (HRA)
  • Medical Coverage
  • Pension / NPS benefits
  • Travel & Accommodation Allowances

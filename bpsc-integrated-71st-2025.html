About BPSC Integrated 71st Combined Exam

The BPSC Integrated Combined Competitive Examination is the gateway to prestigious Group A and Group B posts in the Bihar State Government. It recruits officers through a multi-stage exam process consisting of Prelims, Mains, and Interview.

The 71st CCE marks another major recruitment cycle where thousands of aspirants will compete for limited seats in departments like Bihar Administrative Services (BAS), Bihar Police Services (BPS), Revenue Services, Supply Inspector, Panchayat Officer, and more.

The Prelims exam acts as a qualifying stage and includes objective-type questions based solely on General Studies.

Eligibility Criteria 2025

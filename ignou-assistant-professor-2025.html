Latest Updates

Latest news, notifications & recruitment updates for IGNOU Assistant Professor 2025.

IGNOU Assistant Professor 2025 Notification (Expected Soon)

Official notification expected to release in Q1 2025 covering department-wise faculty posts across IGNOU regional centres.

Apply Online (Expected)

Tentative registration period: Feb–Mar 2025. Application portal link will be updated after notification.

Vacancy Distribution

Vacancies expected across disciplines: Education, Management, Computer Science, Social Sciences, Sciences & Humanities.

Eligibility & UGC Norms

Selection based on UGC regulations – NET/PhD mandatory depending on department & specialization.

Recruiting Body

Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU), Delhi.

Eligibility Snapshot

Master’s degree + NET / PhD (as per UGC 2018/2023 regulations).

Selection Process

Shortlisting → Interview → Document Verification.

Eligibility & Qualification

  • Master’s degree with minimum 55% marks in relevant discipline.
  • NET qualification OR PhD Degree as per UGC Regulations (2016/2023).
  • Research publications in UGC-CARE/peer-reviewed journals desirable.
  • Experience in teaching / research / IGNOU course delivery preferable.
  • Relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/PwD as per Govt. of India rules.

Vacancy & Salary

IGNOU will release the official subject-wise vacancy distribution in the notification. Expected major departments:

  • Education
  • Computer Science
  • Management & Commerce
  • Social Sciences
  • Sciences & Humanities
  • Regional Centres & Study Centres

Salary (UGC Pay Level)

Assistant Professor Pay Level 10 (₹57,700 – ₹1,82,400) + allowances (DA, HRA, TA, medical).

Selection Process

  1. Shortlisting: Based on academic score, NET, PhD, research output & experience.
  2. Interview: IGNOU conducts subject-wise interviews with an expert panel.
  3. Document Verification: Qualification certificates, publications, identity proof etc.

Interview Topics (Important)

  • Subject expertise and recent developments.
  • Teaching methodology & understanding of ODL (Open & Distance Learning).
  • Research contributions, publications, methodology & future plans.
  • Curriculum development and ICT-based learning tools.
  • IGNOU’s distance learning model & course delivery mechanisms.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit IGNOU's official recruitment portal after notification release.
  2. Register using email, mobile number & create login credentials.
  3. Fill application form & upload required documents (degree, NET/PhD, publications).
  4. Pay application fee & submit final form.
  5. Take a printout of submitted form for verification.

Important Dates (Tentative)

  • Notification Release: Feb 2025
  • Apply Online Start: Feb 2025
  • Last Date to Apply: Mar 2025
  • Interview Schedule: Apr–Jun 2025

Eligibility & Qualification

  • Nationality: Indian / subject of Nepal or Bhutan / Tibetan refugee / person of Indian origin (as per IBPS rules).
  • Age: Minimum 20 years, Maximum 28 years (as on specified date in notification). Relaxations for reserved categories as per Government norms.
  • Educational Qualification: Graduate in any discipline from a recognized university.
  • Local Language: Proficiency in local language of the state/UT applied for is required (read official notification for details).
  • Computer Knowledge: Basic computer knowledge is typically required for clerical roles.

Vacancy & Salary

IBPS publishes state-wise vacancy lists and periodically revises totals. During CRP-CSA XV the vacancy figure was updated in the cycle — check IBPS vacancy PDF for the latest numbers.

Salary & Allowances

Clerical cadre pay differs across banks; typical in-hand pay for a Clerk/CSA during initial posting varies (basic pay + DA + HRA + allowances). For exact pay scales check the participating bank’s offer/scale of pay.

Exam Pattern (Quick)

Preliminary Exam (Objective)

  • Sections: English Language, Numerical Ability, Reasoning Ability.
  • Time: typically 60 minutes total (details & sectional timings in official notification).
  • Purpose: Screening for Mains; sectional/overall cut-offs applied.

Main Exam (Objective)

  • Sections include: General/Financial Awareness, General English, Reasoning & Computer Aptitude, Quantitative Aptitude, Data Analysis & Interpretation (exact sections/time as per notification).
  • Negative marking for wrong answers (usually 1/4th) — check notification for confirmation.

Syllabus Highlights

  • English: Reading comprehension, cloze test, error spotting, fill-in-the-blanks, para jumbles.
  • Numerical Ability / Quant: Arithmetic (percentages, ratios, averages), data interpretation, time & work, profit & loss.
  • Reasoning: Puzzles, seating arrangements, syllogism, inequalities, coding-decoding.
  • General & Banking Awareness: Current affairs, banking terms, RBI/financial news, PSB updates.
  • Computer Aptitude: Basic computer hardware/software, MS Office basics, internet and networking basics.

Selection Process

  1. Online Registration: Complete application & pay fee within the window announced in notification.
  2. Prelims: Online objective test for shortlisting to Mains.
  3. Mains: Online objective exam; marks used for final merit & allotment.
  4. Provisional Allotment: Based on merit and preferences; document verification may precede joining.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official IBPS Clerical Cadre page for CRP-CSA XV (link in hero button).
  2. Register with a valid email address and mobile number to receive login credentials.
  3. Fill in personal, educational and bank-preference details. Upload scanned photograph & signature as per guidelines.
  4. Pay the application fee online (fee varies by category) and submit. Save the registration number and print the confirmation.
  5. Keep scanned copies of all required documents ready (degree certificate, identity proof, category certificates if applicable).

6–8 Week Preparation Plan (Quick)

  1. Weeks 1–2: Basics & fundamentals — arithmetic, grammar, basic reasoning; daily current affairs.
  2. Weeks 3–4: Sectional tests, DI practice and puzzles; start previous-year papers.
  3. Weeks 5–6: Full-length mocks, speed & accuracy focus; refine weaker sections.
  4. Week 7–8: Revision, last-minute current affairs, practice moderate and high-difficulty mocks; document check.

Top Books & Free Resources

  • Quant — R.S. Aggarwal / Arun Sharma topicwise practice.
  • Reasoning — R.S. Aggarwal / M.K. Pandey for puzzles & seating.
  • English — Wren & Martin (grammar basics), practice RCs from previous IBPS papers.
  • Banking Awareness — Monthly current affairs for banking; RBI/IBPS circulars & summaries.
  • Mock Tests — Official IBPS sample papers & reputed test series for timed mocks.

Mini Practice Set

  1. Quant: If 5 years ago A's age was 1/3 of his present age, what will be his age after 10 years?
  2. Reasoning: In a row of 7, A is left of B but not adjacent. C sits at the right end... (practice seating arrangements).
  3. English: Practice one RC (200–250 words) daily; summarise and identify author's tone.

Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Accuracy matters — negative marking penalises random guesses.
  • Practice time management on DI sets; divide time between interpretation and calculations.
  • Follow official instructions for scanned document sizes/format to avoid rejection.
  • Check local-language requirement for the applied state; inability to produce proof may affect allotment.

KVS Eligibility 2025

  • PRT: Graduation + B.Ed/DElEd + CTET Paper 1.
  • TGT: Graduation in subject + B.Ed + CTET Paper 2.
  • PGT: Post Graduate Degree + B.Ed.
  • Age Limit: PRT up to 30 yrs, TGT up to 35 yrs, PGT up to 40 yrs (relaxations apply).

KVS Vacancy 2025

Vacancy numbers will be released post official notification. Expected large openings due to retirement cycle and new staffing requirements.

Selection Process

  1. Written Examination (Objective)
  2. Skill Test / Demo / Interview
  3. Document Verification
  4. Final Merit List

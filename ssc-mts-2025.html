SSC MTS 2025 — Key Details

Below is a concise collection of official and widely reported information candidates commonly look for. Always verify final details on ssc.gov.in or your regional SSC website.

Vacancies

The 2025 notification announced tentative vacancies (varies by region). Reported totals in public notices range from 1,075 to several thousand depending on aggregated sources and included posts — confirm region-wise vacancies on the official notice.

Important Dates (reported)

  • Notification release: 26 June 2025 (official release date reported with regional notices).
  • Application window (typical): 26 June 2025 — 24 July 2025 (check exact closing time on SSC portal).
  • Application correction window (if provided): end of July 2025 (regional pages list specific dates).
  • Exam window (reported/expected): from October–December 2025 depending on region.

Exam Pattern & Syllabus — Paper I

Paper I is a Computer Based Test covering four broad areas: General Intelligence & Reasoning, Numerical Aptitude, English Language & Comprehension, and General Awareness. Check the official syllabus PDF for topic-level details (topics include basic arithmetic, history/geography current affairs, grammar/vocabulary basics, and elementary reasoning).

Admit Card / Answer Key

Admit cards are released region-wise on SSC regional portals a few days before the scheduled exam. Provisional answer keys are normally uploaded post-exam with a window for representations.

How to verify official info

  1. Open ssc.gov.in and check the "Recruitment" / "Notices" section for the SSC MTS 2025 advertisement and PDF. (Region-wise links are also provided.)
  2. Download the official notification PDF and read sections on eligibility, fees, vacancies, and exam pattern exactly as published.
  3. Use the regional SSC portal if you need admit cards or region-specific vacancy lists.
Sources: publicly reported official notice and aggregator portals — date and vacancy figures vary by region; always cross‑check with the official SSC notice. (See links in page footer or SSC portal.)

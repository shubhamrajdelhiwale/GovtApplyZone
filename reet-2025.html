Syllabus Updated

Revised Level 1 & Level 2 syllabus for 2025 has been updated below.

Exam Name

REET (Rajasthan Eligibility Examination for Teachers) 2025

Qualification

D.El.Ed / B.Ed + required subject combination depending on Level.

Levels

Level 1 (Primary), Level 2 (Upper Primary)

REET 2025 Eligibility

Level 1 (Class 1–5)

  • 12th + D.El.Ed (2-year) OR
  • 12th + B.El.Ed OR
  • Graduation + D.El.Ed

Level 2 (Class 6–8)

  • Graduation + B.Ed OR
  • Graduation + B.El.Ed OR
  • 12th + B.El.Ed (Science/Maths/SST as per subject)

REET 2025 Syllabus

Level 1 Syllabus

  • Child Development & Pedagogy
  • Language I (Hindi/Sanskrit/English)
  • Language II
  • Mathematics
  • Environmental Studies

Level 2 Syllabus

  • Child Development & Pedagogy
  • Language I
  • Language II
  • Maths & Science (for Science Teachers)
  • Social Science (for SST Teachers)

How to Apply for REET 2025

  1. Visit the official REET website.
  2. Click “Apply Online REET 2025”.
  3. Fill personal and educational details.
  4. Upload photograph and signature.
  5. Pay application fee.
  6. Submit and download the application form.

REET 2025 Important Dates

  • Notification: Feb–March 2025
  • Application Form Start: March 2025
  • Last Date to Apply: April 2025
  • Admit Card: May 2025
  • Exam Date: May–June 2025

Eligibility & Qualifications

PRT Eligibility

  • Senior Secondary (50%) + D.El.Ed / JBT
  • CTET Paper 1 qualified

TGT Eligibility

  • Graduation in relevant subject + B.Ed
  • CTET Paper 2 qualified

PGT Eligibility

  • Master’s degree in subject
  • B.Ed (where required)

DSSSB Teacher Vacancy 2025

Expected vacancies across categories:

  • PRT — Large intake expected

  • PGT — Moderate openings in major subjects

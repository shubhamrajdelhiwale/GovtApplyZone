HTET 2025 Eligibility

Level 1 (PRT)

  • 12th + D.El.Ed OR 12th + B.El.Ed
  • Minimum 50% marks

Level 2 (TGT)

  • Graduation + B.Ed
  • Subject-wise qualifications required

Level 3 (PGT)

  • Post Graduation + B.Ed
  • Subject specialization mandatory

HTET 2025 Syllabus

Common areas for all levels:

  • Child Development & Pedagogy
  • Hindi & English Language
  • Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, GK
  • Environmental Studies (for PRT)
  • Subject-specific syllabus (TGT/PGT)

Exam Pattern 2025

  • Total Marks: 150
  • No Negative Marking
  • MCQ based exam
  • Duration: 2 Hours 30 Minutes

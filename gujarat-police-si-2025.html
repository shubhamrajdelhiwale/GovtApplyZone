How to Apply (Summary)

  1. Visit the official recruitment portal (link in notification).
  2. Register with your mobile and email.
  3. Fill the application form and upload documents (photo, signature, educational certificates).
  4. Pay application fee online.
  5. Download the confirmation page and keep it safe.

Always refer to the official PDF for exact dates. The recruitment portal is the legal source for applications and corrigenda.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university or equivalent. Specific posts (e.g., Technical) may require additional qualifications — check notification.

Age Limit

Minimum age: 18/21 years (as per notification). Maximum age usually around 28–35 years depending on category and post. Relaxation applicable for reserved categories as per government rules.

Nationality

Candidate should be a citizen of India and a resident of Gujarat (where specified) unless otherwise mentioned.

PMT (Physical Measurement Test) & PET (Physical Efficiency Test)

Typical PMT Standards

Male

  • Height: 168 cm (varies by category/district)
  • Chest: 81–86 cm (with expansion)

Female

  • Height: 155 cm (varies)
  • No chest requirement

Typical PET Standards

  • Male: 1600m run in 6 min 30 sec (example) or as per official standard — check notification for exact time.
  • Female: 800m run in 4 min 30 sec (example) — official standards may differ.
  • Long jump/High jump may also be part of PET for some selections.

Note: The exact PMT/PET standards are provided in the notification and can vary by post, district, and category. Some concessions may be available for ex-servicemen or differently-abled categories subject to rules.

Written Exam Syllabus — Gujarat Police SI

The written exam typically covers General Knowledge/Current Affairs, Gujarati Language, Mathematics, Reasoning, and Computer basics. Some recruitments include section-wise papers. Below is a detailed topic-wise breakdown to help your focused preparation.

General Knowledge & Current Affairs

  • Indian History — freedom struggle and modern India
  • Geography of India & Gujarat — rivers, districts, industries
  • Polity & Constitution — fundamental rights, governance
  • Indian Economy basics & state economy highlights
  • Science & Technology trends
  • Recent national and state current affairs

Gujarati Language

  • Grammar & vocabulary
  • Comprehension & précis writing
  • Letter/Application writing

Numerical Ability & Mental Aptitude

  • Basic arithmetic: percentage, profit-loss, ratio, time & work
  • Algebra, averages, mixtures
  • Data interpretation basics

Reasoning & Logical Ability

  • Analogies, series, coding-decoding
  • Syllogism, blood relations, directions
  • Puzzles and seating arrangements

Basic Computer Knowledge

  • MS Office basics (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
  • Internet, email and computer fundamentals

Exact marks distribution, number of papers, and negative marking (if any) will be specified in the official notification. Use previous year papers to understand the pattern and question difficulty.

Preparation Strategy — 6 Months Plan

Months 1–2: Basics & Foundation

  • Read state GK & Gujarat-specific facts (districts, economy, industry)
  • Finish Gujarati grammar basics and practice comprehension
  • Start basic arithmetic & reasoning topics

Months 3–4: Practice & Consolidation

  • Daily MCQ practice (75–100 Qs)
  • Weekly full-length mock tests (simulate exam conditions)
  • Start PET training: running, jumps & basic fitness

Months 5–6: Revision & Test Focus

  • Target weak topics & timed sectional tests
  • Take multiple full mocks and analyze errors
  • Intensify PET practice and maintain recovery

Daily Schedule Example

  • 1 hour: Gujarati language practice
  • 1 hour: General knowledge & current affairs
  • 1 hour: Quantitative & reasoning practice
  • 30–45 mins: Fitness / PET practice

Consistency and regular mock analysis separates top scorers from average. Keep a short revision notebook of facts & formulas.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the eligibility for Gujarat Police SI?
Bachelor's degree from a recognized university and meeting age & physical standards as per notification.
Is there negative marking in the written test?
Negative marking rules (if any) will be specified in the official notification. Check the PDF before taking mocks.
How to prepare for PET?
Start running & interval training early, practice jumps, maintain strength & flexibility, and follow a gradual training schedule to avoid injuries.
Where to get official notification?
Official notification will be available on the Gujarat Police recruitment portal or the state's official recruitment website. Always download the PDF from the authorised source.

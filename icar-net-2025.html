Conducting Body

ASRB (Agricultural Scientists Recruitment Board).

Purpose

Eligibility for Assistant Professor / Lecturer posts in Agricultural Universities.

Exam Mode

Online CBT — single paper of 150 questions.

ICAR NET 2025 Eligibility

  • Master’s degree in the respective subject with minimum 55% marks.
  • Age limit: Minimum 21 years (no upper age limit for NET eligibility).
  • Candidates must meet UGC/ICAR/ASRB norms for subject-specific qualification.

ICAR NET 2025 Syllabus

The syllabus is subject-specific. Major subjects include:

  • Agronomy
  • Soil Science
  • Plant Pathology
  • Agricultural Engineering
  • Animal Science
  • Horticulture
  • Plant Breeding & Genetics

Exam Pattern

  • Single Paper — 150 Multiple Choice Questions
  • Duration — 2 Hours
  • Marks — 150 Marks (1 mark each)
  • Negative Marking — 1/3rd for each wrong answer
  • Subjects chosen according to discipline

How to Apply

  1. Visit ASRB/ICAR NET official portal.
  2. Register using mobile number & email.
  3. Fill form & upload documents (photo, signature, certificates).
  4. Pay online fee.
  5. Submit form & download confirmation.

Conducting Body

National Testing Agency (NTA) conducts UGC NET on behalf of UGC for JRF & Assistant Professor eligibility.

Purpose

Determine eligibility for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) and Assistant Professor posts in Indian universities & colleges.

Mode & Frequency

Computer Based Test (CBT). Multiple cycles per year (check official notification for 2025 frequency).

Eligibility & Qualification

  • Master’s degree (or equivalent) with at least 55% marks (50% for reserved categories) from a recognized university.
  • Candidates appearing in final year may apply provisionally (subject to producing result by the date specified in notification).
  • No age limit for Assistant Professor. For JRF, there is an upper age limit (refer to official notification for exact age relaxation rules).
  • Reservation & relaxation as per Government of India norms.

Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

UGC NET usually consists of two papers (Paper I & Paper II). Basic pattern (subject to official change):

  • Paper I: Teaching & Research Aptitude — ~50 objective questions, 100 marks, common to all candidates, 1 hour.
  • Paper II: Subject-specific — ~100 objective questions, 200 marks, 2 hours (subject code based).
  • No negative marking typically for NTA UGC NET (confirm in notification).
  • Syllabus for Paper II depends on selected subject/discipline (download subject-wise PDF).

Syllabus Highlights

Paper I: Research aptitude, reasoning, comprehension, communication, ICT, mathematical reasoning, data interpretation, higher education system, teaching & learning.

Paper II: Detailed subject topics — e.g., for Commerce: accounting, finance, research methodology; for English: literature, criticism, linguistics. Always download the subject-wise syllabus PDF from the official portal.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official NTA UGC NET website once notification is published.
  2. Complete registration: provide personal details, educational qualifications and select subject code for Paper II.
  3. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required documents in the specified format.
  4. Pay application fee online (SBI/Net banking/cards/UPI as specified).
  5. Submit & take a printout of confirmation page for records and document verification.

8–12 Week Preparation Plan

  • Weeks 1–2: Finish Paper I topics — research aptitude, reasoning, data interpretation; revise basic maths and English comprehension.
  • Weeks 3–8: Deep dive Paper II subject-wise syllabus: read core books, make notes, and consolidate important concepts.
  • Weeks 9–10: Take full-length mock tests (timed) and analyze mistakes; strengthen weaker topics.
  • Final 2 weeks: Revision, short notes, formula sheets, and light mocks focusing on speed and accuracy.

Recommended Books & Free Resources

  • UGC NET Paper I books on Teaching & Research Aptitude (authors: Arihant / Trueman style compendiums).
  • Subject-specific standard texts (check your subject list & previous year papers).
  • Use NTA’s earlier question papers & official mock tests for practice.
  • Free online lectures & topic playlists (YouTube EDU channels, university lecture series).

Sample Mini Practice Set

  1. Paper I style: "Which research method is best suited for exploring perceptions in a cross-sectional survey?" (Choose best-fit option.)
  2. Paper II (subject-specific example — Commerce): "Explain the effect of discounted cash flow on project appraisal — outline key steps."
  3. Objective practice: "If mean = 50 and SD = 5, the z-score for 60 is ___."

Top Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Practice full-length CBT-style mocks to get comfortable with navigation and time management.
  • Do not ignore Paper I — it is qualifying and common to all subjects.
  • Make short, subject-specific revision notes for last-minute recall.
  • Read instructions carefully during application (subject codes, category certificates).

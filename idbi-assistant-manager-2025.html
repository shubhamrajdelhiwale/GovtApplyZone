Assistant Manager / Junior Manager — officer cadre roles in branches, operations, credit, IT, & specialized departments.

Selection Flow

Online Objective Test → Interview (where specified) → Final merit list & appointment as per vacancies and category reservations.

Eligibility & Qualification

  • Nationality: Indian citizen / subject of Nepal or Bhutan / person of Indian origin / Tibetan refugee (as per IDBI rules).
  • Age: Upper and lower age limits are specified in the notification; relaxations apply for reserved categories. Check the official PDF for exact cut-off dates.
  • Educational Qualification: Graduate/Postgraduate/Professional qualification depending on post (e.g., Bachelor’s/Master’s degree, MBA, CA/CS/IT specialization for technical roles). See stream-wise criteria in the notification.
  • Experience: Usually freshers are eligible for entry-level AM posts; some specialized roles may require prior experience.
  • Other Requirements: Meet IDBI's character, medical fitness and document requirements; local language proficiency may be required for certain postings.

Vacancy & Salary

IDBI publishes vacancy numbers and any revisions in the notification and separate vacancy PDF. Vacancy is typically broken down by category and location/department.

Salary & Allowances

Assistant Manager pay depends on IDBI pay scales and may include basic pay + DA + HRA + other allowances. In-hand salary during probation varies by posting and allowances. Refer to IDBI's notification or HR circular for exact pay scale and emoluments.

Exam Pattern (Quick)

Online Objective Test

  • Objective multiple-choice online examination covering sections such as Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, English Language, General/Banking Awareness, and Computer/Domain Knowledge (where applicable).
  • Duration, sectional marks and negative marking (if any) are specified in the official notification.
  • Some cycles include sectional cut-offs and overall qualifying marks for shortlisting to next stage.

Interview / Group Exercise

Shortlisted candidates may be called for an interview and/or group exercise for final selection. Weightage of interview vs written test is provided in the notification.

Syllabus Highlights

  • Reasoning: Puzzles, seating arrangements, syllogisms, inequalities, coding-decoding and logical reasoning.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Arithmetic (Percentages, Ratio & Proportion, Averages), Data Interpretation, Simplification, Mensuration and basic algebra.
  • English: Reading comprehension, cloze test, para-jumbles, error spotting, vocabulary and basic grammar.
  • General / Banking Awareness: Current affairs with emphasis on banking, finance, RBI policy, economic updates and basic banking products.
  • Domain / Computer Knowledge: For technical/specialized roles — basics of computers, programming concepts (where specified), branch operations and credit fundamentals.

Selection Process

  1. Online Registration: Apply through IDBI's recruitment portal within the prescribed window and pay the application fee (if applicable).
  2. Online Test: Objective test used to shortlist candidates for interview/next stage.
  3. Interview / GD: Personal Interview and/or Group Discussion for shortlisted candidates (where applicable).
  4. Document Verification: Verification of original documents for candidates recommended for appointment.
  5. Final Selection: Based on combined performance as per weights mentioned in notification and availability of vacancies.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit IDBI Bank's official careers/recruitment portal and open the Assistant Manager recruitment notification.
  2. Read the notification carefully to ensure you meet stream-wise eligibility and document requirements.
  3. Register with a valid email id and mobile number to obtain login credentials.
  4. Fill in the online application form with accurate personal, educational and preference details. Upload scanned photograph, signature and other documents as specified.
  5. Pay the application fee online (if applicable) and submit the form. Save the registration number and print the confirmation page for future reference.

8-Week Preparation Plan (Practical)

  1. Weeks 1–2: Strengthen basics — arithmetic, grammar, reasoning fundamentals and daily current affairs focused on banking.
  2. Weeks 3–4: Sectional practice (DI, puzzles, quant), start subject-wise mocks and previous year questions.
  3. Weeks 5–6: Focus on speed & accuracy; practice full-length timed tests and domain questions for specialized roles.
  4. Weeks 7–8: Revision, final mocks, interview preparation (if shortlisted) and ensure document readiness for verification.

Top Books & Free Resources

  • Quantitative Aptitude — R.S. Aggarwal / Arun Sharma for systematic practice.
  • Reasoning — R.S. Aggarwal / M.K. Pandey for puzzles & logical reasoning.
  • English — Wren & Martin for grammar; read editorials for comprehension practice.
  • Banking Awareness — RBI/IDBI circulars, current affairs compilations and banking portals.
  • Mock Tests — Reputed online test series & IDBI previous year/sample papers for exam-style practice.

Mini Practice Set

  1. Quant: If a sum triples in 10 years at simple interest, what is the annual rate of interest?
  2. Reasoning: Eight people A–H stand in a line with certain conditions — practice seating arrangements and inference questions.
  3. General Awareness: Explain the impact of a recent RBI policy change (e.g., repo rate movement) on lending rates.

Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Read the official notification carefully for post-specific eligibility — applying to the wrong stream can cause disqualification.
  • Maintain accuracy — negative marking (if applicable) penalizes random guessing.
  • Time management is critical for DI & reasoning sets; use sectional strategies practiced in mocks.
  • Keep digital and physical copies of all supporting documents ready in specified formats before applying.

SSC CGL 2025 Complete Information

SSC CGL 2025 (Staff Selection Commission Combined Graduate Level Examination) is one of the most competitive government exams in India. Every year, lakhs of graduates apply for SSC CGL to secure a stable and prestigious government job in central ministries, departments, and organizations.

The SSC CGL examination is conducted to recruit candidates for Group B and Group C posts such as Assistant Section Officer (ASO), Income Tax Inspector, Assistant Audit Officer (AAO), Auditor, Accountant, Sub-Inspector, and many more. Due to high salary packages, job security, and promotion opportunities, SSC CGL remains a top choice among aspirants.

SSC CGL 2025 Important Dates

As per previous trends, the SSC CGL 2025 notification is expected to be released in the second half of 2025. The application process usually remains open for about one month, followed by Tier 1 and Tier 2 examinations.

  • Notification Release: Expected November 2025
  • Online Application Start: November–December 2025
  • Tier 1 Exam: February–March 2026 (Tentative)
  • Tier 2 Exam: April–May 2026 (Tentative)

SSC CGL 2025 Eligibility Criteria

To apply for SSC CGL 2025, candidates must fulfill the required eligibility criteria in terms of age limit, educational qualification, and nationality. Any mismatch in eligibility may lead to disqualification at later stages.

Educational Qualification

Candidates must possess a bachelor’s degree from a recognized university. Certain posts require specific qualifications such as Statistics or Economics as a subject at the graduation level.

Age Limit

The age limit for SSC CGL varies by post and generally ranges between 18 to 32 years. Age relaxation is applicable for reserved categories as per government norms.

SSC CGL 2025 Exam Pattern

The SSC CGL examination is conducted in multiple tiers to assess a candidate’s knowledge, analytical skills, and suitability for different government posts.

Tier 1 (Computer Based Test)

  • General Intelligence & Reasoning – 25 Questions
  • General Awareness – 25 Questions
  • Quantitative Aptitude – 25 Questions
  • English Comprehension – 25 Questions
  • Total Marks: 200 | Duration: 60 Minutes

Tier 2 (Computer Based Test)

Tier 2 is the main examination stage and includes multiple papers depending on the post applied for. Performance in Tier 2 plays a crucial role in final selection.

SSC CGL 2025 Syllabus (Detailed)

A clear understanding of the SSC CGL syllabus helps candidates prepare in a focused and structured manner.

The General Awareness section covers History, Geography, Polity, Economy, Science, and Current Affairs. Quantitative Aptitude includes Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, and Data Interpretation. English Comprehension tests grammar, vocabulary, comprehension, and sentence structure.

SSC CGL 2025 Vacancy Details

SSC CGL 2025 is expected to announce more than 14,000 vacancies across multiple departments. The exact number of vacancies will be notified officially along with post-wise and category-wise distribution.

SSC CGL Salary Structure

The salary of SSC CGL posts is based on the 7th Pay Commission. The in-hand salary ranges from ₹25,000 to ₹1,50,000 per month, depending on the post, pay level, and allowances.

SSC CGL Cutoff Trends

SSC CGL cutoff marks vary every year based on exam difficulty, number of candidates, and vacancies. Analyzing previous year cutoffs helps candidates set realistic target scores.

To crack SSC CGL 2025, candidates should follow a disciplined study routine, revise regularly, and attempt mock tests frequently. Practicing previous year question papers improves speed and accuracy.

Consistency, time management, and conceptual clarity are the key factors that separate selected candidates from the rest.

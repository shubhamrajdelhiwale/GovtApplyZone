CDS Exam Pattern & Syllabus (Paper-wise)

Who conducts CDS?

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducts the Combined Defence Services Examination twice a year for recruitment to IMA, INA, AFA and OTA (Men & Women — check service-specific rules in the notification). :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

Written Exam — Pattern

CDS written test is a pen-and-paper objective exam. Paper composition depends on the academy applied to:

English

100 marks — comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, sentence correction & usage.

Duration: 2 hours.

General Knowledge

100 marks — current affairs, history, geography, polity, economy, science, defence & security.

Duration: 2 hours.

Elementary Mathematics

100 marks — arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, statistics (for IMA/AFA/INA as applicable).

Duration: 2 hours.

Note: IMA / INA / AFA require all three papers; OTA (Men & Women) usually do not require Mathematics paper — check the notification for exact paper combinations per academy. Full syllabus topics (topic lists) are in the official UPSC notice PDF. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}

Eligibility, Age Limits & Application

Eligibility criteria (educational qualifications, age limits and nationality) vary by academy and post. Typical requirements:

  • IMA / AFA / OTA / INA: specific degree/education requirements (e.g., graduate for IMA/OTA, B.Sc/Naval for INA/AFA — check exact clauses in the notification).
  • Age limits: vary by academy (e.g., IMA: 19–24 years historically). See notification for exact birth-date cutoffs and category relaxations.
  • Application: Apply online via UPSC portal — read the notice carefully for document requirements and the correction window dates. :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}

12-Week Preparation Plan — CDS I 2025

Designed for aspirants starting 3 months before the exam. Adjust daily hours depending on baseline (2–6 hrs/day if working; 6–10 hrs/day if full-time).

Weeks 1–4 — Foundation

  • Daily: 1.5 hrs English (reading & grammar), 1.5 hrs GK/current affairs, 1.5 hrs Maths basics/practice.
  • Start a current-affairs notebook (daily 20-minute summary of news relevant to defence & international affairs).
  • Weekends: one timed sectional test (2 hours), review mistakes immediately.

Weeks 5–8 — Consolidation

  • Increase mock cadence: 1 full mock every 4–5 days. Focus on speed & accuracy—time per question and elimination techniques.
  • Mental maths drills and formula sheet for quick revisions.

Weeks 9–12 — Mock Marathon & Polish

  • 2 full papers/week, simulate exam day (wake time, travel, exam center rules).
  • Final 10 days: light revision, error log corrections, sleep & nutrition prep.

SSB preparation: after written results, start personality & group-task practice — mock SSB sessions, communication skills, confidence building and officer-like appearance. :contentReference[oaicite:17]{index=17}

Practice Questions — Representative (Solved)

  1. English: Choose the correct sentence — "He insisted on (to go / going) alone." — Answer: "going".
  2. GK: Which treaty established the World Trade Organization? — Answer: The Marrakesh Agreement (1994).
  3. Maths: If cosθ = 3/5 and θ in first quadrant, find tanθ. — Solution: sinθ = 4/5 ⇒ tanθ = (4/5)/(3/5) = 4/3.

Do 200–400 past MCQs (timed) before your first full-length mock to build section familiarity.

SSB (Service Selection Board) — What to Expect

The SSB process is a multi-day personality and aptitude evaluation (interviews, group tasks, psychological tests, outdoor activities). It assesses officer-like qualities (OLQs): leadership, teamwork, initiative, problem-solving and communication. Shortlisted candidates from the written exam are called for SSB scheduling by the respective services. :contentReference[oaicite:18]{index=18}

Typical SSB stages

  • Screening test (if any) — OIR/PPDT
  • Psychological tests (TAT, WAT, SRT, PFT)
  • Group tasks & outdoor activities
  • Personal interview

How to prepare

  • Practice group discussion & group tasks with peers
  • Write & speak daily (mock interviews)
  • Physical fitness: pushups, running, required PFT standards

Cutoff Guidance & Target Scores

Cutoffs depend on vacancies, exam difficulty and service-wise demand. Practical approach: aim for previous-year cutoff + comfortable margin (10–15%) in mocks, maintain sectional balance and highest accuracy in GK/English to maximize merit.

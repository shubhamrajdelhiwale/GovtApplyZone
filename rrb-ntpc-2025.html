Conducting Body

Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) across regions — recruitment executed via common CENs and the central apply portal.

Posts Covered

Various NTPC posts: Junior Clerk, Senior Clerk, Goods Guard, Junior Accounts Assistant, Station Master/ASM, Traffic Assistant and others (post list differs by Graduate/UG CEN).

Selection Flow

Two-stage Computer Based Tests (CBT-1 & CBT-2). For some posts, CBT-2 is followed by skill/typing tests and DV/medical.

Eligibility (Key Points)

  • Nationality: Indian citizen / subject of Nepal or Bhutan / Tibetan refugee / person of Indian origin (as per CEN).
  • Age: Age limits differ by post & CEN — check exact upper/lower limits and date-of-birth cutoffs in the CEN PDF.
  • Educational Qualification: Graduate (for Graduate CEN) or 12th pass (for Undergraduate CEN) from a recognized board/university as on the date specified in the CEN.
  • Local/Language Requirements: Some posts may require proficiency in local language — read the regional instructions in the CEN.
  • Debarment: Candidates currently debarred by any RRB/RRC are ineligible (CEN note).

Vacancy & Pay

RRB notifications include state/zone-wise vacancy spreadsheets. The 2025 cycle announced multi-thousand vacancies split between graduate and undergraduate CENs — download the vacancy PDF from the CEN page for exact numbers per RRB and post.

Pay & Allowances

Pay scales vary by post and follow Indian Railways pay rules: basic pay + DA + HRA + other allowances. Exact pay band for each post is listed in the CEN/official documents.

Exam Pattern (Concise)

CBT-1 (Screening)

  • Objective online test — typical sections: General Awareness, Mathematics (Arithmetic), General Intelligence & Reasoning.
  • Marks/Questions: often ~100 questions / 100 marks (confirm exact scheme in the CEN).
  • Negative marking usually applies (e.g., 1/3rd for wrong answers) — check the official scheme.

CBT-2 (Mains)

  • CBT-2 is more comprehensive (120 questions / 90–120 minutes depending on CEN) covering deeper sections and post-specific papers in some cases.
  • CBT-2 scores are used for final shortlisting to skill/typing tests and DV for many posts.

Skill / Typing Test

Posts like Junior Accounts Assistant, Senior Clerk cum Typist, etc., may require typing or skill tests — qualifying criteria in the CEN.

Syllabus Highlights

  • General Awareness: Current affairs, Indian polity, history, geography, basic science, Indian Railways facts & basic economics.
  • Mathematics / Arithmetic: Number systems, percentages, ratios, averages, mensuration, time & work, speed & distance, profit & loss.
  • General Intelligence & Reasoning: Puzzles, seating, syllogisms, inequalities, coding-decoding, blood relations, directions.
  • Language: For some posts/CBT sections, basic English language questions (comprehension, grammar) may appear.

Selection Process

  1. Apply Online → submit application & fee in the window specified in the CEN.
  2. CBT-1: Screening exam — qualifying/shortlisting to CBT-2 based on cutoffs.
  3. CBT-2: Main exam — merit used to call for skill/typing tests (if applicable) & Document Verification.
  4. Skill/Typing Test: Qualifying for specified posts.
  5. Document Verification & Medical: Final stage prior to appointment.

How to Apply Online

  1. Go to the official RRB apply portal (link on the CEN page) and create/register with a valid email & mobile number.
  2. Read the detailed CEN PDF (Graduate/UG) for post-wise eligibility, important dates, fees and document specifications.
  3. Fill the online application carefully—personal, educational and post/zone preferences.
  4. Upload scanned photograph, signature and any required certificates in the prescribed format & size.
  5. Pay application fee online (if applicable) and submit. Save the registration number & print the confirmation receipt.

8–10 Week Preparation Plan (Practical)

  1. Weeks 1–2: Strengthen arithmetic basics and reasoning fundamentals; start daily GK/Current Affairs reading.
  2. Weeks 3–5: Sectional practice (DI, puzzles, quant); cover important railway/current affairs topics.
  3. Weeks 6–8: Full-length mocks (CBT-1 pattern), accuracy drills, and speed building for quant/DI.
  4. Weeks 9–10: CBT-2 level practice, skill/typing practice if applicable, and final revision of GK notes & common railway facts.

Recommended Books & Free Resources

  • Arithmetic & Reasoning — R.S. Aggarwal / Arun Sharma for topic-wise practice.
  • General Awareness — monthly current affairs magazines, The Hindu/Indian Express, and Railway facts from the CEN.
  • Previous Year Papers & Official CBT samples — practice rigorously for pattern familiarity.
  • Typing Practice — for posts requiring typing/skill tests (use online typing tools & region-specific speed requirements).

Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Read the CEN PDF fully — regional instructions and post-specific requirements vary and matter for eligibility.
  • Accuracy > attempts — negative marking penalises wrong answers — avoid guesswork.
  • Keep all supporting documents (DOB, education, category certificates) scanned in required formats before applying.
  • Note your regional RRB — admit cards, schedule & DV are handled by the respective RRB region.

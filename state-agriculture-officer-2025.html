Syllabus Updates

Agronomy, Soil Science, Horticulture, Plant Pathology & General Agriculture included.

Recruitment Body

State Agriculture Department / Public Service Commission (PSC).

Post Name

Agriculture Officer / Agriculture Development Officer.

Salary

₹35,000 – ₹1,12,000 + Grade Pay + DA + HRA.

Eligibility Criteria 2025

  • B.Sc Agriculture / B.Tech Agriculture Engineering / B.Sc Horticulture / Equivalent.
  • Minimum age: 21 years (varies by state).
  • Upper age limit: 35–40 years with relaxations for reserved categories.
  • Knowledge of local language may be required depending on the state.

Vacancy 2025

State-wise vacancies will be updated after official release. Expected distribution:

  • Agriculture Officer (AO)
  • Agriculture Extension Officer (AEO)
  • Agricultural Development Officer (ADO)
  • Horticulture Officer (HO)

Exam Pattern 2025

  • Paper 1 — General Studies (MCQ)
  • Paper 2 — Agriculture Subject (MCQ)
  • Interview / Document Verification
  • Total marks vary state-wise (150–300 marks average).

Syllabus 2025

General Agriculture

  • Agronomy & cropping systems
  • Soil Science & Fertility
  • Irrigation & Water Management
  • Agro-climatic zones

Discipline Specific

  • Horticulture & Plantation Crops
  • Plant Pathology & Diseases
  • Entomology & Pest Management
  • Seed Technology & Genetics

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official State PSC / Agriculture Department website.
  2. Complete registration using mobile & email.
  3. Fill online form & upload documents (photo, signature, degree certificate).
  4. Pay required application fee.
  5. Submit and download the application receipt.

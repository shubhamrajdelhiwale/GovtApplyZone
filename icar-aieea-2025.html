About ICAR AIEEA 2025

ICAR AIEEA (All India Entrance Examination for Admission) is the central national-level entrance exam for UG and PG agricultural courses conducted by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research. AIEEA UG/PG 2025 allows aspirants to seek admission to B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, M.Sc. and other postgraduate programmes at ICAR-designated universities and institutes.

The exam tests domain knowledge in Agriculture/Related subjects, General Aptitude, and subject-specific topics for PG aspirants. Seats are allocated as per the ICAR seat matrix and reservation rules.

Eligibility Criteria

  • AIEEA UG: 10+2 with required subjects (usually PCB/PCM/Agriculture) and minimum qualifying marks as per notification.
  • AIEEA PG: Bachelor’s degree in relevant agricultural/biological subjects; minimum percentage/grade varies by course.
  • Nationality: Indian citizens; foreign nationals should check institute-specific rules.
  • Age Limit: As specified in the official notification for certain categories or scholarships (if any).

Syllabus — Key Topics

AIEEA UG — Important Topics

  • Physics: Mechanics, Optics, Thermodynamics (as per 10+2)
  • Chemistry: Organic, Inorganic & Physical Chemistry basics
  • Mathematics/Biology: Based on 10+2 syllabus
  • Agriculture Basics: Intro to crop science, soil, and basic agronomy (if included)
  • General Aptitude & Reasoning (if applicable)

AIEEA PG — Important Topics

  • Subject-specific advanced topics (Agronomy, Plant Breeding, Soil Science, Horticulture, Entomology, etc.)
  • Research methodology, statistics for agriculture (where applicable)
  • Recent developments and applications in respective subject area

How to Apply — Step by Step

  1. Read the official ICAR AIEEA 2025 notification carefully for course-wise eligibility and instructions.
  2. Register on the ICAR exam portal with a valid email and mobile number.
  3. Fill in the application form, choose UG/PG stream & preferred exam centre(s).
  4. Upload scanned documents (photo, signature, qualifying certificates) as specified.
  5. Pay the application fee online and submit the form before the last date.
  6. Download the confirmation page and fee receipt; keep copies for future reference.

Preparation Tips

  • Cover NCERT basics for Physics, Chemistry, Maths/Biology (for UG).
  • For PG, revise core subject books and previous year ICAR/UGC papers.
  • Solve previous AIEEA question papers and time-bound mock tests.
  • Focus on agricultural fundamentals and recent developments in the sector.

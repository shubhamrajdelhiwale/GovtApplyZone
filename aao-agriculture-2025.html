Post Name

Assistant Agriculture Officer (AAO)

Required Qualification

B.Sc Agriculture / B.Sc Hons Agriculture

Salary

Pay Level 6–7 (Approx ₹35,400 – ₹1,12,400)

Eligibility Criteria

  • B.Sc Agriculture degree from a recognized university is mandatory.
  • Age limit generally 18–40 years (varies state-wise).
  • Relaxation applicable for SC/ST/OBC/EWS categories.
  • Basic knowledge of crop science, farm machinery, horticulture, soil science and agronomy required.

Selection Process

  1. Written Examination (Objective MCQs)
  2. Document Verification
  3. Final Merit List

Syllabus

  • Agronomy – Cropping systems, weed management, irrigation, fertilizers.
  • Soil Science – Soil types, structure, salinity, fertility management.
  • Horticulture – Fruits, vegetables, floriculture basics.
  • Plant Pathology – Pests, diseases & control measures.
  • Animal Husbandry basics.
  • Agricultural Engineering basics.
  • General Knowledge (state agriculture schemes).

How to Apply

  1. Visit official recruitment portal.
  2. Register using mobile number and email ID.
  3. Fill application form & upload documents.
  4. Pay application fee online.
  5. Submit & download final application print.

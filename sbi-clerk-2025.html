Eligibility & Qualification

  • Nationality: Citizen of India / subject of Nepal / Bhutan / person of Indian origin (as specified by SBI).
  • Age: Minimum and maximum age limits are indicated in the notification (typical lower limit 18 years; upper limit varies — check notification for exact cut-off and relaxations).
  • Educational Qualification: Graduate in any discipline from a recognized university/college as on the date specified in notification.
  • Local Language: Proficiency in local language of the applied state/zone may be required for some posts — check notification.
  • Other: No adverse criminal records; meet medical/fitness standards required by SBI.

Vacancy & Salary

SBI publishes detailed vacancy numbers (state/zone-wise) in the official notification. Vacancy counts may be updated during the recruitment cycle — always use the official PDF for final numbers.

Salary & Emoluments

Clerical cadre pay includes basic pay plus DA, HRA, and other allowances. In-hand pay during initial posting varies by location and allowances. Consult SBI’s recruitment notification or HR circulars for the exact pay scale and emoluments.

Exam Pattern (Overview)

Preliminary Exam

  • Objective online test — common sections: English Language, Numerical Ability, Reasoning Ability.
  • Duration is typically 60 minutes (sectional timings and marks detailed in notification).
  • Prelims is primarily a screening test for shortlisting to Mains.

Main Exam

  • Objective online test — sections commonly include: General/Banking Awareness, General English (or Local Language), Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, Computer Knowledge (format per notification).
  • Negative marking for wrong answers is usually applied (check official notification for exact scheme).

Syllabus Highlights

  • English / Local Language: Reading comprehension, cloze test, error detection, sentence improvement.
  • Numerical Ability: Arithmetic fundamentals (percentages, averages, ratio & proportion), data interpretation and simplification.
  • Reasoning: Puzzles, seating arrangements, inequalities, coding-decoding, syllogism.
  • General & Banking Awareness: Current affairs, banking terms, financial news, government schemes and basic economic concepts.
  • Computer Knowledge: Basics of hardware/software, MS Office, internet fundamentals.

Selection Process

  1. Online Registration: Submit application and pay the prescribed fee within the announced window.
  2. Preliminary Exam: Shortlisting exam to filter candidates for Mains.
  3. Main Exam: Objective online test (marks used for final selection/allotment).
  4. Final Allotment: Merit-based as per Mains score, vacancies and local-language/region criteria.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the SBI Careers portal and open the SBI Clerk recruitment notification for 2025.
  2. Register with a valid email address and mobile number to obtain login credentials.
  3. Fill in the online application form with personal, educational and preference details.
  4. Upload scanned photograph, signature and other documents in the specified format and size.
  5. Pay the application fee online (fee varies by category) and submit the form. Save the registration number and print the confirmation page.

8-Week Preparation Plan (Concise)

  1. Weeks 1–2: Strengthen fundamentals — arithmetic basics, grammar rules, reasoning building blocks and daily current affairs.
  2. Weeks 3–4: Sectional practice, DI sets, puzzles and previous-year papers for pattern familiarity.
  3. Weeks 5–6: Focus on speed & accuracy with timed mocks; practice Mains-level questions where applicable.
  4. Weeks 7–8: Full-length mock tests, revision of weak areas, final current affairs revision and document readiness.

Top Books & Free Resources

  • Quantitative Aptitude — R.S. Aggarwal / Arun Sharma for topic-wise practice.
  • Reasoning — R.S. Aggarwal / M.K. Pandey (puzzles & seating).
  • English / Language — Wren & Martin; practice RCs and descriptive writing.
  • Banking Awareness — Monthly current affairs, RBI releases, government scheme updates.
  • Mock Tests — Official sample papers and reputable online test series for timed practice.

Mini Practice Set

  1. Quant: If the sum of three consecutive even numbers is 48, what are the numbers?
  2. Reasoning: A sits to the immediate left of B. C sits opposite D. (Practice arrangement/puzzle formats.)
  3. English: Summarise a short 200-word passage in 40–50 words to build comprehension & summarising skills.

Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Prioritise accuracy — negative marking penalises incorrect attempts; avoid blind guessing.
  • Allocate time smartly across sections; many candidates lose marks by spending too long on DI.
  • Ensure uploaded document images meet SBI's size and format requirements to prevent rejection.
  • Check local language requirements for the applied region — inability to demonstrate local language proficiency may affect final placement.

All About UPSC ESE 2025

The UPSC Engineering Services Examination (ESE), also known as the Indian Engineering Services (IES), is one of India's most prestigious competitive exams conducted annually by the Union Public Service Commission. It recruits Class–I officers for top engineering posts in Central Government departments. Engineers selected through ESE hold highly respected roles in sectors involving national development, public infrastructure, defence, power, manufacturing, and public works.

The ESE exam consists of three stages—Prelims, Mains, and Personality Test. Candidates from four core branches—Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering—can apply. The competition is intense with lakhs of engineers applying every year for around 600–800 vacancies.

Engineering graduates looking for a prestigious, stable, and impactful career in the Government of India consider ESE as the finest opportunity. Officers recruited through ESE enjoy excellent career progression, powerful administrative roles, and the chance to contribute to national projects.

Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must hold a degree in Engineering from a recognized university. Age limit: 21–30 years (with upper age relaxation). Only CE, ME, EE, and ECE graduates are eligible.

Exam Structure

The exam has three stages: Prelims (Objective), Mains (Descriptive), and Interview. Prelims includes two papers, Mains includes two subject papers, and the final interview tests personality and engineering approach.

UPSC ESE 2025 Vacancy

The total number of vacancies for ESE 2025 is expected to be around 700+. The branch-wise distribution (tentative) is:

  • Civil Engineering: 250+
  • Mechanical Engineering: 170+
  • Electrical Engineering: 120+
  • Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering: 150+

Eligibility Criteria

1. Nationality

Indian citizen or as per UPSC rules.

2. Educational Qualification

Degree in Engineering from a recognized university.

3. Age Limit

21 to 30 years (as on 1st January 2025). Relaxation:

  • OBC: +3 years
  • SC/ST: +5 years
  • Ex-Servicemen: +5 years

Exam Pattern

Stage 1: Prelims

  • Paper I: General Studies & Engineering Aptitude – 200 marks
  • Paper II: Engineering Discipline (CE/ME/EE/ECE) – 300 marks

Stage 2: Mains

Two descriptive papers of engineering discipline — 300 marks each.

Stage 3: Interview

Personality Test — 200 marks

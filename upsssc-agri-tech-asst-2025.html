What is UPSSSC ATA?

UPSSSC Agriculture Technical Assistant (ATA) supports district/state agriculture departments in technical activities, lab work, extension support, seed & fertilizer testing, and farmer assistance. This recruitment is state-specific (Uttar Pradesh).

Eligibility

  • Education: Diploma / B.Sc (Agriculture) / relevant degree as specified in notification.
  • Age: Commonly 18–40 years with state category relaxations.
  • Local Requirements: Domicile or local language proficiency may be required.

Syllabus (Core Areas)

  • Crop Production & Improvement
  • Soil Science & Fertility Management
  • Plant Protection & Pests Management
  • Farm Machinery basics (if applicable)
  • Agricultural Extension & Government schemes

Exam Pattern

Pattern typically includes objective MCQs covering agriculture subjects, general aptitude and local knowledge sections. Refer official notification for marks and duration.

How to Apply

  1. Read the official UPSSSC notification for ATA carefully.
  2. Register on UPSSSC's recruitment portal and complete profile details.
  3. Fill the application form, upload required documents and pay fee online.

Preparation strategy & 12-week plan

Actionable plan for aspirants (3–5 hours/day). Mix concept revision, timed practice and physical conditioning.

Weeks 1–4: Foundation

  • Finish NCERT basics / static GK notes for state & national facts.
  • Daily arithmetic practice (30–45 min) + reasoning drills (30 min).
  • Light running & basic physical exercises (30 min) for stamina.

Weeks 5–8: Consolidation

  • Start full sectional mock tests; maintain error log.
  • Increase physical training: running intervals & strength work.
  • Revise high-frequency GK & practice state-specific questions.

Weeks 9–12: Mock marathon & polish

  • Full-length mock every 4–6 days; simulate exam timing & conditions.
  • Pre-PET practice: timed runs, long jump/high jump drills if required.
  • Final revision of error log and essential notes; document readiness for DV.

Tip: Track progress numerically (mock scores + PET timing) to measure readiness; if scores plateau, change resource or focus more on revision than new content.

Recruitment Body

Army Welfare Education Society (AWES).

Posts

PGT, TGT, PRT teacher posts across Army Public Schools.

Selection

OST Exam → Interview → Evaluation of Teaching Skills & Computer Proficiency.

Eligibility & Qualification

  • PGT: Post Graduation + B.Ed with minimum 50%.
  • TGT: Graduation + B.Ed with minimum 50%.
  • PRT: Graduation + D.El.Ed/B.Ed.
  • CTET/TET qualification mandatory for TGT & PRT.
  • Additional experience preferred for some APS schools.

Exam Pattern (OST)

  • Online Screening Test (MCQ based)
  • Negative marking applicable
  • Score is valid for 3 years
  • PGT/TGT: General Awareness, Reasoning, Pedagogy, Subject-specific questions
  • PRT: English, Reasoning, Pedagogy, Basic Teaching Aptitude

Salary Details

Salary varies by school but generally aligned with Army School norms:

  • PGT: ₹35,000 – ₹45,000+
  • TGT: ₹32,000 – ₹42,000+
  • PRT: ₹28,000 – ₹35,000+

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit AWES official website.
  2. Register using email & mobile number.
  3. Fill OST application form.
  4. Upload documents (photo, degree, certificates).
  5. Pay exam fee and submit the form.
  6. Download confirmation page for future use.

Post Type

Assistant General Manager (Technical) — Group A, prestigious FCI managerial role.

Qualification

Engineering / Agriculture / Food Tech / Biotech / Chemistry & related degrees.

Selection Stages

Online Exam → Interview → Document Verification.

Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility varies by discipline. Expected required qualifications:

  • B.Tech / B.E in Food Technology, Agriculture Engineering, Bio-Technology or relevant fields.
  • M.Sc in Agriculture, Microbiology, Chemistry, Food Science or allied disciplines.
  • Minimum 60% marks (relaxation for reserved categories as per norms).
  • Age Limit (Expected): 28–30 years depending on category.
  • Age relaxation applicable for OBC, SC, ST, PwD & departmental candidates.

Vacancy & Salary

FCI is expected to release multiple vacancies for AGM Technical across different zones.

  • Pay Scale: ₹60,000 – ₹1,80,000 (IDA Pay Scale).
  • House Rent Allowance (HRA).
  • Dearness Allowance (DA).
  • Medical benefits, Insurance, and Pension/NPS.

Exam Pattern (Expected)

  • General Aptitude: Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude & English.
  • General Management: HR, Finance, Leadership, Corporate Governance.
  • Technical Section: Subject-specific questions (Agriculture, Food Technology, Chemistry etc.).
  • Interview: Technical + managerial assessment.

Detailed Syllabus (Technical)

  • Agriculture Science, Agronomy, Soil Science
  • Food Technology & Food Chemistry
  • Plant Breeding, Plant Biotechnology
  • Microbiology, Biochemistry
  • Chemical Properties of Food Grains
  • Warehouse, Storage & Quality Control Standards
  • Engineering Concepts in Agriculture

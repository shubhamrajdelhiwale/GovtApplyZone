About CTET July 2025

The Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) is conducted by CBSE to assess the eligibility of teachers for Classes 1 to 8. CTET certificate is now valid for lifetime. CTET July 2025 consists of two papers:

  • Paper 1 — For Classes I–V
  • Paper 2 — For Classes VI–VIII

Eligibility

Paper 1 (Classes I–V)

  • Senior Secondary (50% marks) + D.El.Ed / B.El.Ed
  • Graduation + B.Ed (as per latest norms)

Paper 2 (Classes VI–VIII)

  • Graduation + D.El.Ed / B.Ed
  • Post Graduation + B.Ed

CTET July 2025 Syllabus

Paper 1

  • Child Development & Pedagogy
  • Language I & II
  • Mathematics
  • Environmental Studies

Paper 2

  • Child Development & Pedagogy
  • Language I & II
  • Mathematics & Science (for Maths/Science teachers)
  • Social Science (for Social Studies teachers)

Exam Pattern

  • Mode: Offline (OMR based)
  • Type: MCQ
  • No negative marking
  • Duration: 2.5 hours

How to Apply

  1. Visit CTET official website (link will be updated).
  2. Register with email & mobile.
  3. Fill the application form with correct details.
  4. Upload photograph & signature.
  5. Pay the online fee and submit the form.

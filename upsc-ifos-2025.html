IFoS 2025 — Quick Overview

The Indian Forest Service (IFoS) Examination (IFoS / IFSP) is conducted by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC). For 2025 UPSC issued the IFoS notification on 22 Jan 2025 and the exam follows the Civil Services Prelims schedule for the preliminary stage and a separate IFoS Mains timetable for the mains stage. Key official references: the IFoS notification PDF and the UPSC Mains timetable. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

  • Prelims: IFoS Preliminary is held through Civil Services (Preliminary) Exam — check CS(P) dates for the exact prelims date. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}
  • Mains: UPSC published IFoS (Main) timetable for the 2025 mains session (official PDF/timetable). Example mains schedule issued for November 16–23, 2025 (see UPSC mains timetable). :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}
  • Selection stages: Prelims (Objective) → Mains (Written, discipline-specific papers) → Interview/Personality Test.

Exam Pattern & Full Syllabus (IFoS 2025)

Preliminary Examination (conducted with Civil Services Preliminary)

IFoS Prelims is part of the Civil Services Preliminary Examination (Paper I — GS and Paper II — CSAT). Both papers are objective type. Candidates who clear Prelims and opt for IFoS appear for IFoS Mains consisting of discipline-specific papers. See the official notification for exact qualifying rules. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

Mains Examination — Structure (discipline-specific)

IFoS Mains consists of written papers specific to the candidate’s optional (forestry / agriculture / botany / zoology / engineering disciplines etc.) plus common papers where applicable. UPSC provides detailed syllabus for each optional subject in the notification PDF. Download official syllabus from UPSC. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

Common Topics (useful for all aspirants)

  • Ecology & Environment (ecosystems, biodiversity, conservation biology)
  • Forest Ecology, Forest Management & Silviculture
  • Soil Science & Watershed Management
  • Remote Sensing, GIS & Forest Mensuration
  • Wildlife Management & Conservation Laws
  • Climate Change & International Conventions

Popular Optional Subjects (sample)

  • Forestry (core) — Silviculture, Forest Mensuration, Forest Management
  • Agriculture / Botany / Zoology — theory + applied sections
  • Agricultural Engineering / Mechanical / Civil (discipline papers) — if opted

For subject-wise paper names, marks distribution and exact topic list, consult the official IFoS notification PDF (UPSC). :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

Practical 12-Week Study Plan (IFoS 2025)

Designed for aspirants who cleared prelims or are preparing from scratch — adapt daily hours to your baseline (6–10 hrs/day recommended for full-time aspirants).

Weeks 1–4: Foundation

  • Week 1: Syllabus mapping — break down each paper to subtopics; assemble standard books and previous-year lists.
  • Weeks 2–4: Core theory for Ecology, Silviculture, Forest Mensuration; daily topic notes + 20 practice questions.

Weeks 5–8: Consolidation & Application

  • Weekly full-length subject test + 2 timed practice papers; start revision notes and formula sheets (mensuration formulas, sampling methods).
  • Begin GIS & RS basics with 30 practical questions and map-reading drills.

Weeks 9–12: Mock Marathon & Final Polishing

  • 2 full mains-style papers/week; daily revision of weak topics flagged in error log.
  • Polish answer writing for mains — practice structured answers (intro → body → conclusion) and diagrams/sketches where needed.

Error-log template (copy to your notebook): DATE | TEST | Q.No | Topic | Error Type | Correct Answer | Fix Plan

Practice — Sample Questions & Previous Papers

UPSC’s previous year papers are the single best resource for mains-style questions and subject emphasis. Download subject-wise PDFs from UPSC’s previous questions page. :contentReference[oaicite:15]{index=15}

Sample (Prelims-style) Questions

  1. Ecology: Which of the following is an example of a keystone species? — (Answer & explain: e.g., Sea otter in kelp ecosystems).
  2. Forest Mensuration: Calculate the basal area (m²) of a tree with DBH 40 cm. — (Basal area = π*(d/200)^2 ≈ 1.256 m²).
  3. RS/GIS: Difference between supervised and unsupervised classification in remote sensing (short answer).

Cutoff Strategy & Target Scores

IFoS cutoffs vary with vacancies and difficulty. Practical approach: set targets by aiming for previous-year mains aggregate cutoff + a margin (8–12%). Use mocks to estimate percentile; focus on maximizing marks in your strongest optional papers.

Frequently Asked Questions — IFoS 2025

Q: Where can I download the official IFoS notification & syllabus?

Q: When are IFoS Prelims & Mains held in 2025?

Q: How many stages are there in IFoS selection?
A: Three major stages — Prelims (objective), Mains (written, discipline-specific) and Personality Test (interview). Marks from Mains and interview determine final merit. :contentReference[oaicite:20]{index=20}

Preparation strategy & 12-week study plan

Actionable strategy focused on speed, accuracy and PET readiness.

Weeks 1–4: Fundamentals

  • Finish basics: Hindi grammar, arithmetic, NCERTs for GK fundamentals.
  • Daily 45–60 min current affairs (national + UP).
  • Start light physical training (running 15–20 min 4x/week).

Weeks 5–8: Consolidation

  • Sectional mock tests; fix weak areas from the error log.
  • Increase PET training: interval sprints, long jump practice.
  • Start answer-writing (for aspirants who will also prepare for written descriptive rounds in other posts).

Weeks 9–12: Mock marathon

  • Full-length mock every 4–6 days under exam conditions.
  • Final 10 days: light revision & document readiness for DV.
  • Maintain sleep, diet & avoid last-minute resource changes.

Tip: pair timed CBT practice with PET drills — both are qualifying and affect final selection. :contentReference[oaicite:22]{index=22}

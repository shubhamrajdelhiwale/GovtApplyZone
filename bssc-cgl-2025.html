About BSSC CGL Exam 2025

The BSSC CGL Exam 2025 is a competitive recruitment examination conducted to fill Grade B and Grade C posts in various Bihar Government departments. These include Administrative Assistant, Planning Assistant, Secretariat roles, and several other prestigious positions.

The exam consists of Prelims, Mains, and Document Verification. Each stage evaluates a candidate’s reasoning ability, general knowledge, mathematics, and decision-making skills.

With thousands of applicants every year, BSSC CGL is considered one of Bihar’s top competitive exams.

Eligibility Criteria

Education

Graduation in any stream from a recognized university.

Age Limit

UR: 21–37 yrs
OBC/BC: 21–40 yrs
SC/ST: 21–42 yrs

Nationality

Must be a citizen of India and a resident of Bihar for reservation benefits.

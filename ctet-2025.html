CTET 2025 Eligibility

  • Paper 1 (Class 1–5): 12th Pass + D.El.Ed / B.El.Ed.
  • Paper 2 (Class 6–8): Graduation + B.Ed OR B.El.Ed with specialization.
  • Minimum Marks: 45%–50% (as per NCTE norms).
  • Reservation: Category relaxation as per Govt rules.

CTET 2025 Exam Pattern

  • Paper I: 150 Questions — 150 Marks
  • Paper II: 150 Questions — 150 Marks
  • No Negative Marking
  • Duration: 150 Minutes per paper
  • Mode: Offline OMR

How to Apply for CTET 2025

  3. Upload scanned documents (photo, signature).

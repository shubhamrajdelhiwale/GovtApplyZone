Maharashtra TET 2025 – Notification, Eligibility, Syllabus & Pattern

MAHA TET 2025 is the state-level Teacher Eligibility Test conducted for recruitment of Primary and Upper Primary teachers in Maharashtra government schools. Here you get complete details: notification, eligibility, paper-wise syllabus, exam pattern, qualifying marks, books & preparation plan.

Exam Name

Maharashtra Teacher Eligibility Test (MAHA TET) 2025

Conducting Body

Maharashtra State Examination Council (MSCE)

Exam Levels

Paper 1 (Class 1–5), Paper 2 (Class 6–8)

Maharashtra TET 2025 Notification Details

The MAHA TET notification includes exam dates, paper structure, eligibility, Certificate validity, application fee, and guidelines. Candidates must check the official PDF on the MSCE website.

Important Dates (Expected)

  • Notification Release: April 2025
  • Application Period: April–May 2025
  • Admit Card: June 2025
  • Exam Date: July 2025

Eligibility Criteria

Paper 1 (Class 1–5)

  • 12th pass with minimum 50% + D.El.Ed / B.El.Ed
  • OR 12th + D.Ed (Special Education)

Paper 2 (Class 6–8)

  • Graduation + B.Ed
  • OR Graduation + D.El.Ed
  • OR B.El.Ed / B.Ed (Special Education)

Age Limit

Minimum 18 years. No upper age limit for TET qualifying.

Maharashtra TET 2025 – Paper-wise Syllabus

Paper 1 Syllabus (1–5)

  • Child Development & Pedagogy
  • Language 1 (Marathi / Hindi / Urdu)
  • Language 2 (English)
  • Mathematics
  • Environmental Studies

Paper 2 Syllabus (6–8)

  • Child Development & Pedagogy
  • Language 1 & 2
  • Mathematics & Science (for Math/Science teachers)
  • Social Studies (for Social Science teachers)

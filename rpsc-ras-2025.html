About RPSC RAS

RPSC RAS (Rajasthan Administrative Service) is the premier state-level civil service examination conducted by the Rajasthan Public Service Commission to recruit administrative officers for the Government of Rajasthan. The selection is through a multi-stage process — Prelims (objective), Mains (descriptive) and Interview (personality test).

RAS officers are posted across departments handling administration, planning, revenue, and development. Preparation requires an integrated approach: deep reading for Mains, answer-writing practice, and continuous revision of Rajasthan-specific topics.

How to Apply (Quick Steps)

  1. Visit the RPSC official portal and register.
  2. Complete application form and upload required documents.
  3. Pay the application fee online.
  4. Submit and take a printout of confirmation page.

Keep scanned copies of education certificates, ID proof and photograph in required dimensions for a smooth upload process.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

A bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university or an equivalent qualification as specified in the notification. Certain posts may have additional requirements — check the official document.

Age Limit

The minimum age is usually 21 years. Upper age limit varies by post and category (typical range 35–40 for general category); relaxations exist for reserved categories as per rules.

Domicile & Language

Rajasthan domicile and proficiency in local language may be required for certain posts. Confirm domicile clauses in the notification.

Exam Pattern — Prelims & Mains

RAS follows a three-stage selection — Prelims (screening objective), Mains (descriptive papers), and Interview. Prelims is qualifying for Mains in many cycles but check current notification whether Prelims marks count towards final merit.

Detailed Syllabus — Prelims & Mains (Indicative)

Prelims — General Studies (Indicative)

  • History: Rajasthan & India — ancient, medieval, modern (freedom movement)
  • Geography: Physical, economic & human geography of Rajasthan and India
  • Polity & Governance: Constitution, state administration, public policy
  • Economy: Indian economy & Rajasthan state economy
  • Environment & Ecology, Science & Technology basics
  • Current Affairs: national & state-level

Mains — Papers & Topics

Mains includes several descriptive papers: language papers (qualifying), GS papers (essay-type) and Optional subject papers. Sample paper-wise focus:

  • Essay: Contemporary issues, socio-economic topics
  • GS Paper I: History, Culture of Rajasthan & India
  • GS Paper II: Governance, Administration, Public Policy
  • GS Paper III: Economy, Development, Social Justice
  • GS Paper IV: Science, Environment, Disaster Management
  • Optional Papers: Two papers from selected subject

Check official notification for exact paper titles and marks. Mains requires rigorous answer-writing practice and factual accuracy, especially on Rajasthan-specific issues.

Optional Subjects — Choosing Strategy

Popular optionals include Geography, Public Administration, History, Political Science, Sociology, Economics, Law, Agriculture, and subjects from humanities/science streams. Tips:

  • Prefer a subject with overlap with GS for efficiency.
  • Choose based on background, interest and availability of good resources.
  • Consider scoring trends and availability of coaching/materials.

Preparation Roadmap — 9–12 Month Plan

Months 1–3: Foundation

  • NCERTs for history, geography & basic polity
  • Start Rajasthan-specific notes (history, geography, schemes)
  • Choose optional and begin reading basics

Months 4–6: Prelims Focus

  • Topic-wise MCQs & weekly mock tests
  • Revision and current affairs consolidation
  • Maintain short notes for quick revision

Months 7–9: Mains Writing

  • Daily answer writing practice (150–300 words)
  • Practice previous year mains questions & model answers
  • Peer reviews and timed writing

Final Phase: Interview

  • Mock interviews & confidence-building
  • Current affairs revision & state governance memoranda
  • Polish communication skills & personality traits

Balance depth and breadth. Consistent mocks, iterative revision and disciplined answer writing win mains and interview stages.

Frequently Asked Questions — RPSC RAS 2025

Who can apply for RPSC RAS 2025?
Graduates from a recognized university fulfilling age and other criteria mentioned in the official notification. Certain posts may have specific qualifications.
Does Prelims count for final merit?
Depends on the cycle — often Prelims is a screening test only. Confirm in notification whether Prelims marks are added to final tally.
How to choose optional subject?
Base your choice on background, interest, overlap with GS and availability of resources/coaching for that subject.
Where to download official notification?
Download from the RPSC official website — the PDF there is the legal document. Verify dates & details only from official PDF.

About TN TRB Teacher Recruitment 2025

Tamil Nadu Teachers Recruitment Board (TN TRB) conducts teacher recruitment for government schools, higher-secondary institutions, and special education categories. The exam includes Paper-I/II for TET, subject specialization papers for Secondary Grade Teachers and PG Assistants, and professional ability assessment through certificates and document verification.

TRB exams are crucial for securing teaching positions across Tamil Nadu Government Schools under the School Education Department. More than 4 lakh+ aspirants apply each year. The competition level requires structured preparation, strong pedagogy knowledge, and mastery of subject concepts.

The recruitment includes levels such as Post Graduate Assistant (PG), Graduate Teacher (BT), Primary Teacher (PRT), Special Teacher, and Computer Instructor posts. Each role has specific educational qualifications, TET requirements, and exam patterns.

Eligibility Criteria for TN TRB 2025

1. Educational Qualifications

  • PRT – D.El.Ed / B.El.Ed
  • BT Assistant – B.Ed + UG Degree
  • PG Assistant – Master’s Degree + B.Ed
  • Special Teachers – Diploma/Certificate in respective subjects
  • TET Paper-I or II qualification (as required)

2. Age Limit (Expected)

  • Minimum Age: 21 years
  • Maximum Age: 57 years (as per TN Govt rules)

TN TRB Exam Pattern 2025

PG Assistant – Paper Pattern

  • Single written exam (Objective)
  • Total Marks – 150
  • Duration – 3 hours
  • Sections – General Knowledge + Main Subject

BT Assistant Pattern

  • Objective Exam
  • 150 Marks
  • Consists of Tamil, GK, Pedagogy & Subject

SGT / PRT TET Pattern

  • Paper-I (Classes 1–5)
  • Paper-II (Classes 6–8)
  • Each paper: 150 marks

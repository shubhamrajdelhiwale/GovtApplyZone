Delhi Police Constable Exam Date 2025

SSC Constable Executive (Male/Female) – 7565 Posts

Published by Govt Apply Zone

Short Information

Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the exam date notice for Constable Executive (Male & Female). Recruitment is for 7565 posts. Applications were accepted from 22 September 2025 to 31 October 2025. The CBT examination will be held between 18 December 2025 to 06 January 2026.

  • ⏳ Last Date: 31 October 2025
  • 💳 Fee Payment Last: 31 October 2025
  • ✏ Online Correction: Notify Later
  • 📝 Exam Date: 18 December 2025 – 06 January 2026
  • 🎫 Admit Card: Before Exam

  • SC / ST: 0/-

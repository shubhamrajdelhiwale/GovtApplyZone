All About TSPSC Group 1

TSPSC Group 1 is conducted by the Telangana State Public Service Commission to recruit top-level administrative officers such as Deputy Collector, DSP, Commercial Tax Officer, Municipal Commissioner, District Registrar, and more. It is the state’s most competitive exam with lakhs of aspirants appearing every cycle.

The selection process includes: Preliminary Exam → Mains (Descriptive) → Interview.

This page contains complete details about the 2025 cycle including syllabus, eligibility, exam pattern, vacancy details, cutoff expectations, study plan and recommended books.

Exam Type

State Administrative Services – Telangana Group 1 posts.

Eligibility

Graduation in any discipline from a recognized university.

Selection Stages

Prelims → Mains → Interview.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

A Bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university.

Age Limit

Typical age range: 18–44 years (varies by post).

  • OBC: +3 years
  • SC / ST: +5 years
  • PwD: +10 years

Nationality

Must be an Indian citizen.

Exam Pattern

Preliminary Exam

Single objective-type paper.

  • General Studies & Mental Ability – 150 Questions
  • Total Marks – 150
  • Duration – 2.5 hours

Mains Exam (Descriptive)

  • English (Qualifying)
  • Essay
  • General Studies 1 – Culture, History, Geography
  • General Studies 2 – Polity, Constitution, Governance
  • General Studies 3 – Economy, Development, Science & Tech
  • General Studies 4 – Society, Ethics
  • General Studies 5 – Telangana Economy, Society
  • General Studies 6 – Telangana Movement, Formation

Interview

Final personality assessment for service allocation.

Detailed Syllabus

Prelims Syllabus

  • Indian History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern)
  • Geography – India & Telangana
  • Polity – Constitution, Governance
  • Economy – Indian & Telangana
  • Science & Technology
  • Logical Reasoning, Mental Ability
  • Current Affairs (National + Telangana)

Mains Syllabus (Paper-wise)

  • Paper I – Essay (National Issues, Social Issues, Telangana topics)
  • Paper II – History, Culture, Geography
  • Paper III – Constitution, Governance, Public Administration
  • Paper IV – Economy, Planning, Telangana Economy
  • Paper V – Science, Technology, Environment
  • Paper VI – Society, Ethics, Behaviour
  • Paper VII – Telangana Movement & State Formation

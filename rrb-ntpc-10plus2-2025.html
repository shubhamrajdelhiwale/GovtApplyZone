Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has published the official notification for RRB NTPC 10+2 Under Graduate Level Recruitment 2025 for 3058 posts. Online applying started from 28 October 2025 and the last date to apply is 27 November 2025. Read the full notification and official updates for exam date notices, vacancy breakup, educational qualification, selection process and how to apply below.

Important Dates

  • Application Start Date: 28 October 2025
  • Last Date to Apply: 27 November 2025
  • Fee Payment Last Date: 29 November 2025
  • Correction / Modified Form: 30 November – 09 December 2025
  • Exam Date: Notify Soon (Check Official Notice)
  • Admit Card: Before Exam

Application Fee

  • General / OBC / EWS : ₹500/-
  • SC / ST / PH : ₹250/-

  • Payment Mode: Debit / Credit / Netbanking / UPI etc.

Age Limit (as on 01.01.2026)

Minimum Age: 18 Years | Maximum Age: 30 Years. Age relaxation as per RRB rules.

Educational Qualification

Commercial Cum Ticket Clerk / Accounts Clerk Cum Typist / Junior Clerk Cum Typist:

10+2 Intermediate Exam from any recognized board in India. For General/OBC/EWS — 50% marks; SC/ST/PH — pass only.

Train Clerk:

10+2 Intermediate pass (see official notification for post-wise details).

Typing Requirement (where applicable):

English Typing 30 WPM or Hindi Typing 25 WPM (as per post requirement).

Selection Process

  • Computer Based Test (CBT) – Stage I / Stage II as applicable
  • Typing Test (if applicable for post)
  • Document Verification
  • Medical Examination

How to Apply

  1. Read the official RRB NTPC 10+2 notification carefully.
  2. Visit the RRB official website: indianrailways.gov.in or use links below.
  3. Register / Login and fill the online application form.
  4. Upload documents and pay the required fee.
  5. Submit and take a printout of the final application.

SSC Stenographer Syllabus & Exam Pattern — Paper I (CBT)

Paper I is an objective Computer Based Test covering three sections. Below is a compact, study-friendly breakdown — use the official SSC syllabus PDF for exact wording and marks distribution. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

Sections (typical)

  • General Intelligence & Reasoning — analogies, classifications, series, coding-decoding, puzzles, logical reasoning.
  • General Awareness — current affairs (last 12 months), history, polity, economy basics, geography, science & environment.
  • English Language & Comprehension — grammar, vocabulary, comprehension passages, error spotting, sentence improvement.

12-Week Preparation Plan — SSC Stenographer 2025

This day-by-day plan balances concept, speed and practice for Paper I plus skill test readiness. Adjust hours per your baseline (target 3–6 hrs/day if full-time student; 1.5–3 hrs if working).

Weeks 1–4 — Foundation

  • Daily: 1 hour GA (current affairs), 1 hour English, 1 hour reasoning.
  • Weekends: full-length sectional test (90 min) and error analysis.
  • Start shorthand/typing practice (20–30 mins daily) if you need skill improvement.

Weeks 5–8 — Consolidation

  • Increase practice volume: 2 full mock tests per week + topic drills.
  • Shorthand/typing: increase to 45–60 mins daily focusing on accuracy and speed.

Weeks 9–12 — Mock Marathon & Polishing

  • 4 full tests per week (timed), strong focus on error log and elimination strategy for wrong answers.
  • Polish shorthand/typing on real test paper formats and simulate skill test conditions.

Use an error log: DATE | TEST | Q.No | Topic | Mistake Type | Fix plan. Drill recurring mistakes immediately.

Practice Questions (Representative)

  1. Reasoning: Next in pattern: 3, 6, 11, 18, 27, ? — Solution: differences 3,5,7,9 → next diff 11 → 27+11 = 38.
  2. English: Choose correction: "He don't like apples." — Correct: "He doesn't like apples."
  3. GA: Name the agency that publishes the World Economic Outlook — Solution: International Monetary Fund (IMF).
  4. Quant (basic): Solve x/ (x+2) = 3/4 → x = 6.

Do 500+ MCQs across sections before your first full mock.

Selection Process

Typical flow: Paper I (CBT) → Skill Test (Shorthand/Typing for those who qualify) → Document Verification → Final Merit/List. Skill tests are qualifying in nature but failing to meet skill test standards results in disqualification.

UPSSSC PET 2025 — Apply Online, Syllabus, Eligibility & Preparation

UPSSSC PET (Preliminary Eligibility Test) is a gateway exam for thousands of Uttar Pradesh government job aspirants. This page gives a complete breakdown of notification details, application process, exam pattern, section-wise syllabus, scoring strategy and a robust preparation plan for PET 2025.

UPSSSC PET 2025 — Notification Highlights

The official notification specifies: application start & end dates, exam date, application fee, eligibility conditions, syllabus, exam centres, reservation & relaxation details and instructions on how to apply. Always download and read the notification PDF from the UPSSSC website to ensure compliance with the rules.

How to Apply — Quick Steps

  1. Visit the UPSSSC official portal and create/ login to your account.
  2. Complete the PET online application form with accurate details.
  3. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required supporting documents.
  4. Pay the application fee online (as per category).
  5. Submit and download the confirmation/receipt — keep it safe.

Ensure your Aadhaar and other ID details match your application to avoid verification issues later.

Eligibility Criteria — UPSSSC PET 2025

Educational Qualification

Minimum educational qualification varies by post — typically candidates must have completed 10th / 12th / Graduation as specified in the specific recruitment notice. PET is a preliminary screening; check target recruitment requirements for precise eligibility.

Age Limit

Age limits depend on the post being applied for in later recruitment stages. PET itself often has a general age range stated in the notification. Government norms allow age relaxation for reserved categories; verify exact figures in the official document.

Domicile & Residency

Certain recruitments require Uttar Pradesh domicile. PET qualifying certificate may be used for multiple UPSSSC recruitments across the state.

Exam Pattern — UPSSSC PET

UPSSSC PET is typically an objective exam consisting of multiple choice questions across General Knowledge, Mental Ability, Numerical Ability, Hindi/English, and subject-specific topics where applicable. The total marks and number of questions will be specified in the notification — past patterns show 100–150 questions with 1 mark each.

Detailed Syllabus — Topic-wise (Indicative)

General Knowledge & Current Affairs

Indian & Uttar Pradesh current affairs, history, geography, polity and governance, economic basics, important national & state schemes, awards, sports and important dates.

Reasoning / Mental Ability

Series, coding-decoding, blood relations, directions, order and ranking, seating arrangements, syllogism, puzzles and logical reasoning questions common to competitive exams.

Numerical Ability / Quantitative Aptitude

Basic arithmetic (number system, LCM/GCD, ratio & proportion, percentages), averages, time & work, speed & distance, simple algebra and data interpretation basics.

Hindi / Language

Comprehension passages, grammar, vocabulary, error spotting and sentence rearrangement focused for Hindi and/or basic English as specified.

Use past year PET papers to get a real sense of question difficulty and frequently asked topics. State GK plays an important role — prepare UP-specific facts thoroughly.

Preparation Plan — 3–5 Month Actionable Roadmap

Months 1–2: Foundation & State GK

  • Finish UP state history, geography and polity basics.
  • Master current affairs (last 12 months) with a focus on UP developments.
  • Start basics of reasoning & quantitative aptitude.

Months 3–4: Practice & Mock Tests

  • Take sectional tests; fix time per section to improve speed.
  • Weekly full-length mock tests and detailed error analysis.
  • Create a one-page quick revision sheet for UP facts & formulae.

Final Month: Revision & Accuracy

  • Daily revision of quick notes and high-frequency current affairs.
  • Simulate exam conditions and avoid last-minute topic changes.
  • Maintain accuracy over attempting all questions — avoid wild guesses if negative marking applies.

Daily Routine Sample

  • 1 hour: Current affairs (UP + National)
  • 1 hour: Reasoning practice
  • 1 hour: Numerical ability drills
  • 30 mins: Hindi comprehension & revision

Consistency, focused revision and mock-test feedback loop are the fastest ways to clear PET. Keep a mistake log and revise it weekly.

Frequently Asked Questions — UPSSSC PET 2025

What is UPSSSC PET?
UPSSSC PET is a preliminary eligibility test conducted to create an eligibility pool for various subordinate services recruitments in Uttar Pradesh. Qualifying PET increases chances to appear in multiple post-specific recruitments conducted later.
How many questions are in PET?
The number varies by cycle; typically 100–150 objective questions. Check the official notification for 2025 specifics.
Is there negative marking?
Negative marking rules vary by notification. Many past PET cycles had no negative marking, but always confirm in the official document.
Can PET score be used for multiple recruitments?
Yes — PET qualifiers are often eligible for multiple UPSSSC recruitments during the PET validity period, subject to individual recruitment rules.

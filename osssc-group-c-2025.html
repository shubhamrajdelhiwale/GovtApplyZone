Vacancy 2025 — Expected & Common Posts

OSSSC Group C typically recruits for a wide range of technical and clerical posts across departments. The total vacancies vary year-to-year — expect a few thousands in a full-scale recruitment depending on government approvals.

Common Group C Posts

  • Statistical Assistant / Field Surveyor
  • Amin / Revenue Inspector (Junior)
  • Junior Assistant / Clerk
  • Multi-Tasking Staff / Field Attendants
  • Junior Technician (Electrical/Mechanical)
  • Foreman / Sub-Technical Staff
  • Store Keeper / Junior Stenographer

Post-wise educational eligibility ranges from Matric/10th pass to Diploma/Graduate and specific trade certificates for technical positions. Check the vacancy table in the official PDF for exact breakdowns and category-wise reservations.

Eligibility — Educational Qualifications, Age & Domicile

Educational Qualification

Qualifications vary widely by post. Typical minimums:

  • Matric / 10th pass — for clerical/junior roles and MTS
  • 12th / Intermediate — for some junior technical / clerical posts
  • Diploma / ITI / Certificate — for trade-specific technical positions
  • Graduate (B.A./B.Sc./B.Com) — for statistical assistants, junior officers and some clerical positions

Age Limit & Relaxation

Age criteria depend on the post — typical ranges are 18–32 years for many Group C posts. Relaxations are available as per Odisha government rules for OBC, SC/ST, Ex-Servicemen and PWD categories.

Domicile Requirement

Most posts require Odisha domicile. Check the notification for specific domicile/district reservation rules.

Exact post-wise eligibility and equivalence of qualifications (e.g., vocational certificates) are specified in the notification — confirm before applying.

Exam Pattern — Prelims & Mains / Trade Tests

OSSSC Group C selection commonly begins with an objective-type Preliminary (screening) exam for many posts. Candidates who clear prelims are called for skill tests, trade tests, mains (where applicable) and document verification.

Prelims Syllabus — Topics to Cover

General Knowledge & Current Affairs

  • Important National & International events
  • Odisha specific events, government schemes, development projects
  • Basic environmental, ecological facts and disaster management

Arithmetic & Numerical Ability

  • Number systems, percentages, ratio & proportion
  • Profit & loss, simple & compound interest
  • Time, speed & distance, averages, basic algebra

Reasoning & Mental Ability

  • Analytical reasoning, series, analogy
  • Puzzles and seating arrangements (basic)
  • Data interpretation basics

Odisha Specific Knowledge

  • State geography & economy
  • Cultural heritage, festivals, tribal areas
  • Administrative divisions and major schemes

Prelims is often qualifying for many posts — aim for accuracy and speed. Regular current affairs with Odisha focus will give an edge.

Mains / Trade Test Syllabus — Post-wise Notes

Clerical / Junior Assistant Posts

  • Advanced arithmetic & data handling
  • Office procedures, basic accounting
  • Odisha administrative structure & public policies

Statistical / Field Survey Posts

  • Basic statistics, sampling methods, data tabulation
  • Field survey methods & practical recording
  • Use of simple statistical tools and interpretation

Technical & Trade Posts (ITI / Diploma required)

  • Post-specific trade skills and practical tests
  • Safety, equipment handling and standard operating procedures
  • Basic troubleshooting & maintenance tasks under timed conditions

Amin / Revenue Posts

  • Revenue laws & land record basics
  • Measurement & survey calculations
  • Practice with field forms and record-keeping

Mains / trade tests differ per post — use official syllabus for exact topics and sample practical tasks. Hands-on practice for trade tests is essential.

Preparation Strategy — 6–9 Month Plan

OSSSC Group C demands a combined approach: brush up basics (GK & arithmetic), practice Odisha-specific content, and prepare thoroughly for trade/practical tests where applicable. Below is a recommended roadmap.

Months 1–2: Basics

  • Daily current affairs with Odisha focus
  • Arithmetic basics — speed & accuracy drills
  • Start reading Odisha state reports & GK notes

Months 3–5: Practice & Mock

  • Full-length prelim mock tests weekly
  • Target weak areas from mock analysis
  • Begin trade/test-specific practice (if post needs)

Months 6–7: Revise & Strengthen

  • Revision notes & quick facts for Odisha
  • Speed practice for arithmetic & reasoning
  • Mock trade tests and time-bound practical trials

Final 30 Days

  • Revise high-yield GK & math formulas
  • Light mock tests and sleep & nutrition management
  • Document readiness for application & DV

If appearing for a trade post, allocate at least 30–60 minutes daily to hands-on practice or supervised training in your trade skill during the final months.

Recommended Books & Online Resources

  • General Knowledge: Lucent's General Knowledge; monthly current affairs magazines
  • Arithmetic: R.S. Aggarwal (selective chapters), Fast track objective arithmetic
  • Odisha GK & History: State government portals, Odisha Year Book, Odisha district profiles
  • Reasoning: Verbal & non-verbal reasoning books (objective practice)
  • Trade Preparation: Trade-specific manuals, ITI curriculum, and supervised lab practice
  • Official OSSSC notifications, past papers and sample skill tests (best source for trade tasks)

Use OSSSC past papers to identify pattern and frequently asked topics. For trade tests, prefer practical coaching / vocational labs where possible.

Practical Tips — Trade Tests, Documents & Fieldwork

Practical/trade tests often decide selection for technical posts. Organize short supervised practice sessions, and ensure you have all certificates and work attestations ready for document verification.

  • Carry original and self-attested copies of educational & trade certificates
  • Practice common practical tasks under timed conditions
  • For survey/field posts, practice sample collection, measurement and tabulation
  • Prepare a checklist for DV: ID, domicile, caste certificate (if applicable), medical fitness certificate

Frequently Asked Questions (OSSSC Group C 2025)

What are the eligibility criteria for OSSSC Group C posts?
Eligibility varies by post — many posts require Matric/Intermediate/Graduate or trade certificates (ITI/Diploma). Age limits and Odisha domicile requirements are specified in the notification for each post.
Is there negative marking in OSSSC prelims?
Negative marking depends on the specific exam cycle and will be mentioned in the official notification. Historically, some OSSSC objective tests carried negative marking for incorrect answers.
How important are trade tests?
For technical/trade posts (ITI/Diploma), the practical/trade test is often critical and may be qualifying or scored. Hands-on practice is essential for these roles.
Where do I download the official OSSSC notification?
Official notifications are posted on the OSSSC website: osssc.gov.in. Always verify dates and application details from the official PDF.

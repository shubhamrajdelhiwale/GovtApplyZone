Revised NVS syllabus for 2025 expected with subject competence + teaching aptitude.

Recruiting Body

Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS), Ministry of Education.

Posts

PGT, TGT, Music Teacher, Art Teacher, PET, Librarian.

Selection Mode

Computer-based test + Interview (for PGT) + Document Verification.

Eligibility Criteria

  • PGT: Master’s Degree + B.Ed + Relevant Subject.
  • TGT: Graduation + B.Ed + CTET (Paper 2).
  • Music/Art/PET/Librarian: As per NVS norms.
  • Age Limit: TGT ≤ 35 years, PGT ≤ 40 years (relaxation as per rules).

Vacancy Details

Category-wise, subject-wise vacancies will be updated after official notification. NVS generally announces 1500–2500+ teacher posts across India.

Syllabus (PGT / TGT)

  • General Awareness
  • General English & Hindi
  • Reasoning & Mental Ability
  • Teaching Aptitude / Pedagogy
  • Subject Knowledge (PGT/TGT relevant subject)

How to Apply

  1. Visit NVS recruitment portal.
  2. Register using mobile number & email.
  3. Fill the online application form.
  4. Upload documents (photo, signature, qualification).
  5. Pay application fee & submit form.

Important Dates

  • Notification: Feb 2025 (expected)
  • Apply Online: Feb–Mar 2025
  • Exam Date: Apr–Jun 2025 (tentative)

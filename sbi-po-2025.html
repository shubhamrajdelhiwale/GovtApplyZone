Post

Probationary Officer (PO) — officer cadre with training followed by posting in branches/administration.

Selection Flow

Preliminary Exam → Mains (online objective + descriptive) → Interview / Group Exercise (as per notification) → Final merit list.

Eligibility & Qualification

  • Nationality: Citizen of India / subject of Nepal / Bhutan / person of Indian origin (as specified by SBI).
  • Age: Age limit and important cut-off dates are specified in the notification (typical PO range around early 20s to early 30s with relaxations for reserved categories).
  • Educational Qualification: Graduate in any discipline from a recognized university (check notification for exact qualifying degree and date-of-qualification rules).
  • Other: Candidates should meet SBI's fitness/character requirements and not have disqualifying records. Local language requirements may apply for some postings.

Vacancy & Salary

SBI publishes official vacancy numbers in the notification. Vacancies may be broken down by state/zone and category. SBI may issue revised vacancy lists later in the recruitment cycle.

Salary & Emoluments

SBI PO pay is governed by SBI’s officer pay-scales and allowances. The in-hand salary during probation depends on the posted location and applicable allowances (basic pay + DA + HRA + other allowances). For exact pay structure refer to SBI’s official recruitment notification or offer letter.

Exam Pattern (Overview)

Preliminary Exam

  • Objective online test — sections typically: English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning Ability.
  • Duration and sectional time-limits are specified in the notification (commonly 60 minutes total).
  • Prelims is a screening exam used to shortlist candidates for Mains.

Main Exam

  • Consists of objective tests covering Reasoning & Computer Aptitude, Data Analysis & Interpretation, General/Economy/Banking Awareness, English Language, and an accompanying descriptive paper (English) to assess writing skills.
  • Negative marking for wrong answers usually applies (see notification for exact scheme).

Interview / Group Exercise

Shortlisted candidates from Mains are called for interview and/or group exercise (per SBI rules). Final selection is based on combined scores (weights per notification).

Syllabus Highlights

  • English: Reading comprehension, cloze tests, error detection, para jumbles and essay/letter for descriptive.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Arithmetic, algebra, data interpretation, simplification, ratio & proportion, time & work.
  • Reasoning: Puzzles, seating arrangements, syllogisms, inequalities, input-output, logical reasoning.
  • General/Banking Awareness: Current affairs with emphasis on banking, economy, RBI circulars, budget updates and financial terms.
  • Computer Aptitude: Basic computer fundamentals and practical knowledge of MS Office and internet usage.

Selection Process

  1. Apply & Pay Fee: Complete online application and fee payment within the window announced.
  2. Prelims: Objective screening test to shortlist candidates for Mains.
  3. Mains: Objective + descriptive tests; marks used to shortlist for interview/group exercise.
  4. Interview/Group Exercise: Personality assessment and/or GD for shortlisted candidates.
  5. Final Selection: Based on combined performance (weights for Mains and interview as specified in the notification) and available vacancies.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the SBI Careers website and open the SBI PO recruitment notification for 2025.
  2. Register with a valid email and mobile number to receive login credentials.
  3. Complete the online application form — personal details, educational qualifications and other required information.
  4. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required certificates in the specified format and size.
  5. Pay the application fee online as per category and submit the form. Save the registration details and take a printout of the confirmation page.

8-Week Preparation Plan (Concise)

  1. Weeks 1–2: Build fundamentals — arithmetic, grammar, reasoning basics; begin daily current affairs focused on banking & economy.
  2. Weeks 3–4: Sectional practice, DI sets & puzzles; start previous-year SBI papers & timed drills.
  3. Weeks 5–6: Focus on Mains-level problems, descriptive writing practice (essay & letter), and area-specific revision.
  4. Weeks 7–8: Full-length mocks, revision of weak areas, interview/GD preparation (if shortlisted) and final document check.

Top Books & Free Resources

  • Quantitative Aptitude — R.S. Aggarwal / Arun Sharma for topic-wise practice.
  • Reasoning — R.S. Aggarwal / M.K. Pandey for puzzles & seating arrangements.
  • English — Wren & Martin, Word Power Made Easy and previous SBI PO descriptive answers.
  • Banking Awareness — Monthly compilations, RBI circulars, budget & financial news summaries.
  • Mocks — SBI/official sample papers and reputed online test series for realistic timed practice.

Mini Practice Set

  1. Quant: A boat covers a distance upstream in 6 hours and returns in 4 hours. Find the ratio of speed of boat in still water to speed of current.
  2. Reasoning: In a certain code, 'APPLE' is written as 'QKKFV'. Decode the pattern and write 'MANGO'.
  3. English: Write a 150-word essay on the importance of digital banking (practice for descriptive section).

Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Prioritize accuracy — negative marking penalizes wrong answers; avoid wild guessing.
  • Time management on DI and reasoning sections is critical; develop shortcuts and mental math skills.
  • Practice descriptive writing under timed conditions — clarity & structure matter.
  • Ensure scanned documents match the required specifications before uploading to prevent rejection.

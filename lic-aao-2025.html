Official Notification

LIC releases the AAO recruitment notification on LIC's careers portal with post-wise vacancies, eligibility and application instructions.

Registration Window (Tentative)

Online registration typically opens after notification; candidates must apply and pay the fee within the specified window.

Vacancy & Reservation

LIC publishes vacancy counts and reservation details in the notification — vacancies may be state-wise or zonal and can be revised.

Selection Stages

Common flow: Online Preliminary Exam → Online Main Exam (objective + descriptive where applicable) → Interview (for some streams) → Final selection.

Recruiting Body

Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) — recruitment for Assistant Administrative Officers (AAO).

Post Profile

Assistant Administrative Officer — managerial entry-level role involving underwriting, claims, accounts, IT, actuarial support, and branch/office administration depending on stream.

Selection Flow

Prelims → Mains → Interview (where applicable) → Document verification & appointment.

Eligibility & Qualification

  • Nationality: Citizen of India / subject of Nepal/Bhutan / Tibetan refugee / person of Indian origin (as per LIC rules).
  • Age: Age limits and cut-off dates are specified in the notification — relaxations for reserved categories apply. Check exact dates in the official PDF.
  • Educational Qualification: Graduate degree in any discipline from a recognized university for general posts. Specialized streams (Actuarial, IT, Finance, Legal, etc.) require specified qualifications — refer to stream-wise criteria in the notification.
  • Experience: Some specialist roles may require experience; many AAO posts are open to freshers with required qualifications.
  • Other: Good character, medical fitness and ability to work at any place of posting as per LIC norms.

Vacancy & Salary

LIC publishes post-wise & zone-wise vacancy numbers in the notification and may release revised vacancy statements during the recruitment cycle. Always use the official PDF for final numbers.

Salary & Emoluments

LIC AAO pay depends on LIC pay-scales and allowances. Typical in-hand pay for an AAO during initial posting includes basic pay + DA + HRA + other allowances (varies by city). LIC officers also receive pension/benefits as applicable. Check the notification for exact pay scale.

Exam Pattern (Quick)

Preliminary Exam

  • Objective (online) test — typical sections: English Language, Reasoning Ability, Quantitative Aptitude.
  • Duration typically 60 minutes (sectional timings may apply) — Prelims is used to shortlist candidates for Mains.
  • Negative marking often applies for incorrect answers — check notification for exact scheme.

Main Exam

  • Objective & Descriptive components depending on stream: Reasoning, Data Analysis, English Language, Insurance & Financial Awareness, Professional Knowledge (for specialized streams like IT/Actuarial/Legal/Finance).
  • Descriptive/essay or subject-specific short answers may be part of Mains for certain streams — verify in notification.
  • Final shortlisting for interview (if applicable) based on Mains scores.

Interview (If applicable)

Some cycles include an interview for final selection — weightage between Mains and Interview is specified in the notification. Document verification follows before appointment.

Syllabus Highlights

  • English: Reading comprehension, para jumbles, error spotting, cloze, précis, descriptive writing (if applicable).
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Arithmetic (Percentages, Ratio & Proportion, Averages), Data Interpretation, Algebra, Time & Work.
  • Reasoning: Puzzles, seating arrangements, syllogism, inequalities, input-output, blood relations.
  • Insurance & Financial Awareness: Basics of life insurance, products, underwriting, claims, bancassurance, financial markets, RBI & IRDA basics.
  • Professional / Stream-specific: IT fundamentals for IT stream, actuarial basics for Actuarial stream, accounting/finance for Finance stream, law topics for Legal stream.

Selection Process

  1. Apply Online: Complete application & fee payment within the window announced in notification.
  2. Prelims: Online objective test for shortlisting to Mains.
  3. Mains: Objective & descriptive/stream-specific tests. Scores used to shortlist for Interview (if applicable).
  4. Interview: Personal interview for shortlisted candidates (where prescribed).
  5. Final Merit & Appointment: Based on combined scores (weights as per notification), document verification and medical fitness.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit LIC's official recruitment/careers portal and open the AAO recruitment notification page.
  2. Read the notification carefully for post-wise eligibility, important dates and document requirements.
  3. Register with a valid email and mobile number to obtain login credentials.
  4. Fill the online application form with accurate personal, educational and stream preference details.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required certificates in the prescribed format and size.
  6. Pay application fee online (as applicable) and submit. Save/print the application confirmation & registration number.

Important Dates (Tentative)

  • Notification Release: 2025 (check LIC Careers for the official PDF)
  • Online Registration Start: As specified in notification
  • Last Date to Apply: Typically within 2–3 weeks of registration opening
  • Preliminary Exam: Scheduled a few weeks after application window closes (dates in official timetable)
  • Main Exam: Conducted after Prelims (check admit card for exact dates)
  • Interview: Dates/venues announced for shortlisted candidates (if applicable)

Note: Dates above are indicative. Always confirm exact dates and timelines in the official LIC notification before taking action.

8–10 Week Preparation Plan (Practical)

  1. Weeks 1–2: Strengthen fundamentals—arithmetic basics, reasoning concepts, and reading practice for English; start daily insurance & financial current affairs.
  2. Weeks 3–4: Sectional practice (DI, puzzles, RCs), begin previous-year papers for pattern familiarity.
  3. Weeks 5–7: Mains-level practice—advanced DI, subject-specific study for chosen stream (IT/Finance/Actuarial/Law), descriptive answer writing if applicable.
  4. Weeks 8–10: Full-length mocks, time management drills, interview prep (if applicable) and document readiness checks.

Top Books & Free Resources

  • Quant & Reasoning — R.S. Aggarwal / Arun Sharma for topic-wise practice.
  • English — Wren & Martin, The Hindu/Mint editorials for RC practice and writing clarity.
  • Insurance Awareness — LIC annual reports, IRDAI guidelines, basic insurance textbooks and online explainers on life products & underwriting.
  • Professional Stream Guides — standard texts for IT/Actuarial/Finance/Law as per stream syllabus.
  • Mocks & PYQs — LIC previous papers (if available) and reputable online test series for timed practice.

Mini Practice Set

  1. Quant: If the compound interest on a principal of ₹10,000 for 2 years at annual rate r% is ₹2,102, find r (assume annual compounding).
  2. Reasoning: Seven persons P,Q,R,S,T,U,V sit in a row. Q is between S and T; R is left of Q but not adjacent to S. (Practice seating/puzzle setup & inference.)
  3. Insurance Awareness: Explain the difference between term insurance and endowment policies and one advantage of each.

Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Read the official notification carefully — stream-wise eligibility and document list matter a lot for specialized posts.
  • Prioritise accuracy — negative marking penalises random guessing in objective tests.
  • Practice descriptive writing (if Mains requires it) with clear structure: introduction, points with examples, conclusion.
  • Keep scanned documents in required format before applying to avoid last-minute rejections.
  • For interview rounds, prepare to discuss any written answers you submitted and your understanding of insurance products & recent industry developments.

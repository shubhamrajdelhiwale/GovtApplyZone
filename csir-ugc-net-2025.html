Exam Mode

Computer-Based Test (CBT) conducted by NTA for eligibility in JRF and Lectureship.

Subjects Offered

Life Sciences, Physical Sciences, Mathematical Sciences, Chemical Sciences, Earth Sciences.

Authority

National Testing Agency (NTA) on behalf of CSIR.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Master’s degree/Integrated BS-MS/BE/B.Tech/BPharma/MBBS or equivalent with required percentage.
  • Age limit for JRF: 28 years (relaxation applicable). No age limit for Lectureship (LS).
  • Subject choice must match the qualifying degree discipline.

Exam Pattern

  • Single paper, 3 hours duration.
  • MCQ based exam with negative marking.
  • Part A (General Aptitude), Part B (Subject Knowledge), Part C (Higher-level research questions).

Syllabus (Subject-wise)

Part A — General Aptitude

  • Quantitative reasoning
  • Graphical analysis
  • Logical and analytical ability

Subject Sections (Part B & C)

  • Chemical Sciences
  • Life Sciences
  • Physical Sciences
  • Earth Sciences
  • Mathematical Sciences

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit CSIR NET official portal (after activation).
  2. Register with email & mobile.
  3. Fill the application form and upload documents.
  4. Pay fees through online modes.
  5. Download confirmation page.

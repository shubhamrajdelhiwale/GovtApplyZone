SSC MTS Admit Card 2025 — How to Download

  1. Visit the official SSC website: ssc.nic.in.
  2. Go to the “Candidate’s Corner” and click “Admit Card” or “SSC MTS Admit Card 2025”.
  3. Enter your registration number / roll number and date of birth and click submit.
  4. Download the SSC MTS admit card PDF and print it for the exam day.

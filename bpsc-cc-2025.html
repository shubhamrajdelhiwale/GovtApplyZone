About BPSC Combined Competitive Exam

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) conducts the Combined Competitive Exam (CCE) to recruit state civil servants for administrative and managerial roles across Bihar. The exam tests candidates on general studies, aptitude, language, and optional subjects relevant to administration.

Why BPSC CCE?

BPSC posts offer social impact, stable government career paths, structured promotions, and the opportunity to influence policy and governance in Bihar. Many aspirants prepare especially for state-specific topics (Bihar history, geography, culture).

Syllabus & Exam Pattern

Prelims (Screening — Objective)

Typically two papers: General Studies Paper I (GS I) and General Studies Paper II (Aptitude/CSAT) — check official notification for the exact structure. Prelims is qualifying for Mains.

  • GS I: History, Polity, Geography, Economy, Environment, Current Affairs (national & Bihar specific)
  • GS II: Aptitude, reasoning, basic numeracy, comprehension

Mains (Descriptive)

Mains usually consists of 4 descriptive papers + optional/subject-specific papers for some posts. Papers demand structured, well-argued answers with facts and examples.

  1. Essay & General Studies
  2. Advanced General Studies / Optional Subject
  3. Language papers (Hindi / English as per notification)
  4. Post-specific papers (where applicable)

Interview / Personality Test

Interview evaluates candidate's mental alertness, clarity, balance of judgment, leadership and overall personality.

Eligibility

  • Nationality: Indian citizen (as per BPSC rules)
  • Educational: Graduate (any discipline) from a recognized university
  • Age Limit: Check official notification — typical range 21–37 with category relaxations
  • Physical/Medical: As required for specific posts like DSP

Vacancy Details (Snapshot)

Notification indicates post-wise vacancies — approximate count: 1000+ (SDM, DSP, Revenue Officer, Block Welfare Officer etc.). For exact numbers, consult the official PDF.

Exam Centres & Important Instructions

BPSC conducts exams at multiple centres across Bihar — major cities include Patna, Gaya, Muzaffarpur, Bhagalpur, Darbhanga, and others. City intimation and centre details are provided on admit cards.

  • Check city intimation carefully (download admit card and verify exam centre)
  • Reach exam centre at least 60 minutes before reporting time
  • Carry required documents and follow COVID / safety protocols if any

Preparation Strategy — 12 Week Plan

A focused 12-week plan to build syllabus coverage, speed, accuracy and answer-writing skills.

Weeks 1–4: Foundation

  • Finish Bihar history & geography basics
  • Daily 1-hour newspaper/current affairs summary
  • Quant & aptitude basics — 30 mins daily

Weeks 5–8: Practice & Mock Tests

  • Sectional mocks (GA, Aptitude)
  • Start answer writing practice for Mains
  • Revise high-weight topics weekly

Weeks 9–12: Revision & Full Mocks

  • Full-length timed mocks every 4 days
  • Polish weak topics, consolidate notes
  • Interview prep: current events discussion & profile work

Answer Writing Tips for Mains

  1. Start with a clear introduction
  2. Use headings/bullets where helpful
  3. Support points with examples and data
  4. Conclude with a balanced view

Bihar-specific GK: High-impact Topics

  • History: Maurya, Gupta, Chanakya era in Bihar; historic sites (Nalanda, Vikramshila)
  • Geography: River systems (Ganga tributaries), climate, plain & flood management
  • Economy: Agriculture profile, major industries, state schemes and development indicators
  • Culture: Festivals, languages/dialects, notable personalities from Bihar

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: When will BPSC CCE 2025 application window open?

A: Check the official notification PDF and the BPSC website. Application windows typically remain open for 2–3 weeks.

Q: What are best books for BPSC CCE?

A: Standard NCERTs for basics, Laxmikanth for Polity, Spectrum/Modern India for History, and state-level resources for Bihar GK. Pair books with daily current affairs and test series.

Q: How to prepare for Mains answer writing?

A: Regular practice with previous Mains questions, timed answer writing, feedback from mentors and maintaining a personal note of model answers works best.

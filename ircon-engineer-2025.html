Recruiting Body

IRCON International Ltd. — a government-owned engineering & construction PSU specializing in transport infrastructure and turnkey projects.

Typical Posts

Executive / Works Engineer (Civil, Electrical, S&T), Site Supervisor, Safety Engineer, Managerial roles in Projects/Contracts & Specialist posts (IT/Finance/HR).

Recruitment Modes

Regular (executive) recruitment, contract / project hires, and walk-in interviews — each mode has its own eligibility and selection steps.

Eligibility & Essential Qualifications

  • Educational Qualification: Engineering degree/diploma in the relevant discipline (Civil/Electrical/Mechanical/S&T) for technical posts; qualifications for specialist posts (Finance/HR/Legal/IT) are listed post-wise in the notification.
  • Experience: Post-specific — some executive posts require 0–2 years (freshers) while many managerial roles ask for post-qualification experience; contract roles typically list minimum experience explicitly.
  • Age Limits: Specified per advertisement with relaxations for reserved categories — check cut-off dates in the PDF.
  • Nationality & Other: Indian citizen (or categories allowed as per the notification); medical fitness & willingness to be posted to project sites are usually required.

Vacancy & Salary

IRCON publishes vacancy counts in the official notification and a separate vacancy PDF — numbers vary by region/project and are authoritative in those documents.

Remuneration

Regular Executive posts use the company's pay-scale (IDA/Executive pay bands) and include allowances; contract hires mention consolidated monthly remuneration. See the notification for exact pay level or consolidated pay for each post.

Selection Process (Common Flows)

  1. Online Application: Apply via IRCON careers portal — fill form, upload documents and pay fee (if applicable).
  2. Screening / Shortlisting: Based on eligibility, educational score & experience; some posts shortlist for written test or interview.
  3. Written Test / CBT / Technical Test: For some executive posts — objective/subjective tests or trade/practical tests.
  4. Interview / Walk-in / Skill Test: Personal interviews, walk-in interviews or skill assessments for contract/project roles.
  5. Document Verification & Medical: Final verification of originals and medical fitness before appointment.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit IRCON's Careers / Vacancy page and open the relevant advertisement PDF.
  2. Read the advertisement fully for post-wise eligibility, experience, age limits and the number of vacancies.
  3. Register / create profile (if required), fill the online form carefully and upload scanned photograph, signature & certificates in prescribed format.
  4. Pay application fee online (if applicable) and submit. Save/print the confirmation and the uploaded application snapshot for future reference.
  5. If called for written/interview, keep original certificates handy for Document Verification & medical checks as instructed.

Preparation Tips & Practical Plan

  • For technical posts, revise core engineering subjects, site practices, estimation & project management basics (Civil/Electrical/S&T as per discipline).
  • Practice previous IRCON question papers (if available) and sample papers for objective tests; prepare a short project/experience summary for interviews.
  • Prepare documents (degree, mark sheets, experience certificates, caste/disability proofs) in the required scanned formats before applying.
  • For contract/walk-in notices, attend early with hard copies and one set of originals for quicker verification.

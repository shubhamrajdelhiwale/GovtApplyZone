Eligibility Criteria — Rajasthan Police Constable

Educational Qualification

Minimum: 10+2 (Higher Secondary) or equivalent from a recognized board. Some posts may accept 10th pass for certain categories — check notification.

Age Limit

Typically 18–23/21–28 years (varies by notification). Relaxation applicable as per government rules for reserved categories — verify exact limits in PDF.

Nationality & Domicile

Must be Indian citizen; domicile of Rajasthan required where specified. Local language proficiency may be required.

PMT (Physical Measurement Test) & PET (Physical Efficiency Test)

Typical PMT Requirements

Male

  • Height: 168 cm (tribal and some categories may have concessions)
  • Chest: 81–86 cm (with expansion)

Female

  • Height: 152–155 cm (varies by category)
  • No chest requirement

Typical PET Standards

  • Male: 1600m/1.6 km run in 7–8 minutes (varies)
  • Female: 800m run in 4–5 minutes (varies)
  • Some recruitments include long jump and high jump for both sexes

Exact PMT/PET standards, concessions and measurement methodology will be in the official notification. Begin fitness training early — running, interval training, plyometrics and core strengthening help.

Written Exam Syllabus — Rajasthan Police Constable

The written exam generally covers General Knowledge, Reasoning, Numerical Ability, and Basic Computer/English where applicable. Some recruitments include state-specific GK (Rajasthan) sections.

Topic-wise breakdown

  • General Knowledge: Indian History, Geography, Polity, Current Affairs (National & State)
  • Rajasthan Specific GK: State history, districts, economy, culture
  • Arithmetic: Number system, percent, ratio, averages, time & work, profit & loss
  • Reasoning: Verbal & non-verbal reasoning, blood relations, series, puzzles
  • Basic Computer Knowledge (if included): MS Office basics, internet)

Use past year papers to understand question pattern and difficulty. Time management is crucial.

Preparation Strategy — 6 Month Plan

Months 1–2: Foundation

  • Cover Rajasthan-specific history & geography
  • Basics of arithmetic & reasoning
  • Daily current affairs reading (state + national)

Months 3–4: Practice Phase

  • Sectional quizzes & timed tests
  • Start full-length mock tests weekly
  • Begin PET training for stamina & jumps

Months 5–6: Revision & Mocks

  • Frequent full mocks & error analysis
  • Quick revision notes for GK & formulas
  • Maintain fitness & recovery for PET

Daily Routine Sample

  • 1 hour: Current affairs & Rajasthan GK
  • 1 hour: Quant & reasoning practice
  • 45 mins: PET training / running
  • 30 mins: Mock revision & notes

Discipline and consistent mocks are key. Maintain a short formula & facts notebook for last-minute revision.

Frequently Asked Questions — Rajasthan Police Constable 2025

What is the minimum qualification for Rajasthan Police Constable?
Minimum usually 10+2 (Higher Secondary). Some notifications may allow 10th pass for certain posts. Always check the notification.
Is PET mandatory?
Yes, PET is mandatory. Candidates must pass PMT first and then qualify PET to appear for the written exam.
How to prepare for Rajasthan GK?
Create a state-focused notes file covering history, geography, districts, culture, government schemes and recent events. Use Rajasthan GK compendiums and past papers.
Where to download official notification?
Official notification will be available on the Rajasthan Police recruitment portal and state's official recruitment website. Download only from authorised sources.

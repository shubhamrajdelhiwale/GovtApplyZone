Regional RRBs publish a combined CEN (Centralised Employment Notice) for Group D posts — PDF contains post-wise vacancies and instructions.

Application windows run regionally — register on the central apply portal or your regional RRB's apply link within the specified dates.

Vacancies are published zone/RRB-wise and may be updated. Download the vacancy PDF from your regional CEN page for exact numbers.

PET / Skill Tests

Selected posts require PET (Physical Efficiency Test) or skill/typing tests after CBT — qualifying nature and standards specified in CEN.

Conducting Body

Regional Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) — recruitment carried out region-wise under the central CEN instructions.

Posts Covered

Group D posts include Track Maintainer, Helper/Assistant, Porter, Assistant Pointsman, Helper-II, Hospital Attendant and other non-technical posts (post list varies by RRB).

Selection Flow

Online CBT (Stage I) → Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and/or Typing/Skill Test (as applicable) → Document Verification → Medical Examination → Final appointment.

Eligibility (Key Points)

  • Nationality: Indian citizen / subject of Nepal or Bhutan / Tibetan refugee / person of Indian origin (per CEN).
  • Age: Minimum & maximum age limits differ by post — check the CEN for exact cut-off dates and relaxations for reserved categories.
  • Educational Qualification: Minimum 10th pass or ITI (post-specific) from a recognized board/institute as on the date specified in the CEN. Some posts specify physical/medical fitness or experience.
  • Local Requirements: Language / domicile requirements may be mentioned for specific regions — read the regional instructions.
  • Debarment & Restrictions: Candidates with debarment orders from previous RRB/RRC recruitment are ineligible — refer to the CEN notes.

Vacancy & Pay

RRB Group D vacancies are published RRB-wise in a vacancy PDF. Totals often run into thousands across all regions — download the official vacancy spreadsheet for exact post-wise and category-wise numbers.

Pay & Allowances

Group D posts follow Indian Railways pay scale for the respective grade (basic pay + DA + HRA + other allowances). Exact pay band and initial in-hand pay varies by post and posting location; check the CEN for pay matrix details.

Exam Pattern (CBT) — Quick

CBT — Stage I (Objective)

  • Objective computer-based test covering: General Science, Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness on current affairs (including railway awareness), and Basic Computer Knowledge where applicable.
  • Questions/Marks & Duration: Varies by CEN — commonly 100 questions / 90–120 minutes (confirm with your regional CEN PDF).
  • Negative marking usually applies for incorrect answers — check CEN for the exact scheme.

PET / Skill Tests

  • PET: Physical Efficiency Test is qualifying in nature — typical events include running (100m, 800m/1000m) and load-carrying standards; standards differ by gender and region (see CEN).
  • Skill/Typing Test: For posts that require it (e.g., typist/clerical roles), qualifying speed & accuracy criteria are prescribed in the CEN.

Syllabus Highlights

  • General Science: Basics of physics, chemistry & biology (school-level), practical day-to-day science questions.
  • Mathematics: Number systems, percentages, ratios, averages, time & work, time & distance, mensuration and basic arithmetic.
  • General Intelligence & Reasoning: Analogy, classification, series, coding-decoding, puzzles, seating arrangement, blood relations, directions.
  • General Awareness: Current affairs (national & state), Indian polity, history, geography, basic economics, and railway-related facts/terminology.
  • Computer Basics (if applicable): Fundamentals of computers, MS Office basics, internet & email concepts.

Selection Process

  1. Online Application: Register and apply on the central portal / regional RRB apply link; upload documents & pay fee as prescribed.
  2. CBT (Stage I): Computer-based screening test — shortlist to next stage based on cut-offs and reservation norms.
  3. PET / Skill Test: Qualifying physical tests or skill/typing tests for relevant posts — marks may be qualifying only.
  4. Document Verification: Verification of original certificates/ID for shortlisted candidates.
  5. Medical Examination: Final medical fitness test as per Indian Railways standards before appointment.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official RRB apply portal or your regional RRB's recruitment page and locate the Group D CEN/notification.
  2. Read the notification fully — note post-wise eligibility, PET/skill requirements, fees and document specifications.
  3. Register with a valid email & mobile number to receive login credentials.
  4. Fill in the online application: personal, educational, post & region preferences; upload scanned photograph & signature as per guidelines.
  5. Pay the application fee online (if applicable) and submit. Save/print the registration confirmation and application summary.
  6. Keep original documents and scanned copies ready for Document Verification stage.

Important Dates (Typical / Tentative)

  • Notification / CEN Release: 2025 (check your regional RRB page for the PDF)
  • Online Registration Start: As per CEN (region-wise)
  • Last Date to Apply: Region/CEN dependent — usually 2–3 weeks after opening
  • CBT Dates: Announced region-wise in admit-cards (typically within 1–2 months of application close)
  • PET / Skill Test: Dates published for shortlisted candidates after CBT
  • Document Verification & Medical: Following PET/skill stages — dates shared by regional RRBs

Note: Dates and process details may vary by region — always confirm with your regional RRB CEN and official notifications before applying.

8–10 Week Preparation Plan (Practical)

  1. Weeks 1–2: Build arithmetic basics (speed & accuracy), reasoning fundamentals and daily current affairs reading.
  2. Weeks 3–5: Sectional practice — mock tests for General Science, Reasoning & Arithmetic; practice previous-year RRB Group D papers.
  3. Weeks 6–8: Full-length timed mocks, improve speed on calculation-heavy topics and revise GK notes (railway facts & current affairs).
  4. Weeks 9–10: PET readiness (if applicable): start light endurance & sprint training depending on PET criteria; final revision and document readiness.

Recommended Books & Free Resources

  • Quant & Arithmetic — R.S. Aggarwal / Arun Sharma (select topics relevant to RRB level).
  • Reasoning — R.S. Aggarwal / M.K. Pandey for puzzles & seating practice.
  • General Science & GK — NCERT basics (class 6–10) and monthly current affairs compilations.
  • Railway Awareness — official CEN glossary/terminology, RRB FAQs and past CEN notes.
  • Mocks & PYQs — regional RRB previous papers and reputable online test series for timed practice.

Mini Practice Set

  1. Mathematics: If the ratio of A:B is 3:4 and A+B = 140, find A.
  2. General Science: Which gas is primarily responsible for the greenhouse effect?
  3. Reasoning: In a series 2, 6, 18, 54,... what is the 6th term?

Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Read the CEN carefully — PET/skill test criteria, post-wise educational requirements and regional notes differ and are decisive.
  • Negative marking reduces benefit of blind guessing — focus on accuracy in CBT.
  • Prepare PET safely — follow gradual training and consult a doctor if you have health concerns before attempting rigorous physical tests.
  • Keep documents (DOB, education, SC/ST/OBC certificate if applicable, and ID) scanned as per format before starting application.
  • Note your RRB region carefully — non-transferable region preferences may affect final appointment and DV handling.

Eligibility & Qualification

  • Nationality: Indian citizen / subject of Nepal or Bhutan / Tibetan refugee / person of Indian origin (as per CEN).
  • Age: Age limits & relaxations are specified in the CEN — check the exact upper/lower age and cut-off date.
  • Educational Qualification:
    • JE (Civil): Diploma or Degree in Civil Engineering (as per post requirement).
    • JE (Electrical): Diploma or Degree in Electrical Engineering / Electrical & Electronics.
    • JE (Mechanical): Diploma or Degree in Mechanical Engineering or related disciplines.
    • Some posts accept ITI with relevant trade/discipline — consult the CEN for post-specific eligibility.
  • Experience: Freshers are usually eligible; certain posts may specify experience — check stream-wise notes in the CEN.
  • Local/Language Requirements: Local language proficiency or domicile instructions may apply region-wise — read the regional CEN carefully.

Vacancy & Pay

RRB JE vacancy lists (RRB-wise & post-wise) are provided in the CEN vacancy PDF. Totals may be revised — always use the official vacancy spreadsheet for precise numbers.

Pay & Allowances

JE pay follows Indian Railways pay scales for the respective technical grade (basic pay + DA + HRA + allowances). Exact level and in-hand salary depend on pay matrix and posting location — check CEN / offer letters for exact emoluments.

Exam Pattern (Summary)

CBT-1 (Screening)

  • Objective online test covering: General Awareness, Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, Basic Science & Engineering basics (depending on CEN).
  • Purpose: Shortlisting for CBT-2 — sectional & overall cut-offs may apply.
  • Negative marking usually applicable — confirm marking scheme in the CEN.

CBT-2 (Mains / Trade-Specific)

  • More technical: includes discipline-specific questions (Civil / Electrical / Mechanical) plus aptitude & engineering drawing/subject knowledge where applicable.
  • CBT-2 scores used for final shortlisting to Document Verification and Medical Examination.

Syllabus Highlights (By Discipline)

  • General Awareness: Current affairs, Indian polity, basic economics, railway-specific facts & recent developments.
  • Mathematics: Number systems, percentages, averages, ratios, algebra, geometry, mensuration, time & work, time & distance.
  • General Intelligence & Reasoning: Puzzles, seating arrangements, syllogisms, coding-decoding, blood relations, directions.
  • Civil Engineering (JE-Civil): Strength of materials, surveying, building construction, concrete technology, RCC/steel design basics, hydrology, soil mechanics, estimation & costing, structural analysis.
  • Electrical Engineering (JE-Electrical): Circuits, AC/DC machines, power systems, transformers, generation & distribution, measurements, control systems, power electronics, electrical materials.
  • Mechanical Engineering (JE-Mechanical): Thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, manufacturing processes, machine design basics, strength of materials, IC engines, refrigeration & air-conditioning, workshop practice.

Selection Process

  1. Online Apply: Complete application & pay fee in the announced window; upload documents per specifications.
  2. CBT-1: Objective screening exam to shortlist candidates for CBT-2.
  3. CBT-2: Discipline-specific objective test — merit used to call for Document Verification.
  4. Document Verification (DV): Original certificates verification for shortlisted candidates.
  5. Medical Examination: Final medical fitness test as per Indian Railways standards before appointment.
  6. Final Allotment: Based on merit, category reservations, and vacancy — appointment letters issued by respective RRB.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official RRB apply portal (link in the CEN) or your regional RRB's recruitment page.
  2. Read the CEN PDF carefully for post-wise eligibility, exam pattern, important dates and document specifications.
  3. Register with a valid email & mobile number to receive login credentials.
  4. Fill the online application with accurate personal, educational and discipline details; choose preferred RRB/region.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, degree/diploma certificate and other required documents in specified format and size.
  6. Pay the application fee online (if applicable) and submit. Save/print the confirmation & application summary for future reference.

  • Online Registration Start: As per CEN (region-wise)

10–12 Week Preparation Plan (Practical)

  1. Weeks 1–2: Strengthen fundamentals — arithmetic, reasoning, basics of engineering discipline & daily GK.
  2. Weeks 3–6: Discipline-specific topic coverage (Civil / Electrical / Mechanical) and practice of previous-year JE questions.
  3. Weeks 7–9: Timed mocks (CBT-1 pattern) and deep technical revision for CBT-2; practice drawing/diagram-based questions if required.
  4. Weeks 10–12: Full-length CBT-2 mocks, error analysis, revision of weak topics & document readiness for DV.

Recommended Books & Free Resources

  • Discipline textbooks — standard diploma/degree level books for Civil/Electrical/Mechanical engineering.
  • General Aptitude & Reasoning — R.S. Aggarwal / Arun Sharma for practice and shortcuts.
  • Mathematics — Hall & Knight basics, practice arithmetic & algebra topic-wise.
  • RRB JE Previous Year Papers & Official CBT Samples — crucial for pattern familiarity.
  • Online Lectures & Reputed Test Series — timed mocks to build speed & accuracy for CBT environment.

Mini Practice Set

  1. Mathematics: If 5 workers can finish a job in 12 days, how many days will 8 workers take (same efficiency)?
  2. Civil (Concept): What is the purpose of a shear wall in building structures?
  3. Electrical (Concept): Define RMS value and its importance for AC circuits.
  4. Mechanical (Concept): Explain the difference between a centrifugal pump and a reciprocating pump.

Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Carefully match your qualification to the post code — applying under a mismatched discipline can lead to rejection.
  • Practice discipline-specific numerical problems — conceptual clarity reduces time per question.
  • Keep original certificates & scanned copies ready in required dimensions/formats before applying.
  • Use official previous-year papers to understand question framing & frequently asked topics.
  • Monitor your regional RRB site for corrigenda, vacancy revisions and admit-card announcements.

TSPSC Group 1 Syllabus 2025

Prelims — General Studies & Mental Ability

  • Indian History & Telangana Movement
  • Economy & Development
  • Geography & Environment
  • Science & Technology
  • Current Affairs & State-specific issues

Prelims is objective (screening). Prepare NCERT, state reports and current affairs.

Mains — Descriptive Papers

  1. Paper 1 – Essay (English/Telugu/Urdu)
  2. Paper 2 – History, Culture & Geography of Telangana
  3. Paper 3 – Indian Society, Constitution & Governance
  4. Paper 4 – Economy & Development (state & national)
  5. Paper 5 – Science, Tech & Data Interpretation
  6. Paper 6 – Telangana History & Movement (detailed)

Mains requires structured answers & time management. Practice previous answer scripts.

Exam Pattern & Marks (Summary)

Prelims

  • Objective type — single paper
  • 200–300 questions (varies by notification)
  • Negative marking applicable

Mains

  • Descriptive papers — 6 papers
  • Each paper: essay/long answers (100–200 marks)
  • Marks aggregated for final merit

Eligibility

  • Indian citizen (state domicile rules may apply)
  • Graduate degree from a recognized university
  • Age & post-specific qualifications in notification

Always cross-check eligibility details in the official notification.

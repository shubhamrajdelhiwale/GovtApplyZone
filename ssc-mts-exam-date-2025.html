All About SSC MTS

The SSC MTS (Multi Tasking Staff) exam is a national-level recruitment test conducted by the Staff Selection Commission to hire Group C staff for various Ministries, Departments, Offices, and Field units across India. If you are searching for ssc mts exam date or mts exam date 2025, this page provides the latest schedule, admit card instructions, syllabus and preparation tips.

Key highlights: SSC MTS tests elementary arithmetic, reasoning, general awareness and basic language skills. The exam is conducted in multiple shifts and the dates are released officially in the PDF notification. Bookmark this page for the most recent ssc mts exam date 2025 and quick links to the ssc login portal and admit card downloads.

Candidates must check the official notification PDF for application window dates, age limits, educational requirements and post-wise vacancies. For step-by-step guidance on how to download the admit card, check the admit card section below.

Exam Schedule

Official notification for SSC MTS 2025 published on Nov 13, 2025. Key exam dates: application window, admit card release, city intimation and exam days. Use ssc login to check application status and download admit cards.

Syllabus & Pattern

MTS Tier I: Objective CBT — General English, Numerical Aptitude, Reasoning, General Awareness. Paper-II (where applicable) may be descriptive/short tasks. Total marks and sectional weightage vary per notification.

Apply & Login

Use the SSC login portal to register, apply and download admit cards. Keep your registration ID and password ready. If you forget password, use the reset link on SSC official site.

Vacancies

Vacancy breakdown, post-wise eligibility and category-wise seats are published with the notification. Check carefully for age relaxations and post-specific educational criteria.

Selection Process

Typically: Computer Based Test (CBT) → Descriptive / Skill Test (if applicable) → Document Verification → Final Merit. Prepare for speed, accuracy and current affairs for GA.

Success Tips

Start with basics, practice previous papers, take sectional mocks, maintain a mistake log, and focus on accuracy. For GA, read monthly current affairs and NCERT fundamentals.

Preparation Strategy — SSC MTS 2025

Tier 1: Tips & Study Plan

Focus on speed, accuracy and time management

  • Daily practice: 60–90 minutes — English & Numerical basics
  • Weekly full mocks to build stamina and section timing
  • Review GA from last 12 months — focus on static GK + important dates (like 13 November 2025 news items)
  • Keep a formula sheet for quick revision before exam

Syllabus Focus Areas

What to prioritise for high score

  • General English — synonyms, antonyms, spotting errors
  • Numerical Aptitude — percentages, ratio, time-speed, basic algebra
  • Reasoning — puzzles, seating arrangement, series
  • General Awareness — current affairs, static GK, national news

Mock Tests & Revision

How to schedule mocks

  1. Month 1: Basics + short topic tests
  2. Month 2: Sectional timed tests + 2 full-length mocks weekly
  3. Month 3: Revision, accuracy drills, and last-minute strategy

Admit Card / SSC Login Steps

Download steps

  • Visit the SSC official website → Candidate Login
  • Enter registration ID and password → Go to ‘Admit Card’ section
  • Download PDF and print multiple copies

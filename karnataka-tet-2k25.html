Karnataka TET 2025

Karnataka Teacher Eligibility Test (KARTET) 2025 is conducted by the School Education Department of Karnataka for recruitment of **Primary Teachers (Classes 1–5)** and **Upper Primary Teachers (Classes 6–8)**. The exam includes **Paper 1** and **Paper 2**, covering Child Development, Language I & II, EVS, Maths & Science, and Social Studies.

Eligibility

  • Indian citizen
  • Minimum 50% in PUC + D.Ed/B.El.Ed for Paper 1
  • Graduate + B.Ed for Paper 2
  • Age: As per state rules

Exam Pattern

  • Paper 1 → Classes 1–5
  • Paper 2 → Classes 6–8
  • MCQ format (150 questions)
  • No negative marking

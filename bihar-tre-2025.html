About Bihar TRE 2025

Bihar TRE 2025 is conducted by the Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) for recruitment of permanent teachers across government schools. It covers four categories:

  • Primary Teacher (Class 1–5)
  • Middle School Teacher (Class 6–8)
  • Secondary Teacher (Class 9–10)
  • Higher Secondary Teacher (Class 11–12)

TRE includes written exam + document verification. No interview.

Exam Pattern & Syllabus

Primary (1–5)

  • Child Development & Pedagogy
  • Language I (Hindi/Urdu)
  • Language II (English)
  • Mathematics
  • Environmental Studies

Middle (6–8)

  • Child Development
  • Language I & II
  • Math/Science / Social Science
  • Pedagogy & Teaching Methods

Secondary (9–10)

  • Subject Knowledge (Math, Science, SST, English, Hindi)
  • Pedagogy + Reasoning
  • General Awareness

Higher Secondary (11–12)

  • Subject Expertise (Arts/Science/Commerce/Vocational)
  • Teaching Aptitude
  • General Studies

Eligibility Criteria

  • Indian Citizen
  • B.Ed or D.El.Ed depending on category
  • TET/CTET/Bihar STET required
  • Age 21–37 years (relaxation applicable)

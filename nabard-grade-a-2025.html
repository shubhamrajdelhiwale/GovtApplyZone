Eligibility & Qualification

  • Nationality: Indian citizen / subject of Nepal/Bhutan / Tibetan refugee / person of Indian origin (as per NABARD rules).
  • Age: Age limits & relaxations are specified in the notification (dates and upper/lower limits vary by cycle).
  • Educational Qualification: Graduate / Postgraduate / professional qualification depending on stream (e.g., CA/ICWA/LLB/Economics/Statistics) — check stream-wise criteria in the official PDF.
  • Experience: Some specialized streams may require post-qualification experience — see the notification.
  • Other: Good character, required documents and fitness as per NABARD guidelines.

Vacancy & Salary

NABARD publishes stream-wise vacancy numbers in the notification along with category-wise reservation. Vacancy totals may be revised — always refer to the official vacancy PDF for exact figures.

Salary & Perks

Assistant Manager (Grade A) pay scale is as per NABARD's pay structure (basic pay + DA + HRA + other allowances). In-hand remuneration depends on posting city and allowances. Confirm precise pay band in the official notification or offer letter.

Exam Pattern (Summary)

Preliminary Exam

  • Online objective test — common sections often include: Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, English Language, General Awareness (with emphasis on agriculture & rural development).
  • Time & sectional structure vary by cycle — check official notification.
  • Prelims is usually qualifying/screening to Mains.

Main Exam

  • Stream-specific papers: General English, Economic & Social Issues / Agriculture & Rural Development, Finance/Accounting (for Finance stream), Law (for Legal stream), etc.
  • Descriptive/essay-type questions may be included for certain papers; evaluate per notification.
  • Interview: Shortlisted candidates (where applicable) appear for a personal interview; weightage details in notification.

Syllabus Highlights

  • General Awareness: Agriculture & rural development issues, government schemes, NABARD/RBI publications, current affairs related to rural economy.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Arithmetic, Data Interpretation, Simplification, Time & Work, Ratio & Proportion.
  • Reasoning: Puzzles, seating arrangements, syllogisms, coding-decoding.
  • Stream-specific: Finance & Accounts — accounting standards, banking finance; Law — constitutional & banking law; Economics — macro & micro topics relevant to rural development.
  • English: Comprehension, précis, essay, grammar and vocabulary (for descriptive where applicable).

Selection Process

  1. Online Registration & Fee: Apply within the published window; upload documents as specified.
  2. Prelims: Online objective screening exam.
  3. Mains: Stream-specific objective/descriptive papers; marks used for shortlisting to interview (if applicable).
  4. Interview: For streams requiring it — personality assessment & subject knowledge.
  5. Final Merit & Appointment: Based on combined performance and vacancies; document verification before joining.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit NABARD's official recruitment/careers portal and open the Grade A notification page.
  2. Read the notification carefully for stream-wise eligibility, exam pattern and documents required.
  3. Register using a valid email & mobile number to get login credentials.
  4. Fill in the online application: personal, educational, stream & preference details; upload scanned photograph & signature.
  5. Pay application fee online (if applicable) and submit. Save the registration number and print confirmation for records.
  6. Keep scanned copies of degrees, category certificate, experience certificate (if applicable) & ID ready for verification.

10-Week Preparation Plan (Practical)

  1. Weeks 1–2: Basics — reasoning, arithmetic fundamentals, read daily agriculture & rural economy current affairs.
  2. Weeks 3–5: Sectional practice, DI & puzzles; start stream-specific study (finance/accounting, law, economics as applicable).
  3. Weeks 6–8: Mains-level practice, descriptive writing (if required), mock tests & previous-year papers.
  4. Weeks 9–10: Full-length mocks, revision, interview prep & documents ready for verification.

Top Books & Free Resources

  • General Aptitude — R.S. Aggarwal / Arun Sharma (topicwise practice).
  • Agriculture & Rural Development — NABARD publications, Ministry of Agriculture releases, basic textbooks on agricultural economics.
  • Finance & Accounts — accounting textbooks, practice with finance MCQs and bank finance topics.
  • Law — basic constitutional provisions, banking & financial laws for Legal stream.
  • Mocks & PYQs — NABARD previous-year papers and reputable online test series for timed practice.

Sample Mini Practice Set

  1. Quant: A train covers 360 km in 4 hours. What is its average speed in km/hr?
  2. General Awareness: Name two flagship schemes of the Government of India for rural development and briefly state their objectives.
  3. Stream-specific (Finance): Explain the difference between NPA classification under RBI norms and its impact on bank balance sheets.

Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Read the official stream-wise eligibility carefully — applying to an incorrect stream may lead to disqualification.
  • Practice subject-specific answer-writing for Mains (if descriptive papers are present).
  • Keep scanned documents in the required format and sizes to avoid upload rejections.
  • Focus on agriculture & rural-development current affairs — NABARD emphasizes domain knowledge.

How to Check UPSC Mains Result 2025 — Step by Step

  1. Go to the official UPSC website: upsc.gov.in.
  2. Find the notification link titled "UPSC Mains Result 2025" or "UPSC Mains Result PDF".
  3. Click the PDF link to open the UPSC Mains Result 2025 PDF.
  4. Use Ctrl + F and enter your Roll Number to locate your result in the upsc mains merit list 2025.
  5. Download and save the PDF for future reference (Interview call letter, original documents verification).

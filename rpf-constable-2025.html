Official Notification

The recruitment notification lists posts (Constable / SI), region/zone-wise vacancies, eligibility and the complete schedule. Read the PDF thoroughly.

Registration Window

Online registration opens for a limited period — ensure scanned documents & category certificates are ready before applying.

PET / PST Notices

PET / PST is usually qualifying in nature. Standards (timings, distances, loads) differ by gender and post; check annexures in the notification.

Selection Stages

Typical flow: Apply Online → CBT (objective) → PET/PST → Document Verification → Medical → Final merit & appointment.

Recruiting Bodies

Railway Protection Force (RPF) / Government Railway Police (GRP) or associated recruiting authority — recruitment may be centralised or region-wise depending on the cycle.

Posts Covered

Constable (Male/Female), Sub-Inspector (SI) — protective, law & order and security roles within railway premises and assets.

Selection Flow

Online CBT → PET/PST (Qualifying) → Document Verification → Medical Examination → Final appointment based on merit and vacancies.

Eligibility & Qualification

  • Nationality: Indian citizen or other categories as allowed in the notification (e.g., Nepal/Bhutan/Tibetan refugees) — check the CEN for exact list.
  • Age: Age limits and relaxations (SC/ST/OBC/PwD/Ex-Servicemen) are specified in the notification — confirm the cut-off date for DOB calculation.
  • Educational Qualification: Minimum educational requirement usually 10+2 (Constable) or Graduate for SI — confirm post-wise criteria in the notification.
  • Physical Standards: Height, chest (for male) and minimum physical endurance standards are prescribed; regional relaxations may apply for hill/tribal areas.
  • Other: Character, antecedents and medical fitness per Railway medical standards; candidates with debarments from earlier cycles may be ineligible.

Vacancy & Pay

Vacancy tables (zone/region-wise and category-wise) are published in the official notification / vacancy PDF. Totals and allocations are authoritative in the published vacancy spreadsheet.

Pay & Allowances

Pay scales follow Central Government / Railway pay rules for constabulary and SI ranks — basic pay + DA + HRA and other allowances. Exact pay level and in-hand pay depend on the pay matrix and posting location.

Exam Pattern (CBT Overview)

Computer Based Test (CBT)

  • Objective multiple-choice test often covering: General Awareness, General Intelligence & Reasoning, Numerical Ability/Mathematics, and Basic Computer/Domain Knowledge (if applicable).
  • Number of questions, marks, duration and negative marking (if any) are specified in the notification — some cycles use sectional cut-offs.
  • CBT score is used to shortlist candidates for PET/PST (qualifying) and subsequent stages.

Physical Efficiency Test / Physical Standards Test

  • PET standards (race timings, long jump, high jump, etc.) and PST (height/chest) are qualifying in nature — standards vary by gender and post.
  • Candidates must produce original certificates of DOB, education and category at PST/DV stages.

Syllabus Highlights

  • General Awareness: Current affairs (national & state), Indian polity, geography, basic economy, recent railway/security news and national schemes.
  • General Intelligence & Reasoning: Analogies, classification, series, puzzles, seating arrangement, coding-decoding, syllogisms.
  • Numerical Ability / Mathematics: Arithmetic, percentage, ratio & proportion, averages, time & distance, mensuration basics and data interpretation.
  • Domain / Computer Basics (if applicable): Basic computer knowledge, MS Office basics, common internet/email concepts.

Selection Process

  1. Online Application: Register & apply during the published window; upload photograph, signature & certificates in prescribed formats.
  2. CBT: Objective test used to shortlist candidates as per merit & reservation rules.
  3. PET / PST: Qualifying physical tests to verify fitness & standards for the job.
  4. Document Verification (DV): Verification of original documents including education, category, domicile, NOC (if applicable).
  5. Medical Examination: Final medical fitness test following Railway medical standards before appointment.
  6. Final Merit: Prepared using CBT (and post-CBT qualifiers where applicable) and subject to vacancy & reservation norms.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official RPF / recruiting authority portal or the link provided in the notification.
  2. Read the notification carefully — note post-wise eligibility, important dates, required certificates and application fee details.
  3. Register with a valid email & mobile number to obtain login credentials.
  4. Complete the online application form accurately and choose region/post preferences as applicable.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required certificates in the prescribed format & size.
  6. Pay application fee online (if applicable) and submit. Save/print the confirmation & registration number for future reference.

Important Dates (Typical / Tentative)

  • Notification Release: 2025 (check the official RPF / recruiting authority PDF for the exact date)
  • Online Registration Start: As announced in the notification
  • Last Date to Apply: Typically within 2–3 weeks of registration opening (see notification)
  • CBT Dates: Announced via admit cards — check your registered email & regional portal
  • PET / PST Dates: Published for shortlisted candidates post-CBT
  • Document Verification & Medical: Dates shared by recruiting authority for shortlisted candidates

Note: Dates & process details vary by recruitment cycle and region — always verify exact schedules on the official notification before acting.

8–10 Week Preparation Plan (Practical)

  1. Weeks 1–2: Strengthen arithmetic basics, reasoning fundamentals and start daily current affairs focused on national & railway news.
  2. Weeks 3–5: Sectional practice — reasoning puzzles, DI basics, arithmetic speed drills and GK revision.
  3. Weeks 6–7: Full-length timed mocks for CBT pattern, error analysis and improvement of weaker sections.
  4. Weeks 8–10: PET readiness — cardiovascular & sprint training (gradual), final mock revision, document checklist and exam-day practice.

Recommended Books & Free Resources

  • Reasoning & Aptitude — R.S. Aggarwal / Arun Sharma for topic-wise practice and shortcut methods.
  • Arithmetic — NCERT basics (Class 6–10) and practice books for speed & accuracy.
  • General Awareness — Daily newspapers (Hindu/Indian Express), monthly current affairs compilations and official RPF / Railway-related news.
  • PET Preparation — basic endurance & sprint routines; consult a fitness trainer if required and follow a gradual plan to avoid injury.
  • Mock Tests & PYQs — official previous year papers (if available) and reputable online test series for CBT simulation.

Mini Practice Set

  1. Numerical: A shopkeeper sold an item for ₹1,250 at a loss of 20%. What was the cost price?
  2. Reasoning: If in a code LANGUAGE -> MZBKZFT, how would you encode PEOPLE? (Practice substitution/shift patterns.)
  3. General Awareness: Name two flagship national security initiatives and one objective of each.

Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Read the official notification thoroughly — small details (age cut-off, relaxations, local language requirements) matter at DV stage.
  • Prioritise accuracy in CBT to avoid losing marks due to negative marking (if applicable).
  • Do PET practice progressively; sudden intense training may cause injury—consult a physician if in doubt.
  • Keep scanned copies of all required documents in the exact formats and sizes specified to avoid upload rejection.
  • Note region/zone preferences carefully — some recruitments fix region-wise postings and language requirements.

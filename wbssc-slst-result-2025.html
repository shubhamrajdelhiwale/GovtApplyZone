WBSSC SLST 2025 Selection Process

Selection for WBSSC SLST 2025 will be completed via multiple stages:

  1. Written Examination (CBT / OMR as per notification).
  2. Academic Qualification Verification.
  3. Interview / Lecture Demonstration.
  4. Document Verification.
  5. Medical Examination.

Candidates must carefully follow the instructions in the official notification for stage-wise details, marks weightage and document checklist.

How to Download WBSSC SLST Result 2025 (Scorecard / PDF)

Follow these steps to download the WBSSC SLST Result 2025 and scorecard (WBSSC SLST result 2025 pdf download):

  1. Visit the official WBSSC website: westbengalssc.com or use the direct PDF link above.

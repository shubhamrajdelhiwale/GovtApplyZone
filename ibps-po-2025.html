Eligibility & Qualification

  • Nationality: Indian citizen / subject of Nepal/Bhutan / Tibetan refugee / person of Indian origin as per IBPS norms.
  • Age: Typically between 20–30 years (relaxation for reserved categories as per government rules). See official notification.
  • Educational Qualification: Graduate in any discipline from a recognized university.
  • Language: Proficiency in local language of the state/region applied for is often required.
  • Other: No criminal record; meet physical/medical standards where applicable.

Vacancy & Salary

Official state-wise vacancy numbers will be published in the IBPS PO 2025 notification. Vacancies are allocated across participating banks.

  • State/UT-wise distribution
  • Participating Banks' roster-based allotment
  • Category-wise seat reservation per Government rules

Salary & Allowances

Probationary Officer / MT pay varies by bank — generally starting in the range of ₹40,000–₹50,000 in-hand (including allowances) during probation with basic pay per bank pay-scales (basic + DA + HRA + special allowances). Confirm exact pay scale in bank-specific offer letter.

Exam Pattern (Quick)

Preliminary Exam

  • Objective type (Online). Sections: English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning Ability.
  • Total time: ~60 minutes (sectional timings as per notification).
  • Qualifying in nature — marks are used for shortlisting to Mains.

Main Exam

  • Objective & Descriptive paper. Objective sections include: Reasoning & Computer Aptitude, General/Economy/Banking Awareness, English Language, Data Analysis & Interpretation.
  • Descriptive Test (English): Letter/Essay writing — usually to be attempted online.
  • Negative marking typically applies for wrong answers (e.g., 1/4th).

Interview

Candidates shortlisted from Mains appear for interview conducted by participating banks/IBPS. Final merit based on Mains + Interview marks (weights per notification).

Syllabus Highlights

  • English: Reading comprehension, Error spotting, Para jumbles, Vocabulary, Para-summary.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Arithmetic (Percentages, Profit & Loss), Algebra, Data Interpretation, Permutation & Combination, Time & Work, Ratio & Proportion.
  • Reasoning: Puzzles, Seating Arrangements, Syllogisms, Input-Output, Sufficient/Assumption-based reasoning.
  • General/Banking Awareness: Current Affairs (banking/finance), RBI/Monetary policy, Budget, Financial terms, Banking Acts.
  • Computer Aptitude & Data Analysis: Basics of computer, MS Office, Graphs, Tables, Interpretation techniques.

Selection Process

  1. Apply & Pay Fee: Submit application form & pay online fee (as per category).
  2. Prelims: Online objective test for screening.
  3. Mains: Online objective + descriptive test. Marks used for shortlisting for interview.
  4. Interview: Personal interview for shortlisted candidates; document verification follows.
  5. Final Allotment: Based on combined marks and bank preferences; provisional allotment issued by IBPS.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official IBPS recruitment portal (ibps.in) and navigate to the CRP for PO/MT recruitment notification.
  2. Register with a valid email and mobile number to generate login credentials.
  3. Fill all personal, educational & preference details in the online application form.
  4. Upload scanned documents: photograph, signature, category certificate (if applicable), age proof, and any other required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee online and submit the form. Note the registration number and take a printout for records.

8-Week Preparation Plan (Brief)

  1. Weeks 1–2: Strengthen basics — arithmetic, grammar, reasoning fundamentals, daily current affairs (banking focus).
  2. Weeks 3–4: Timed sectional practice, DI sets, puzzles, and mock Prelims every 3–4 days.
  3. Weeks 5–6: Shift focus to speed & accuracy; start Mains-level practice (longer DI, advanced reasoning, GA depth).
  4. Weeks 7–8: Full-length mocks, descriptive writing practice (essay/letter), interview pointers & document readiness.

Top Books & Free Resources

  • Quantitative Aptitude — RS Aggarwal / M. Tyra (topic practice)
  • Reasoning — R.S. Aggarwal / MK Pandey (puzzles & seating)
  • English — Word Power Made Easy ( Norman Lewis ), previous year papers
  • Banking Awareness — Monthly current affairs compilations; RBI/IBPS circulars
  • Mock Tests — Online test series from trusted coaching platforms; IBPS previous year papers

Sample Mini Practice Set

  1. Quant: If a sum becomes double in 7 years at simple interest, what is the rate per annum?
  2. Reasoning: Five people A,B,C,D,E sit in a row. B sits second to the left of D. C sits at one of the ends. A is immediate left of E. Who sits at center?
  3. English: Read the passage and answer a detail-based question (practice RCs for speed).

Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Maintain accuracy over attempting too many questions — negative marking penalizes guessing.
  • Practice sectional time management; don't spend too long on a single DI set.
  • For Mains descriptive, practice clear structured essays and formal letters within time limits.
  • Keep scanned copies of all certificates ready before applying to avoid last-minute issues.

Eligibility Snapshot

Typically: Master’s degree (55%+), NET/PhD as per UGC/DU rules — department-specific relaxations may apply.

Selection Process

Shortlisting → Presentation / Interview → Final merit & appointment.

Eligibility & Qualification

  • Educational Qualification: Master’s degree in relevant subject with a minimum of 55% marks (or equivalent grade) from a recognized university.
  • NET / SLET / PhD: As per UGC regulations — many posts require NET or PhD for direct recruitment or for relaxation in marks.
  • Experience: Post-PhD research experience or publications may be desirable for some departments.
  • Reservation / Relaxation: Category-wise age and percentage relaxations as applicable under Govt. rules.

Vacancy & Pay Scale

Vacancy numbers will be department-wise in the official notification. Typical pay scale for Assistant Professor (UGC) follows Pay Level-10/Level-11 depending on selection and institution rules.

  • Entry Pay: As per 7th CPC / UGC norms (indicative range ₹57,700–₹1,82,400 depending on level and allowances).
  • Benefits: DA, HRA, medical facilities, pension/NPS contributions and other allowances as per DU rules.

Selection Process

  1. Application & Shortlisting: Online application → Shortlisting based on qualifications and experience.
  2. Presentation / Seminar: Shortlisted candidates invited for a presentation or seminar on a given topic.
  3. Interview: Departmental & selection committee interview assessing teaching & research capability.
  4. Document Verification: Original documents, qualifications, and caste certificates verified before appointment.

Syllabus / Topics (Interview & Presentation)

There is no common written syllabus for assistant professor posts; evaluation is based on subject expertise. Prepare these areas:

  • Core subject fundamentals and advanced topics in your discipline.
  • Teaching methodology and pedagogy relevant to higher education.
  • Research contributions, publications, and proposed research plan.
  • Current developments & applications in the subject area.

How to Apply (Expected Steps)

  1. Read the official DU notification carefully for department-wise eligibility and documents required.
  2. Register on DU recruitment portal and create a login (email & mobile verification).
  3. Fill the online application form, upload scanned documents (degree, NET/PhD certificate, publications).
  4. Pay application fee (if applicable) and submit final form before the last date.
  5. Download and save the submitted application & fee receipt for future reference.

Important Dates (Tentative)

  • Notification Release: Feb 2025 (expected)
  • Start Date to Apply Online: Mid Feb 2025 (expected)
  • Last Date to Apply: Early Mar 2025 (expected)
  • Departmental Shortlisting / Interviews: Apr – Jun 2025 (tentative)

Preparation Tips

  • Focus on high-quality research publications and list of your conferences & workshops.
  • Prepare a clear and concise presentation (10–15 minutes) highlighting research impact and teaching plan.
  • Revise core concepts and recent developments in your subject area.
  • Be ready to discuss pedagogical approaches and potential course outlines.

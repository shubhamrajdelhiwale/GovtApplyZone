CRP-RRB 2025 Notification

IBPS issues a combined notification for RRB recruitment covering Office Assistant (Clerk) and Officer Scale posts. Official PDF contains post-wise & region-wise vacancies.

Apply Online (Tentative)

Registration typically opens shortly after notification; candidates must complete registration and fee payment within the specified window.

Vacancy Details

Vacancies published state/zone-wise for participating Regional Rural Banks (RRBs). Expect separate PDFs for Office Assistant and Officer scales.

Exam Stages

Officer Scale I: Prelims → Mains → Interview. Officer Scale II/III: Single level or Mains as per notification. Office Assistant: Prelims → Mains (where applicable).

Recruiting Body

Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) — Common Recruitment Process for Regional Rural Banks (CRP-RRB).

Posts Covered

Office Assistant (Clerk), Officer Scale I (Assistant Manager), Officer Scale II (Manager - General/IT/Agriculture), Officer Scale III (Senior Manager).

Selection Flow

Preliminary Exam → Main Exam → Interview (for Officer posts) → Final allotment based on merit & bank preferences.

Eligibility & Qualification

  • Nationality: Indian citizen / subject of Nepal or Bhutan / Tibetan refugee / person of Indian origin (rules as per IBPS).
  • Age: Varies by post — e.g., Office Assistant typically 18–28 years; Officer Scale I usually 18–30 years; relaxations for reserved categories apply. Check notification for exact cut-off dates.
  • Educational Qualification:
    • Office Assistant: Bachelor’s degree in any discipline from a recognized university.
    • Officer Scale I: Graduate in any discipline (specializations for certain posts may be specified).
    • Officer Scale II/III: Postgraduate / specified experience or qualifications for specialized posts (IT/Agriculture) — refer to notification.
  • Local Language: Must be proficient in the local language of the applied RRB/region (read official notification for specific requirements).
  • Experience: Some officer posts may require prior experience; check post-wise eligibility criteria.

Vacancy & Salary

IBPS publishes detailed state/zone-wise vacancy lists for each CRP-RRB cycle. Vacancy numbers and any subsequent revisions will be available in the official vacancy PDF.

Salary & Allowances

Pay scales depend on the recruiting RRB and the post. Typical rough ranges:

  • Office Assistant (Clerk): In-hand pay varies, basic pay per bank scale + allowances.
  • Officer Scale I: Manager-level pay scale; basic + DA + HRA & other allowances.
  • Officer Scale II/III: Higher managerial scales with commensurate allowances.
For exact pay band and emoluments, refer to participating RRBs' recruitment pages or the official notification.

Exam Pattern (Summary)

Office Assistant (Prelims)

  • Objective online test — sections: Numerical Ability & Reasoning (time and structure per notification).
  • Screening purpose — shortlisted for Main exam based on cut-offs.

Office Assistant (Main)

  • Objective online test covering: Reasoning, Numerical Ability, General Awareness, English/Hindi, Computer Knowledge (as applicable).
  • Negative marking usually applicable (see notification).

Officer Scale I (Prelims & Main)

  • Prelims: Objective test (Reasoning & Quantitative Aptitude).
  • Mains: Objective test covering Reasoning, Quant, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge and English/Hindi; descriptive/skill tests where applicable.

Officer Scale II/III

Selection typically through online single-level exams or mains + interview (post-dependent). Check notification for exact stage & weightage.

Syllabus Highlights

  • Reasoning: Puzzles, seating, directions, inequalities, input-output.
  • Numerical Ability / Quant: Arithmetic (percentages, ratio, averages), data interpretation, simplification.
  • General Awareness: Banking & financial awareness, current affairs (esp. rural banking & agriculture updates), government schemes.
  • English/Hindi: Grammar, comprehension, vocabulary, sentence improvement.
  • Computer Knowledge: Basics of hardware/software, MS Office, internet concepts.
  • Specialized (Officer II - IT/Agriculture): Technical knowledge & domain-specific questions.

Selection Process

  1. Apply & Upload Documents: Complete online application & upload required scanned documents.
  2. Prelims (where applicable): Objective test for shortlisting to Mains.
  3. Mains / Single-level exam: Objective/Descriptive/Skill test as per post.
  4. Interview: For certain Officer posts (Scale I/II/III) — weightage and qualifying marks specified in notification.
  5. Final Allotment: Based on merit, preferences, and region-wise vacancies (document verification prior to joining).

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official IBPS website (ibps.in) and open CRP-RRB recruitment notification page.
  2. Read the notification carefully for post-wise eligibility, regional allocations and dates.
  3. Register using a valid email & mobile number to generate login credentials.
  4. Complete the online application form: personal details, education, post & region preferences.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature and category/disability certificates (if applicable) as per specified format.
  6. Pay the application fee online and submit. Save/print the application confirmation and registration number.

  • Online Registration Start: Within days of notification
  • Last Date to Apply: 2–3 weeks after registration opens
  • Preliminary Exam: A few weeks to months after application window closes
  • Main Exam: Scheduled after Prelims (for posts with Prelims)
  • Interview (Officer posts): Dates announced after Mains / single level exam

Note: Dates above are placeholders — always confirm exact schedule in the official IBPS notification for CRP-RRB 2025.

8-Week Preparation Plan (Overview)

  1. Weeks 1–2: Strengthen basics — arithmetic, grammar, reasoning basics, start daily current affairs focused on banking/rural schemes.
  2. Weeks 3–4: Sectional practice, DI sets, puzzles; begin previous-year papers for pattern familiarity.
  3. Weeks 5–6: Mains-level practice for Officer posts; domain/technical revision for specialized posts (IT/Agriculture).
  4. Weeks 7–8: Full-length mocks, time management drills, interview prep for officer posts and document readiness for all candidates.

Recommended Books & Free Resources

  • Quant & Reasoning — R.S. Aggarwal, Arun Sharma (topicwise practice)
  • English/Hindi — Wren & Martin / previous IBPS papers for RC & grammar
  • General Awareness — Monthly banking current affairs, RBI releases, government scheme summaries
  • Computer Awareness — Basic computer books and online tutorials
  • Mocks & PYQs — IBPS sample papers and reputed online test series for timed practice

Sample Mini Practice Set

  1. Quant: If the ratio of A:B is 3:4 and A+B = 140, find A.
  2. Reasoning: Seven people sit around a table. X is opposite Y. (Practice seating/puzzle patterns.)
  3. General Awareness: What is the role of NABARD in rural finance? (Short answer)

Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Read the notification carefully for region-specific language/locality requirements — failing to meet local language criteria can affect selection.
  • Practice sectional time allotment; many candidates lose marks on DI/Quant due to poor time management.
  • Keep scanned copies of all documents in required format before starting the application.
  • For Officer posts with interviews, prepare for role-specific questions and basics of rural banking & agricultural finance.

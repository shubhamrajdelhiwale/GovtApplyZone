All About UPSC NDA/NA I 2025

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducts the NDA & NA exam twice each year to select cadets for the National Defence Academy and Naval Academy. NDA recruits young aspirants directly after 12th grade for a career in the Indian Armed Forces — Army, Navy and Air Force — while NA is specific to Naval Academy entries where applicable.

NDA/NA I 2025 is ideal for disciplined, physically fit and academically strong candidates who wish to serve in the defence services. The exam tests mathematics aptitude and general awareness (GAT) followed by the Services Selection Board (SSB) interview which evaluates personality, leadership, psychological suitability and physical fitness.

This page compiles the official rules, syllabus, eligibility criteria, SSB guidelines, medical standards, exam pattern and practical preparation plan to help you apply, prepare and succeed.

Eligibility — Age, Education & Nationality

Nationality

A candidate must be a citizen of India, or subject of Nepal/Bhutan, or Tibetan refugee (arrived before Jan 1962) or person of Indian origin who migrated from specified countries with intention of permanently settling in India as per UPSC notification.

Educational Qualification

For NDA: 12th Class pass of the 10+2 pattern of School Education or equivalent examination conducted by a State Education Board or a University. For NA: same educational requirement plus specified medical & physical standards for naval training.

Age Limit

Candidates must be unmarried and typically between 16.5 to 19.5 years (exact dates in notification). Example: Born not earlier than 2nd Jan 2006 and not later than 1st July 2009 — check the notification for the correct cut-off.

Other Conditions

  • Candidates must be physically fit according to physical standards given in the NDA/NA Rules.
  • Marital status: Unmarried at the time of application.

Always verify exact birth date windows and special relaxations (if any) from the official UPSC NDA/NA I 2025 notification PDF.

Exam Pattern — Written Exam (Objective)

UPSC NDA/NA written exam consists of two papers — Mathematics and General Ability Test (GAT). Both are objective (MCQ) and written on the same day in two separate sessions.

Paper Marks Duration Type
Mathematics3002.5 hoursObjective (MCQ)
General Ability Test (GAT)6002.5 hoursObjective (MCQ) — English & GK sections

Total Marks (Written): 900. Shortlisted candidates after written exam are called for SSB interviews which add to the final merit.

Syllabus — Mathematics & General Ability Test (GAT)

Mathematics

Algebra, Matrices & Determinants, Trigonometry, Analytical Geometry, Differential Calculus, Integral Calculus, Vector Algebra, Statistics & Probability, Mensuration, Complex Numbers and sequences & series.

General Ability Test (GAT)

GAT is divided into two parts:

Part A — English

  • Grammar & vocabulary
  • Comprehension
  • Improving expression

Part B — General Knowledge

  • Physics, Chemistry, General Science
  • History — Indian & World
  • Geography — India & World
  • Current Events — national & international
  • Matters of everyday observation & experience in their scientific aspects

Questions often test clarity of basic concepts of 10+2 level. Emphasize NCERT-level understanding and regular current affairs for the GK component.

SSB Interview & Selection Process

Shortlisted candidates from the written exam are called for SSB (Services Selection Board) — an extensive five-day assessment of personality, intelligence, leadership, and physical fitness. SSB comprises two stages: Stage I (Officer Intelligence Rating and Picture Perception & Description Test) and Stage II (Psychological tests, Group Testing Officer tasks, Personal Interview).

SSB Stages

  • Day 1–2: Stage I tests (shortlisting for Stage II)
  • Day 2–5: Stage II — Psychological Tests, GTO Tasks, Personal Interview
  • Conference & final recommendation (After all tests)

Recommended prep: practice picture perception, group tasks, mock interviews, physical activities (running, push-ups, pull-ups), and honest self-reflection—SSB evaluates personality over time, not just knowledge.

Physical & Medical Standards

General Fitness

Candidates must be physically fit as per physical standards for admission to NDA & NA. Standards cover vision, hearing, orthopedics, and general health. Some disqualifications are color blindness, certain cardiac conditions, and severe myopia (check PDF for specifics).

Height & Weight

Minimum height and proportional weight requirements vary; different services may have slightly different norms. Keep fitness logs and prior medical records handy.

Note: Final medical is done by Service Medical Boards. Ensure honest disclosure of medical history to avoid later disqualification.

How to Apply — UPSC NDA/NA I 2025 (Step-by-step)

  1. Visit UPSC Online (https://upsconline.nic.in) and open the NDA/NA I 2025 application link.
  2. Register with a valid mobile number and email.
  3. Fill the online application; select examination center & exam (NDA/NA I).
  4. Upload scanned photograph and signature as per specified dimensions and format.
  5. Pay the application fee (if applicable) and submit.
  6. Download and print the confirmation page for records; keep extra passport photos for SSB when required.

Keep original certificates ready for later stages — DOB proof, 12th marksheet/ passing certificate, and ID proof (Aadhaar, passport, etc.).

Preparation Strategy — Targeted Plan for NDA/NA I 2025

A balanced study plan combining rigorous Physics/Maths practice and daily current affairs reading for GAT is the key. Work on accuracy for MCQs and stamina for long exam durations and SSB physical tasks.

3-Month Focused Plan

  1. Week 1–4: NCERT basics for Maths & Physics — cover essential topics thoroughly.
  2. Week 5–8: Topic-wise MCQ practice, past-year papers & timed tests.
  3. Week 9–12: Full-length mock tests, GK revision and SSB physical conditioning.

Daily Routine

  • 3–4 hours Maths/Physics practice
  • 1 hour English and grammar practice
  • 1 hour current affairs reading & notes
  • Physical training: running, push-ups, core workouts — 30–60 minutes

Practice previous years’ NDA papers, time yourself, and develop elimination techniques for MCQs. For SSB, join mock SSB workshops if available and practice group tasks and personal interview communication.

Frequently Asked Questions (NDA/NA I 2025)

Who can apply for NDA/NA?
Unmarried male and female (as per notification) candidates who have passed or are appearing in 12th class and meet age & nationality criteria can apply. Check official notification for gender-specific rules (NA entries and eligibility differ sometimes).
How many papers are there in NDA written exam?
Two papers: Mathematics (300 marks) and General Ability Test (600 marks). Both are objective.
Is there negative marking?
Yes, negative marking is applicable for incorrect answers — refer to the notification for exact marking scheme.
What is SSB? How long is it?
SSB (Services Selection Board) is a 5-day selection process including psychological tests, group tasks and interview to assess officer-like qualities in candidates.
Are NDA candidates required to attend medicals?
Yes. Candidates recommended by SSB undergo medical examination by Service Medical Boards before final selection.
Can I apply for both NDA & NA simultaneously?
The application form allows selecting both options where applicable. Read the notification for details on preferences and cadetship options.
Which books should I use for GAT?
For GAT, combine NCERTs, a good objective GK book like Lucent, Current Affairs from The Hindu/Indian Express, and practice question banks for English.
How do I improve for SSB?
Work on communication skills, group discussions, physical fitness, and practice mock SSB sessions for group tasks and personal interviews.

Paper Code: AG

GATE Agriculture Engineering paper is one of the technical papers used for M.Tech, PhD & PSU recruitment.

Exam Mode

Computer Based Test (CBT), 3 hours, 65 questions, 100 marks (MCQ, MSQ, NAT).

Key Subjects

Farm Machinery, Soil & Water Conservation, Irrigation, Food Processing, Energy, Farm Power, Surveying.

GATE AG Syllabus 2025

General Aptitude (GA)

  • Verbal Aptitude
  • Numerical Aptitude
  • Analytical Reasoning

Agriculture Engineering (AG) Core Topics

  • Farm Machinery & Power — Engines, tractors, implements, testing.
  • Soil & Water Conservation — Hydrology, erosion, watershed management.
  • Irrigation & Drainage — Canal design, pumps, micro-irrigation.
  • Agricultural Processing — Drying, milling, storage, food engineering.
  • Surveying & Land Management — Leveling, GPS, GIS.
  • Energy in Agriculture — Renewable energy, biogas systems.

Eligibility Criteria

  • B.E/B.Tech in Agricultural Engineering or equivalent.
  • Final year students can apply.
  • No age limit to appear for GATE.
  • Foreign degree students also eligible (as per notification).

Exam Pattern 2025

  • General Aptitude: 15 marks
  • Agriculture Engineering: 85 marks
  • Question Types: MCQ, MSQ, NAT
  • Duration: 3 hours

Previous Year Cut Off (AG)

  • 2024: General – 34.7, OBC – 32.1, SC – 27.3, ST – 25.0
  • 2023: General – 30.4, OBC – 27.7, SC – 22.0, ST – 21.0
  • 2022: General – 26.3, OBC – 23.8, SC – 19.4, ST – 18.0

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit GATE application portal (GOAPS).
  2. Register with email + mobile.
  3. Upload documents (photo, signature, degree proof).
  4. Select paper “AG – Agriculture Engineering”.
  5. Pay fees and submit final form.

Preparation Strategy

  • Solve previous year papers (10+ years mandatory).
  • Focus on scoring units: Soil Mechanics, Machinery, Irrigation.
  • Attempt weekly full-length mocks to improve speed.
  • Revise formulas & numerical concepts regularly.

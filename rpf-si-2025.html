Eligibility & Essential Qualifications

  • Nationality: Indian citizen / subject of Nepal or Bhutan / Tibetan refugee / person of Indian origin as specified in the notification.
  • Age: Upper & lower age limits and relaxations are defined in the notification — check the cut-off date for DOB calculation.
  • Educational Qualification: Typically a Graduate degree from a recognized university for SI posts — exact requirement may vary by post/zone (check notification).
  • Physical Standards: Height, chest (where applicable), vision and other fitness standards listed in the annexure — regional relaxations for hill/tribal areas may apply.
  • Debarment: Candidates debarred in previous recruitments are ineligible — read the ineligibility clauses carefully.

Vacancy & Pay Scale

The notification includes a detailed vacancy table (zone/region & category-wise) and any subsequent corrigenda. Use the official vacancy PDF for exact numbers.

Pay & Perks

Sub-Inspector pay follows Central Government / Railway pay rules for the rank — basic pay + DA + HRA + other allowances. Exact in-hand salary varies by place of posting and allowances; check the notification/offer letter for precise emoluments.

Exam Pattern — CBT Overview

Computer Based Test (CBT)

  • Objective multiple-choice test covering: General Awareness, General Intelligence & Reasoning, Numerical Ability, and Basic Computer/Domain Knowledge where applicable.
  • Number of questions, total marks, duration and negative marking (if any) are specified in the official notification.
  • CBT scores are used to shortlist candidates for PET/PST (qualifying) and subsequent stages; sectional/overall cut-offs may be applied.

Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Physical Standards Test (PST)

  • PET: Timed running/jumping events (e.g., 1600m/800m run, long jump) with gender-specific standards — qualifying in nature.
  • PST: Height and chest (for male) standards measured as per annexure. Medical screening for fitness (vision, hearing, chronic conditions) follows PST.
  • Detailed standards and permissible relaxations for reserved categories/hilly areas are included in the notification annexures.

Syllabus Highlights

  • General Awareness: Current affairs (national & state), Indian polity, geography, economy basics, railway security & law enforcement updates.
  • General Intelligence & Reasoning: Analogy, classification, series, coding-decoding, puzzles, seating, blood relations, syllogism.
  • Numerical Ability: Arithmetic (percentages, ratio & proportion, averages), time & work, speed & distance, basic data interpretation.
  • Computer/Domain Basics: Basic hardware/software, MS Office, internet usage, email and applications in policing/security (if included).

Selection Process (Step-by-Step)

  1. Apply Online: Fill application, upload documents and pay fee within the published window.
  2. CBT: Objective exam used for shortlisting to PET/PST. Cut-offs applied as per notification.
  3. PET / PST: Qualifying physical tests and measurement of physical standards.
  4. Document Verification (DV): Original certificates verification, character check, category proofs, etc.
  5. Medical Examination: Final medical fitness test in line with Railway/RPF medical standards.
  6. Final Merit & Appointment: Prepared using CBT (and any prescribed weightage for PET/PST if applicable), subject to vacancy and reservation norms.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the official RPF / recruiting authority recruitment portal and locate the RPF SI 2025 notification page.
  2. Read the notification thoroughly — note eligibility, physical standards, fee and document list.
  3. Register with a valid email & mobile number; complete the online application with accurate personal and educational details.
  4. Upload scanned photograph, signature and required certificates in the prescribed size & format.
  5. Pay application fee online (if applicable) and submit. Save/print the confirmation & registration number.
  6. Keep originals and multiple photocopies of documents ready for DV & medical rounds.

Important Dates (Tentative)

  • Notification Release: 2025 — check official portal for the PDF.
  • Online Registration Start & Last Date: Specified in notification (usually window of 2–3 weeks).
  • CBT Date(s): Announced via admit cards after application close.
  • PET / PST: Scheduled for shortlisted candidates after CBT.

10-Week Preparation Plan (Practical)

  1. Weeks 1–2: Strengthen basics: arithmetic fundamentals, reasoning concepts and daily current affairs (security & railway focus).
  2. Weeks 3–5: Sectional timed practice (CBT-style), start practicing previous-year CBT questions and short tests.
  3. Weeks 6–7: Full-length CBT mocks, DI & error analysis; begin light PET training (running, jumps) gradually to build stamina.
  4. Weeks 8–9: Intensive revision, mock PETs under timed conditions (simulate event & rest cycles), and document readiness checks.
  5. Week 10: Final mocks, mental conditioning, rest & exam-day planning (travel, documents, medical certificates).

Recommended Books & Free Resources

  • General Aptitude & Reasoning — R.S. Aggarwal / Arun Sharma for practice & shortcuts.
  • Current Affairs — Daily newspapers (The Hindu / Indian Express), monthly compendiums and security/railway news updates.
  • Computer Basics — Basic MS Office & internet tutorials (free online courses).
  • PET Training — Gradual running programs, plyometrics for jumps; consult a trainer for safe training plans.
  • Mocks & PYQs — Official previous-year papers (if available) and reputable online timed test series for CBT practice.

Mini Practice Set

  1. Numerical: If a train covers 180 km in 3 hours, what is its average speed (km/h)?
  2. Reasoning: Arrange the words: {planet, star, galaxy, moon} in increasing order of size. Explain briefly.
  3. GA: Name two central government schemes aimed at railway safety or infrastructure and one objective of each.

Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Read the official notification thoroughly — physical standards & medical clauses are crucial and non-negotiable at DV/medical stages.
  • Start PET practice early and progress gradually to avoid injuries — PET is qualifying and failure eliminates candidacy.
  • Accuracy in CBT is more valuable than reckless attempts if negative marking applies — practice calculated attempts.
  • Keep all documents (education, DOB, category, no-objection, discharge certificate if Ex-Serviceman) scanned in required formats.
  • Monitor official portals and registered email/phone for corrigenda, admit card and call letters—don't rely solely on third-party sites.

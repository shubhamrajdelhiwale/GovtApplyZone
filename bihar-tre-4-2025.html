About TRE-4 2025

Teacher Recruitment Examination TRE-4 is Bihar’s largest teaching recruitment cycle, conducted under the School Education Department & BSEB.

The TRE-4 exam structure is designed for:

  • Primary Teacher (Class 1–5)
  • Middle Teacher (Class 6–8)
  • Secondary Teacher (Class 9–10)
  • Higher Secondary Teacher (Class 11–12)

Candidates preparing for Bihar TRE 2025 can also apply for TRE-4 depending on their eligibility & certificates.

TRE-4 Exam Pattern & Syllabus

Primary (Class 1–5)

  • Child Development & Pedagogy
  • Language I (Hindi/Urdu)
  • Language II (English)
  • Math
  • Environmental Studies

Middle (Class 6–8)

  • Language I & II
  • Pedagogy
  • Mathematics / Science / Social Science
  • Teaching Aptitude

Secondary (Class 9–10)

  • Subject Knowledge (Math, Science, SST, Hindi, English)
  • General Awareness
  • Pedagogy + Reasoning Ability

Higher Secondary (Class 11–12)

  • Subject Expertise (Science/Arts/Commerce/Vocational)
  • Teaching Aptitude
  • General Studies

Eligibility Criteria

  • D.El.Ed / B.Ed (category-wise)
  • Bihar STET / CTET / TET (mandatory)
  • Indian Citizen & Bihar domicile relaxation
  • Age limit: 21–37 years (category relaxation)

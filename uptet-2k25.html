Exam

UPTET (Uttar Pradesh Teacher Eligibility Test) for Primary & Upper Primary teachers.

Papers

Paper I – Primary (Class 1–5), Paper II – Upper Primary (Class 6–8).

Mode

Offline (OMR) / Online — follow official notification for final mode.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Nationality: Indian citizen — residence in UP not mandatory for appearing but may affect recruitment preference.
  • Educational Qualification (Paper I): Senior Secondary (or equivalent) with 2-year diploma in elementary education / B.El.Ed / D.El.Ed / BTC as per latest norms.
  • Educational Qualification (Paper II): Graduation with B.Ed / or relevant secondary teacher eligibility qualifications.
  • Age Limit: As per notification (usually no strict upper age for TET eligibility but check recruitment rules where TET is required).

Exam Pattern

Paper Subjects Questions Marks Duration
Paper I (Primary) Child Development & Pedagogy, Language I & II, Maths, Environmental Studies 150 150 2 hours
Paper II (Upper Primary) Child Development & Pedagogy, Language I & II, Mathematics & Science / Social Studies 150 150 2 hours

Syllabus — Key Topics

Paper I (Primary) — Highlights

  • Child Development & Pedagogy
  • Language I (Hindi / Urdu) — reading, comprehension, pedagogy
  • Language II (English) — grammar, comprehension, teaching)
  • Mathematics — numbers, basic operations, shapes, pedagogy
  • Environmental Studies — basics of science & social studies

Paper II (Upper Primary) — Highlights

  • Child Development & Pedagogy (upper primary focus)
  • Language I & II (teaching methods, comprehension)
  • Mathematics & Science — higher-level concepts
  • Social Studies — history, geography, civics (if opted)
  • Teaching methodology & assessment techniques

How to Apply

  1. Wait for official UPTET notification PDF — it will list the portal link and schedule.
  2. Register on the UPTET / UP Government recruitment portal with valid email & mobile.
  3. Fill the application form, choose Paper I / Paper II / Both, upload required documents, and pay fee.
  4. Submit and download the confirmation/receipt. Keep a copy for later recruitment processes.

Preparation Tips

  • Focus on pedagogy — understand how children learn and common classroom strategies.
  • Practice previous-year UPTET question papers and mock tests (time-bound).
  • Revise basic maths and science concepts for Paper II thoroughly.
  • Maintain short notes for current affairs related to education & state policies.

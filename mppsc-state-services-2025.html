Mains — Model answers & writing tips

Q1: Discuss the major irrigation projects in Madhya Pradesh and their role in agricultural development. (250 words)

Introduction: Irrigation projects are crucial for stabilizing agricultural output in Madhya Pradesh, a largely rainfed state with variable monsoons.

Major projects & impact:

  1. Betwa & Rajghat projects: Increase irrigation acreage and support cropping intensity in Bundelkhand and adjacent regions.
  2. Indira Sagar & Omkareshwar: Multipurpose projects that combine irrigation with hydroelectricity and flood control.
  3. Watershed programs: Jalyukt-like interventions improve groundwater recharge and smallholder resilience.

Conclusion & recommendation: Strengthen command area development, adopt micro-irrigation, and integrate agro-advisory services to maximize benefits.

Q2: Analyse the challenges of urbanization in Bhopal and suggest policy measures. (250 words)

Intro: Rapid urbanization in Bhopal has created pressures on housing, transport and sanitation.

Challenges & measures:

  • Infrastructure lag: invest in public transport & decentralized sewage systems.
  • Affordable housing: incentivize rental housing & PPP models.
  • Green cover & resilience: expand urban green spaces and stormwater management.

Conclusion: Integrated urban planning with citizen participation will improve liveability and service delivery.

Q3: Explain the role of the Finance Commission in fiscal federalism and its relevance to MP. (250 words)

Intro: Finance Commissions recommend transfer of resources between Centre and States, shaping state finances.

Key functions & MP relevance:

  1. Vertical & horizontal distribution of taxes influences MP's resource pool.
  2. Performance grants incentivize sectoral reforms (health, education).
  3. Special grants for disaster-prone or backward districts can help MP's development goals.

Conclusion: MP should align state reforms with incentive structures to maximize Finance Commission transfers.

Mains tip: use recent state data, cite government schemes & include a short recommendation to score higher. Refer to official state surveys for figures. :contentReference[oaicite:18]{index=18}

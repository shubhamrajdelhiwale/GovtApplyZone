Eligibility Criteria

  • UPTET/CTET qualified candidates are eligible to apply.
  • Graduation + B.Ed or D.El.Ed compulsory (as per UP govt norms).
  • Minimum 50% marks in graduation (category relaxations apply).
  • Age: 21–40 years (relaxations as per UP state rules).

Exam Pattern 2025

  • Total Marks: 150
  • No negative marking
  • Duration: 2 hours 30 minutes
  • Subjects: Child Development, Hindi, English, Maths, EVS, Teaching Skills, GK, Reasoning

Syllabus 2025

  • Child Development & Pedagogy
  • Environmental Studies
  • Hindi & English Language
  • Maths & Reasoning
  • General Knowledge & Current Affairs
  • Teaching Methodology & Educational Psychology

How to Apply for SUPER TET 2025

  1. Visit official website of UP Basic Education Board.
  2. Register using mobile number and email.
  3. Fill application form with personal & educational details.
  4. Upload scanned documents (photo, sign, certificates).
  5. Pay application fee online.
  6. Submit and print application for future use.

Important Dates (Tentative)

  • Notification Release: March 2025
  • Online Registration: March–April 2025
  • Admit Card Download: May 2025
  • Exam Date: May–June 2025
  • Result: July 2025 (expected)

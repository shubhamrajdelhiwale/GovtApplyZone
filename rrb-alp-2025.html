Posts Covered

Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) and Technician trades (Electrician, Fitter, Carpenter, Welder, Machinist, Signal & Telecom, Electronics, etc.) — trade codes listed in the CEN.

Selection Flow

CBT-1 → CBT-2 (trade-specific) → Computer Based Aptitude Test / Skill Test / Typing (where applicable) → Document Verification → Medical Examination → Final appointment.

Eligibility (Key Points)

  • Nationality: Indian citizen / subject of Nepal or Bhutan / Tibetan refugee / person of Indian origin — refer to CEN for full list.
  • Age: Age limits and relaxations are specified in the CEN — check upper/lower limits and cut-off dates per RRB.
  • Educational Qualification:
    • ALP: Typically requires ITI / Diploma / Degree in engineering or prescribed trade qualification as per CEN.
    • Technician: ITI (in relevant trade) or Diploma/degree as specified for each technician post — verify trade-specific criteria in the CEN.
  • Medical & Fitness: Candidates must meet medical fitness standards prescribed for ALP/Technician roles — details appear in the CEN and medical standards annexures.
  • Debarment: Candidates debarred by any RRB/RRC are ineligible; check CEN notes.

Vacancy & Pay

Vacancy numbers are provided RRB-wise in the CEN vacancy PDF. Totals and trade-wise allocations may be revised — always use the official vacancy spreadsheet for exact post-wise and category-wise counts.

Pay & Allowances

ALP & Technician pay follow Indian Railways pay scales for the respective grades — basic pay + DA + HRA + other allowances. Exact pay level and in-hand salary vary by posting city and grade; refer to CEN / Indian Railways pay matrix for precise figures.

Exam Pattern (Overview)

CBT-1 (Screening)

  • Objective online test — common sections include: General Awareness, Arithmetic / Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, and Basic Science & Technical Awareness (level as per post).
  • Typical structure: ~100 questions, time ~90–120 minutes — confirm exact marks/time in your regional CEN.
  • Negative marking commonly applies for wrong answers — consult CEN for the marking scheme.

CBT-2 (Mains / Trade-specific)

  • More detailed test including trade/technical questions for Technician streams and discipline-specific items for ALP shortlisting.
  • CBT-2 score primarily used for shortlisting to aptitude/skill tests and final merit computation where applicable.

Aptitude / Skill / Typing Tests

Depending on post: Computer Based Aptitude Test (specially for ALP), Skill Test (practical trade test for Technician posts), and Typing Test (for clerical/typing-designated posts). These are usually qualifying in nature with minimum standards set in the CEN.

Syllabus Highlights

  • General Awareness: Current affairs, Indian polity, basic geography, economy, and railway-specific awareness.
  • Mathematics / Arithmetic: Number systems, percentages, ratio & proportion, averages, mensuration, speed & distance, time & work.
  • General Intelligence & Reasoning: Puzzles, seating arrangements, syllogisms, coding-decoding, analogies, classification, directions, blood relations.
  • Technical / Trade Knowledge: Fundamentals relevant to the trade — e.g., electrical basics for electrician, fitting/machining for fitter, signal & telecom fundamentals for signal posts, etc.
  • Computer Basics (if applicable): Basic hardware/software, MS Office, networking concepts, and usage of diagnostic tools where relevant.

Selection Process

  1. Online Application: Apply on the central apply portal or regional RRB link; upload required documents & pay fee within the window.
  2. CBT-1: Screening test to shortlist candidates for CBT-2.
  3. CBT-2: Trade/discipline-specific objective test to shortlist candidates for aptitude/skill tests.
  4. Aptitude / Skill / Typing Test: Qualifying evaluations for ALP and Technician posts — minimum standards prescribed in the CEN.
  5. Document Verification (DV): Verification of original certificates for shortlisted candidates.
  6. Medical Examination: Railway medical fitness test as per standards for ALP/Technician roles prior to appointment.
  7. Final Merit & Appointment: Based on CBT/skill/aptitude performance (weights per CEN), category reservation and vacancy availability.

How to Apply Online

  1. Open the official RRB apply portal or your regional RRB recruitment page and access the ALP & Technician CEN/notification.
  2. Read the CEN PDF carefully — note post/trade-wise eligibility, documents list, medical/aptitude requirements and fee details.
  3. Register using a valid email & mobile number to get login credentials.
  4. Complete the online application with correct personal, educational and trade/post preferences.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, and certificate copies (education, ITI/diploma, category certificate if applicable) in the prescribed format & size.
  6. Pay the application fee online (if applicable) and submit. Save the application confirmation and registration number for future reference.

10-Week Preparation Plan (Practical)

  1. Weeks 1–2: Strengthen arithmetic, reasoning basics and daily GK; review trade fundamentals (basic circuits, fitting operations, etc.).
  2. Weeks 3–5: Sectional practice — speed-building in arithmetic, reasoning puzzles, and technical MCQs by trade.
  3. Weeks 6–7: CBT-2 level practice (trade-specific questions), previous-year papers and diagnostic tests.
  4. Weeks 8–9: Aptitude/skill test preparation — practice computer-based aptitude (if ALP), hands-on skill drills for Technician trades, and typing practice if required.
  5. Week 10: Full-length mocks, error analysis, revise trade notes & ensure document readiness for DV.

Recommended Books & Free Resources

  • General Aptitude — R.S. Aggarwal / Arun Sharma for arithmetic & reasoning practice.
  • Trade Basics — Standard ITI / diploma textbooks and practical manuals for specific trades (Electrician, Fitter, Electronics, Signal & Telecom, etc.).
  • Computer Aptitude — basic electronics & circuits primers, online simulators for aptitude tests (ALP).
  • Typing Practice — online typing tools for posts requiring typing tests (set target speed & accuracy as per CEN).
  • Previous Year Papers & Official CBT samples — essential for understanding question framing and time management.

Mini Practice Set

  1. Arithmetic: If a train covers 120 km in 2 hours and 30 minutes, what is its average speed (km/h)?
  2. Reasoning: Find the next number in the series: 3, 9, 27, 81, ...
  3. Technical (Electrician): Define Ohm's Law and state how current changes if resistance is doubled at constant voltage.
  4. Technician (Fitter): What is the primary purpose of a lathe machine in a workshop?

Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Read the CEN's post/trade-wise eligibility closely — a mismatch in qualification/trade can lead to rejection during DV.
  • Practice trade-specific MCQs and hands-on tasks (where possible) — conceptual clarity reduces time per question in CBT-2.
  • For ALP, computer-based aptitude tests are crucial — simulate these under exam conditions to build familiarity.
  • Start typing practice early if your post requires it — speed + accuracy is often the deciding factor.
  • Ensure medical fitness — some physical standards and color-vision/height requirements may apply for certain technical roles.

All About SSC GD Constable 2025

The SSC GD Constable (General Duty) recruitment is conducted by the Staff Selection Commission to fill Constable (GD) vacancies in various Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), such as Border Security Force (BSF), Central Industrial Security Force (CISF), Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF), Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP), Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB), and selected state police units that opt to fill quotas through SSC.

The selection process for SSC GD is largely objective and merit-based: qualifying the Computer-Based Examination (CBT) is the first step, followed by Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST). Medical examination, document verification and final merit list prepare the ground for appointments. Roles are field-oriented and demand high physical fitness, discipline and willingness to serve in diverse geographies.

This page provides a force-wise vacancy forecast, the exact syllabus and exam pattern for 2025, detailed PST/PET requirements, preparatory schedule, expected cutoffs and sample preparation resources so you can approach exam day with confidence.

Important Dates

Apply Online: 13 Nov 2025 – 04 Dec 2025. CBT (expected): Feb–Mar 2026. PST/PET: Apr–May 2026 (tentative).

Eligibility & Age Relaxation

Educational Qualification

Candidates must be matriculation pass (10th class) or equivalent from a recognized board.

Nationality

A candidate must be a citizen of India or a subject of Nepal/Bhutan or a Tibetan refugee who came to India before 1962 intending to permanently settle, or a person of Indian origin who migrated from specified countries.

Age Limit

Minimum 18 years and maximum 23 years as on cut-off date specified in the notification. Age relaxations apply to SC/ST/OBC/Ex-Servicemen/PwBD as per government rules.

Age Relaxation Table

  • SC/ST: +5 years
  • OBC (NCL): +3 years
  • Ex-Servicemen: As per rules
  • PwBD: +10 years (with other relaxations as applicable)

Physical Standards & PET/PST

Physical requirements vary by force. Below are the commonly used minimum measurements and PET standards.

Minimum Physical Standards

Category Height (Male) Chest (Male) Height (Female)
General (Plain Areas) 170 cm 80–85 cm (with expansion) 157 cm
Hilly / Tribal Areas 165 cm 79–84 cm 152 cm
Relaxation for ST (where applicable) +2.5 cm — +2.5 cm

PET Standards (Common)

  • Male: 5 km race in 24 minutes (time varies by force)
  • Female: 1.6 km race in 8.5–11 minutes (depending on force)
  • Some forces conduct high jump, long jump and endurance tests as per guidelines

Note: Always refer to force-specific PST/PET tables in the official notification. Some forces allow small measurement tolerances for practical measurement errors.

Medical Standards & Disqualifying Conditions

Candidates must be medically fit as per standards prescribed by the CAPFs. Common causes for rejection include chronic respiratory issues, severe visual defects (unless corrected), major orthopedic disorders, certain hernia conditions and hearing impairments.

Vision Standards

  • Normal vision: 6/6 or 6/9 in one eye and 6/9 or 6/6 in the other (force specifics vary)
  • Color blindness or night blindness may be disqualifying in some forces

Medical checks usually follow the PET/PST stage and include detailed physical, ENT, ophthalmic and orthopedic examinations. Keep medical documents and prior surgeries declared.

Salary, Allowances & Career Progression

SSC GD Constables are appointed in pay matrix levels as per government norms (typically Level 3 of 7th CPC). Starting basic pay, dearness allowance (DA), house rent allowance (HRA), and other field allowances vary by force and posting (field, plain area, border, cantonment).

  • Approximate starting salary (CTC) including allowances: ₹25,000 – ₹40,000 per month (varies by posting & force).
  • Field allowances, risk allowances, transport & accommodation benefits often apply for deployed personnel.
  • Promotion path: Constable → Head Constable → ASI → SI → Inspector (force-specific promotional exams & tenure rules).

Preparation Strategy — Plan & Resources

A focused plan must combine concept clarity, timed practice and PET/PST fitness preparation. Below is a 12-week plan and recommended resources.

12-Week Study Plan

  1. Weeks 1–4: Basic concepts — arithmetic, reasoning basics, GA through daily news summary.
  2. Weeks 5–8: Topic-wise practice, speed drills, begin full-length sectional mocks.
  3. Weeks 9–11: Full-length CBT mocks, review weak topics, revise GK notes.
  4. Week 12: Quick revision, light mocks, physical conditioning ramp-up for PET/PST.

Fitness Plan (PST/PET)

  • Daily: Running 3–5 km (progressively increase speed), bodyweight training (squats, lunges, push-ups).
  • Alternate days: Sprint intervals to build speed for 1.6/5 km runs.
  • Weekly: Mock PET—practice long jump, high jump and endurance drills under timed conditions.

How to Apply & Document Checklist

Step-by-step Apply

  1. Visit the official SSC portal (ssc.nic.in) and register using a valid email & mobile number.
  2. Fill the SSC GD online application form carefully and choose preferred forces/states as per options available.
  3. Upload scanned photograph and signature as per guidelines in JPG/PNG format.
  4. Pay the application fee (if applicable) through online modes and submit.
  5. Save the confirmation page and application reference number for future communication.

Document Checklist

  • 10th class pass certificate / mark sheet (proof of educational qualification)
  • Birth certificate / 10th class for date of birth proof
  • Category certificate (if applicable)
  • Ex-servicemen discharge certificate (if applicable)
  • Two passport-size photos & scanned signature

Ensure scanned documents are clear and within prescribed file sizes. Keep originals for document verification stage.

Job Profile — What to Expect After Selection

CRPF

Deployed for internal security, anti-Naxal operations, riot control and law & order duties. High emphasis on endurance and active field deployments.

BSF

Border guarding force with postings along India’s international borders. Tough field conditions, border outposts and patrol duties.

CISF

Industrial and infrastructure security (including airports, power plants and government installations). Duties include access control, surveillance and emergency response.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Who is eligible for SSC GD Constable 2025?
A1: Candidates who have passed 10th class (matriculation) and satisfy the age criteria (typically 18–23 years) are eligible. Check official notification for nationality & other details.
Q2: What is the SSC GD exam pattern?
A2: CBT of 100 marks (4 sections x 25 marks each). Candidates clearing CBT undergo PET/PST and medical examination.
Q3: How to prepare for the PET/PST?
A3: Build endurance via daily running, sprint training and plyometrics. Practice long jump/high jump if required and maintain a strict diet & sleep schedule in the final weeks.
Q4: Is the exam bilingual?
A4: Yes — questions are usually provided in English and Hindi (where applicable). Some state-specific instructions could include regional languages.
Q5: What is the negative marking?
A5: Typically 0.25 marks deducted for each wrong answer. Refer to the official notification for precise details for the 2025 cycle.
Q6: Can I apply for multiple forces?
A6: The SSC application allows preferences; final allocation depends on vacancies, merit and preferences. Check the application form options carefully.
Q7: What documents are required at DV?
A7: Original educational certificate, DOB proof, domicile, category certificate (if applicable), ID proof and other documents mentioned in the notification.
Q8: How important are previous year papers?
A8: Extremely important — they help you understand question patterns, sectional weightage and typical difficulty levels. Include shift-wise analysis in your mocks.
Q9: Is there relaxation in height for certain regions?
A9: Yes. Relaxations for hilly/tribal areas and for ST in some forces are common. Official rules specify exact relaxations.
Q10: When will the admit cards be released?
A10: Admit cards are usually released 10–15 days before the exam. Keep your SSC login details ready to download the admit card and check city intimation details.

RRB Group D Admit Card 2025 LIVE: City Intimation Slip & Exam Schedule Released

By Manish Kumar · Govt Apply Zone · Nov 22, 2025, 17:36 IST

RRB Group D Admit Card 2025 LIVE: Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the RRB Group D City Intimation Slip 2025 on its official portal. The written CBT exam for Group D posts is scheduled from 27 November 2025 onwards across India. Candidates can now check their exam city, date and shift details through the city slip, while the RRB Group D Admit Card 2025 will be made available approximately four days before the exam date on regional RRB websites.

RRB Group D City Intimation Slip 2025 – Key Highlights

  • RRB Group D City Intimation Slip 2025 download link is active at rrb.digialm.com.
  • RRB Group D exam to be held between 27 November 2025 and 16 January 2026.
  • Admit Card (E-Call Letter) will be released 4 days before the exam date.
  • Over 1.08 crore candidates have applied for Group D posts under CEN 08/2024.
  • Vacancies: 32,438 posts including Track Maintainer Grade-IV, Helper/Assistant, Pointsman etc.

How to Download RRB Group D City Intimation Slip 2025?

Candidates can download the RRB Group D City Intimation Slip 2025 by following these steps:

  1. Visit your regional RRB website or city slip portal (e.g. rrbcdg.gov.in / rrb.digialm.com).
  2. On the homepage, click on the link titled "RRB Group D City Intimation Slip 2025".
  3. Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth.
  4. Complete the captcha verification and click on the Submit/Login button.
  5. Your Group D exam city slip will appear on the screen showing exam city, exam date, and shift.
  6. Download and take a printout for easy reference.

How to Download RRB Group D Admit Card 2025 (Once Released)?

The RRB Group D Admit Card 2025 will be available around four days before your exam date. Steps:

  1. Visit your regional RRB website (e.g. rrbcdg.gov.in) or central portal rrbapply.gov.in.
  2. Click on the link labelled "RRB Group D Admit Card 2025" or "E-Call Letter".
  3. Login using your Registration Number and Date of Birth.
  4. Check all details like name, photo, signature, exam city, date, shift and reporting time.
  5. Download the admit card PDF and take a clear printout (preferably A4 size).

What to Do If Photo or Signature is Wrong on Admit Card?

If your photo or signature on the RRB Group D admit card appears blurred, incorrect or not visible:

  • Immediately contact your regional RRB Helpline number or email.
  • Mention your Registration Number and describe the issue clearly.
  • Attach a copy of your application form and a valid ID proof (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.) if asked.
  • Follow instructions given by the RRB to avoid problems on the exam day.

RRB Group D Selection Process 2025

Under the RRB Group D recruitment drive, candidates must clear multiple stages of selection:

  • Computer Based Test (CBT) – Objective-type online exam.
  • Physical Efficiency Test (PET) – As per male/female standards.
  • Document Verification (DV) – Verification of eligibility and certificates.
  • Medical Examination – Final fitness test as per Railway norms.

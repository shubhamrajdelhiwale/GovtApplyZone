Official Notification

DFCCIL publishes a recruitment notice listing Executive-level posts (Civil, Electrical, S&T, Operations, Accounts, HR, Finance, IT etc.), stream-wise vacancy and eligibility.

Apply Online

Online registration window and fee details are provided in the notification — read instructions carefully and upload documents in specified format.

Vacancy & Streams

Vacancies are shared stream-wise (Civil/ Electrical/ S&T/ Operations/ HR/ Finance/ IT). DFCCIL may publish a separate vacancy PDF with category-wise breakup.

Selection Stages

Typical flow: Online CBT → Document Verification and/or Skill Test (for certain posts) → Interview (where applicable) → Final merit & appointment.

Recruiting Body

Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Ltd. (DFCCIL) — central PSU managing freight corridors; hires Executive cadre for multiple disciplines.

Posts Covered

Executive posts across disciplines: Civil, Electrical, Signal & Telecom (S&T), Operations, Accounts, HR, Law, Finance, IT and others as per notification.

Selection Flow

CBT (online) → Skill / Trade test (if required) → PSV/DV and Interview (if applicable) → Final selection based on merit & reservation norms.

Eligibility & Qualification

  • Nationality: Indian citizen or categories allowed as mentioned in the notification (e.g., Nepal/Bhutan/Tibetan refugees/persons of Indian origin).
  • Age: Age limits (lower & upper) and category relaxations are specified in the notification — check exact cut-off dates and relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwD/Ex-Servicemen.
  • Educational Qualification: Discipline-specific — typically Degree/Diploma in Engineering (Civil, Electrical, Electronics, Mechanical), Graduate/Postgraduate for HR/Accounts/Finance/IT as specified for each post. Professional qualifications (CA/ICWA/LLB/MBA) as applicable for specialist posts.
  • Experience: Some posts may be open to freshers while others call for post-qualification experience — check stream-wise eligibility in the official PDF.
  • Other: Medical fitness, character verification and ability to relocate to project sites as per DFCCIL requirements.

Vacancy & Salary

DFCCIL publishes a vacancy list in the notification and a separate vacancy PDF (stream & location-wise). Vacancies and reservations are authoritative in those documents.

Salary & Perks

Executive-level pay scale varies by post (often following IDA/DFCCIL pay matrix) — basic pay + DA + HRA + transport/medical allowances. Executive posts also include project allowances and other PSU benefits (PF/Gratuity/leave). Check the official notification or offer letter for exact pay scale and grade.

Exam Pattern (Concise)

Online Computer Based Test (CBT)

  • Objective multiple-choice exam — common sections generally include: General Awareness, General Intelligence & Reasoning, Arithmetic/Quantitative Aptitude, and Discipline-specific Technical Knowledge for the applied post.
  • Number of questions, duration, total marks and negative marking scheme are published in the notification — some posts have sectional timings and minimum qualifying marks per section.
  • CBT may be followed by skill test (practical/computer) or interview for certain specialized posts.

Skill Test / Interview

Shortlisted candidates for some posts will undergo trade/skill tests (qualifying) or interviews (for managerial/technical posts). Weightage and qualifying marks detailed in the notification.

Syllabus Highlights

  • General Awareness: Current affairs (national & international), government policies, infrastructure & transport sector updates, DFCCIL/Railway projects and basic economic indicators.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Number systems, percentages, ratio & proportion, averages, time & work, speed & distance, basic data interpretation.
  • Reasoning & Logical Ability: Puzzles, seating arrangements, coding-decoding, blood relations, syllogisms, series & analogies.
  • Technical / Discipline-specific: Civil — design, construction methods, surveying, soil mechanics, RCC, steel structures, estimation; Electrical — power systems, motors, protection, wiring; S&T — signaling basics, telecom, electronics; IT — networking, databases, software fundamentals; Finance/Accounts — accounting standards, financial management; HR — labour laws, HR policies.
  • Computer Basics: For many posts: basic computer operations, MS Office, fundamental networking and cybersecurity awareness where applicable.

Selection Process

  1. Online Registration: Fill the online form, upload photograph, signature and required certificates as per specifications.
  2. CBT: Online objective exam — shortlist to next stage based on merit, category reservations and qualifying criteria.
  3. Skill/Trade Test or Document Verification: For posts requiring practical skills or to verify eligibility documents.
  4. Interview (if applicable): For managerial/technical posts where personality & domain knowledge weigh in final selection. Weightage details provided in the notification.
  5. Final Merit & Appointment: Based on combined performance and vacancy; offer letters issued after document verification and medical clearance.

How to Apply Online

  1. Visit DFCCIL's official recruitment portal and open the Executive recruitment notification page.
  2. Read notification carefully — note post-wise eligibility, educational requirements, experience (if any), and important dates.
  3. Register with a valid email & mobile number to obtain login credentials.
  4. Complete the online application with accurate personal, educational and work-details; choose discipline & preference where asked.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature and other documents in the format & sizes specified in the notification.
  6. Pay application fee online (if applicable) and submit. Save/print the acknowledgement/confirmation & take a copy for future reference.

Important Dates (Typical / Tentative)

  • Notification Release: 2025 (check DFCCIL careers for the official PDF)
  • Online Registration Start: As specified in the notification
  • Last Date to Apply: Usually within 2–3 weeks of opening (see the notification)
  • CBT Date(s): Announced via admit cards after application close
  • Skill Test / Interview / Document Verification: Dates published for shortlisted candidates

Note: Dates and vacancy numbers above are indicative. Always verify exact schedules & vacancy details from DFCCIL's official recruitment notification before applying.

8–10 Week Preparation Plan (Practical)

  1. Weeks 1–2: Strengthen basic quantitative aptitude, reasoning and read daily current affairs related to infrastructure & transport.
  2. Weeks 3–4: Discipline-specific theory/revision (Civil/Electrical/S&T/IT/Finance) and sectional practice tests.
  3. Weeks 5–7: Timed full-length mocks (CBT pattern), improve speed & accuracy; practice past papers or sample questions if available.
  4. Weeks 8–10: Final revision, brush up domain notes, skill test practice (if applicable) and interview preparation for managerial roles.

Recommended Books & Free Resources

  • Technical: Standard engineering textbooks (discipline-specific) used at diploma/degree level for Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Electronics & Telecom.
  • Quant & Reasoning: R.S. Aggarwal / Arun Sharma for focused practice and shortcuts.
  • General Awareness: The Hindu / Indian Express, PIB releases, Ministry & Railway/DFCCIL project updates for infrastructure news.
  • Finance & Accounts: Accounting standards primers, basics of project finance and PSU accounting practice for Finance/Accounts posts.
  • IT & Computer: Networking basics, DBMS fundamentals, OS & systems design basics for IT posts.
  • Mocks & PYQs: Official sample papers (if DFCCIL provides) and reputable timed test series for CBT simulation.

Mini Practice Set

  1. Quant: If the average of five numbers is 48 and four of them are 45, 50, 44 and 49, find the fifth number.
  2. Reasoning: In a code language 'BRIDGE' is written as 'CSJEFH' — identify the pattern and encode 'TRACK'.
  3. Civil (Concept): What is the difference between working stress method and limit state method in structural design?
  4. Electrical (Concept): Explain why power factor correction is important in distribution systems.

Tips & Common Pitfalls

  • Read the official notification carefully — mismatch in educational qualification or experience can lead to disqualification at DV stage.
  • Carefully follow document upload size & format instructions to avoid rejection during online submission.
  • Focus on conceptual clarity for discipline-specific technical questions — rote learning often fails for application-based CBT items.
  • Keep copies of all certificates, experience letters & ID proofs ready in required formats before applying.
  • For interview rounds, prepare to explain technical projects, internships or any professional experience listed in your application.
